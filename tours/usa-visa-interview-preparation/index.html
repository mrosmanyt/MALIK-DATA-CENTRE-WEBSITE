USA Visa Interview Preparation (B1/B2 Coaching)

One-to-one coaching for your US B1/B2 visa interview in Pakistan: a file review, a consulate-style mock interview and a question bank built for your profile. Sessions in Islamabad, Rawalpindi or online.

Ask on WhatsApp Call +92 348 9057646

What's included

  • File review comparing your DS-160, documents and your story
  • Personal mock interview in consulate style: standing, short answers and follow-up questions
  • Question bank for your profile: first-time traveller, student, businessman or previous refusal
  • Guidance on presenting your ties to Pakistan: job, business, family, property and return travel
  • Consulate-day briefing: the Islamabad embassy process, what to bring and what to leave at home

Who it's for

  • First-time applicants
  • Applicants with a previous refusal
  • Families (each adult is interviewed)
  • Applicants whose sponsor lives in the USA

How it works

  1. Message usSend your interview date and city on WhatsApp.
  2. Get your listWe send a pre-session document list.
  3. RehearseYour mock interview session, in person or online.

Good to know

The goal is short, consistent answers, not memorised speeches.
You are interviewed alone: no agent can attend the embassy interview.
No one can guarantee approval: the consular officer decides.
