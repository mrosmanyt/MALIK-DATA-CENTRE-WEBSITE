## What do you get with ChatGPT Go?

ChatGPT Go includes everything in the free plan, plus higher limits on the features people use most for real work.

Based on OpenAI's official help page, Go adds:

- **More image generation.** Create more images for posts, thumbnails, mockups or school projects.
- **More file uploads.** Upload more PDFs, spreadsheets and documents to summarise or analyse.
- **More advanced data analysis.** Use the built-in Python tool more often for charts, calculations and data cleanup.

## What does ChatGPT Go not include?

ChatGPT Go does not include everything in ChatGPT Plus or Pro, so check this list before you buy.

According to OpenAI's help page:

- Some advanced products and higher-tier features need Plus or a higher plan.
- Legacy models are not included in Go.
- API usage is not included. If you build apps that call OpenAI's API, that is billed separately.
- Some apps and plugins in the directory need Plus or higher.
- OpenAI is testing ads with eligible Go users in some regions.

For most writing, study, research and everyday work, these limits do not matter. If you are a developer who needs API access, look at a coding tool instead, such as [Cursor Pro](/tools/cursor-pro/).

## Is our ChatGPT Go plan private or shared?

Our ChatGPT Go plan is a shared premium account, which means one paid login used by more than one buyer.

This is the main reason the price is low, and it is important to understand what it means in practice:

- **Other users may see chat history.** On a shared login, chats, uploaded files and saved memory can be visible to everyone using it.
- **Do not save personal data.** Avoid CNIC numbers, bank details, passwords, client contracts or private photos.
- **Your history does not carry over.** When the plan ends, your chats stay on that account, not on your own email.

A shared account works well for drafting, learning, brainstorming, grammar fixes and general research. It is not the right choice for confidential client work. If you need privacy, ask us on WhatsApp before ordering and we will tell you what private options are available that day.
