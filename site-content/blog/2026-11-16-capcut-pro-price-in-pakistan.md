## 2 devices, private, or team

These are three different ways the Pro features arrive.

**2-device login.** The 1, 3 and 6 month plans are written as a login on 2 devices. Phone plus PC is the usual pair. Our tool page says the same thing: those three lengths work on two devices, and on a shared-style login you should export important projects to your own device. Do not treat the login as a private hard drive. Finish the video, export it, and keep the file on your phone or laptop.

**Private activation.** The Rs 1,250 row is monthly access set up as a private activation. It costs more than the 2-device month at Rs 800 because the listing is your own activation rather than a 2-device login. Choose it when the drafts are client work you do not want on a shared login. The 2-device month is the better price when you edit your own page and you can export as you go.

**Team invite.** The team rows are Pro through a team workspace: Rs 650 for 1 month, or Rs 4,100 for 6 months. The 1-month team invite is the lowest price in the table. It is a seat in a team, not a private login and not a pair of devices you fully control. Use it for your own editing if you are comfortable joining a workspace. Do not use it as the place you store a client's only copy. Export when the edit is done.

The 6-month team plan at Rs 4,100 is cheaper than the 6-month 2-device plan at Rs 4,800. The tradeoff is the access type, not a hidden feature list. Both listings say Pro effects, AI tools and high-resolution exports. The 2-device listing also names a 180-day warranty. The team listing names 6 months of validity. If you want the login on your phone and your PC under the 2-device wording, pay Rs 4,800. If a team invite is acceptable, the Rs 4,100 row is the lower 6-month price.

## What you are paying for in the edit

CapCut's free editor already cuts clips. Pro is the upgrade people feel on export and on effects.

- Pro effects, transitions and templates that the free timeline locks.
- Auto captions, which matter because many viewers watch without sound.
- Higher-resolution export. The 1-month 2-device specs name 4K export. Check the export menu on your device after activation, because phone and desktop builds do not always show the same ceiling.
- AI helpers such as background removal, as listed on the tool page. These depend on the CapCut version you have installed.

None of that types the video idea for you. A clear first frame, a spoken or written offer, and a caption the viewer can read still decide whether the Reel works. Pro removes the watermark and the locked effects. It does not fix a confused script.

Update the app from the official store after we activate. An old install often hides Pro behind a "subscribe" button even when the login is already Pro. Sign out, update, sign in, then open a project.
