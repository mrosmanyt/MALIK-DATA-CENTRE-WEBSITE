The tool pages separate them this way: Gumloop is the easier builder for people who are not writing nodes. n8n is the more flexible one for developers and for workflows that have to branch. A lead list that needs scraping and a sheet can be a Gumloop job. A WhatsApp inbox that has to call your own API, wait, and write back is closer to n8n.

Do not buy both to "see which one feels nicer" in the same week unless you already know you have two different jobs. Rs 6,500 plus Rs 4,550 is a lot of builder for one follow-up message. Pick the job:

- You will maintain the workflow yourself and you may outgrow a simple canvas. n8n at Rs 6,500.

- You want neither login and you want the workflow handed to you working. That is the automation service quote, and you may still need one of these seats underneath it. Ask which seat the quote assumes.

Claude API and other API packs are a third thing. They are price on request and they are not a chat login. This n8n page does not quote those packs. If a node in your workflow calls an API, the key is a separate order. Say so before you pay for Starter alone and expect tokens to be inside it.
