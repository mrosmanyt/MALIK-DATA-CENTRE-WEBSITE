## Start with the photo, not the chatbot

The thing a customer sees first is the picture in the status or the catalog. [Canva Pro](/tools/canva-pro/) is Rs 400 for 12 months, activated on your own email, with a listed original price of Rs 3,000. That is the plan to buy if you will make posters every week. Magic resize, background removal and the template library are what the listing is for. You design on the account you control, so last month's price list is still there next Eid.

Use it for work that is actually a poster:

- A white-background photo of one product, the price in large type, the shop name, and the city.
- A story size and a post size of the same design. Resize once. Do not rebuild it.
- A simple menu or a Ramadan timing card. Canva is enough. You do not need Photoshop for text on a photo.

There is also a Canva admin panel at Rs 4,500 for 12 months, listed with an original price of Rs 15,000, for up to 500 seats. That row is for someone allocating seats, not for a shop with one phone. Ignore it unless you already know you run a panel.

If you retouch inside the Picsart app every day, that is a different editor, priced in the [Picsart Pro guide](/blog/picsart-pro-price-in-pakistan.html) at Rs 3,500 for 11 months on your email. Most shops should not start there. A clean poster in Canva sells the item. A heavy retouch does not, if the price and the size are missing.

Pay for Canva from the [Pakistan buying guide](/buy-ai-tools-in-pakistan/) with EasyPaisa, JazzCash, NayaPay, UPaisa or a bank transfer. The year is long. Use an email you will still open in month twelve.

## WhatsApp replies, without putting customer data in a shared chat

The second job is words. A customer asks "available?" and the shop answers late, or answers in a way that starts an argument. [ChatGPT Go](/tools/chatgpt-go/) is the draft tool.

| ChatGPT Go | 6 months | Shared account | Rs 2,200 |
| ChatGPT Go | 3 months | Ready-made account | Rs 2,900 |

The 6-month listing shows an original price of Rs 5,000. The 3-month price and original price are both Rs 2,900. Neither row is a plan named ChatGPT Plus. [ChatGPT Business](/tools/chatgpt-business/) is a separate workspace from Rs 4,000 for 1 month. A shop with one owner does not need Business to answer whether the blue one is still the colour they sell.

How to use the shared plan without creating a mess:

1. Write the reply yourself in one line: price, size, when it can be delivered.
2. Ask the chat to make that line polite in Urdu and in English. You already know the facts. The tool only cleans the sentence.
3. Paste the result into WhatsApp yourself. Do not connect the shop's WhatsApp to a shared login.
4. Do not type customer phone numbers, addresses, card numbers or the shop's JazzCash PIN into a shared account. A shared login is not private. The difference is explained in [private, shared and invitation accounts](/blog/private-vs-shared-vs-invitation-ai-accounts-pakistan.html).
5. If the reply must mention a real order, a ready-made 3-month account at Rs 2,900 is the tighter login. It is still not a place to store your password list.

If you only send ten replies a day and you already know what to say, skip ChatGPT. Rs 2,200 is worth it when the shop loses sales because nobody answers in full sentences, not because a blog said every business needs a chatbot.

## A month that matches a real shop

Week one: Canva on your email. Make ten product posts from photos you already have. Put the price on every image.

Week two: if you are slow to answer, add ChatGPT Go and write five reply templates: the price, the size you actually have, the delivery area, the timing, and a polite no. Keep customer details out of the shared chat.

Week three: one CapCut reel of the item that already sells. Post it. Do not buy a second video tool until that reel has been up for a week.

That sequence is the whole recommendation. The prices are Rs 400, then Rs 2,200 if you need words, then Rs 650 or Rs 800 if you need the edit. A shop that does the photos and answers WhatsApp will feel the difference. A shop that buys all three and posts nothing will not.
