## Dictate, then edit. Do not skip the read

The product turns speech into text and formats it. It does not know which sentence you meant to keep. Read the paragraph before it goes to a client, a lecturer or a GitHub comment.

Use it for:

- A first draft of an email or a document you will edit.
- Notes after a call, if you are allowed to record or dictate that call. The tool is not permission to record someone who has not agreed.
- Prompts and comments while you code, if you already have the editor open.

Do not use the first dictated draft as the final text when a wrong word would cost money. Prices, names, medicine, and legal lines need a human read. Filler-word removal can also drop a word you meant. The spec says smart formatting. It does not say the paragraph is checked.

[Grammarly Pro](/tools/grammarly-pro/) is the tool for text that already exists. The 1-month plan is Rs 1,400, activated on your email.
