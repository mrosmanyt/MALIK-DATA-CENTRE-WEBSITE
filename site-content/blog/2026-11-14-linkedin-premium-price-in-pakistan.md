## What LinkedIn itself includes

LinkedIn's help article on the [difference between free and Premium](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a545596) is the table that matters. InMail credits per month on that chart are:

- Premium Career: 5
- Premium Business: 15
- Sales Navigator Core: 50
- Sales Navigator Advanced: 50

Career is the job-seeker plan. LinkedIn's [Premium Career](https://premium.linkedin.com/careers/career) page describes applicant insights, who viewed your profile, private browsing, profile help, and LinkedIn Learning. The help chart says who-viewed-your-profile insights on Career go back 365 days.

Business adds company insights and a custom profile button, with more InMail. It is aimed at people selling or representing a small business, not at someone applying to graduate roles.

Sales Navigator is a different product with its own interface. LinkedIn's [Sales Navigator account types](https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a104003) page says Core is the single-user plan: advanced lead and account search, saved leads, custom lists, alerts and InMail. Advanced adds team tools Core does not have: shared lists, Buyer Intent, Smart Links, and CSV upload of a book of business. Advanced Plus is the CRM version. We do not list Advanced Plus. If you need Salesforce sync, buy that from LinkedIn.

LinkedIn Learning comes with Career, Business and Sales Navigator on that comparison chart. It does not, by itself, make you a stronger candidate. You still have to finish the courses. If you want a year of certificates rather than a short Premium window, our [Coursera Plus](/tools/coursera-plus/) plan is Rs 3,200 for 12 months on a private activation. The two products overlap on "I should learn a skill" and do not overlap on "I need InMail".

## Which plan should you buy?

Use the job, not the logo.

| Your week | Plan to buy | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Applying to jobs, checking who viewed you | Career, 3 months, Rs 1,800 | Built for hiring, cheapest multi-month Career row |
| You want the Career voucher specifically | Career Duo, 2 months, Rs 3,200 | Redeemed on your account; shorter and priced higher |
| You sell to local businesses and need company insights | Business Duo, 2 months, Rs 3,700 | More InMail than Career, plus business insights |
| You prospect on LinkedIn most days | Sales Navigator, 2 months, Rs 1,800 | 50 InMail and lead search, if the account is Core |
| A team sharing lead lists for a month | Sales Navigator Advanced, Rs 5,200 | Only if you will use the team features |

Do not buy Sales Navigator for a job search. The interface is for sellers, and the extra search filters will not write your CV. Do not buy Career if you send twenty cold messages a day. Five InMail credits are gone on Monday.

Premium does not guarantee interviews. LinkedIn's own pages market higher reply rates for InMail than for a cold email. That is a comparison with email, not a promise that a recruiter will answer. A precise message to the right person beats five vague ones.

## How to use Career for a job search in Pakistan

Buy the month only after the profile is ready. Premium will not fix an empty headline.

1. Write a headline that says the role you want, not "seeking opportunities". Open to work can be visible to recruiters only.
2. Put two or three projects in Featured, with a real outcome in the first line.
3. Turn on Career, then use applicant insights on roles you are actually qualified for. If you are a weak match, fix the CV or skip the role. Spraying Easy Apply is how people burn a week.
4. Spend InMail on hiring managers you cannot reach any other way. Five credits means five careful notes, not five templates.
5. Read who viewed your profile once a day, not once an hour. Reply to relevant viewers while the visit is recent.
6. Run every InMail through [Grammarly Pro](/tools/grammarly-pro/) first. Our Grammarly plan is Rs 1,400 for 1 month or Rs 3,500 for 12 months, on your own email. Tone and clarity matter more on a short InMail than on a long post.

LinkedIn is the same muscle: a clear offer, sent to a named person.

## How to use Sales Navigator without wasting it

Core is a search tool. Treat it like one.

- Build a lead list with filters you could explain out loud: country, industry, headcount, title. "Marketing" alone is not a search.
- Save accounts you can actually serve from Pakistan. A 10,000-lead cap does not mean you should save 10,000 leads.
- Write one observation from their profile in the first two lines. The InMail credit is the expensive part. The research is the part that gets the reply.
- Advanced is worth Rs 5,200 only if more than one person will share lists or you need Buyer Intent. A solo freelancer rarely needs it for a single month.

When the voucher ends, export anything you are allowed to keep and do not leave the only copy of a client list inside a seat you no longer have.
