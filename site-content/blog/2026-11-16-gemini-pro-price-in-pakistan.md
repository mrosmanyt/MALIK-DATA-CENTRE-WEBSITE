## Who the 1-year plan is for

The Rs 1,500 year fits people who write and research inside Google.

- A student drafting essays in Docs, with Gemini for an outline, a counterargument and a cleanup pass. The final sentences should still be yours. Departments differ on what they allow. Our [guide for university students](/blog/best-ai-tools-for-university-students-pakistan.html) puts Gemini next to Grammarly and QuillBot and says where each one stops.
- A freelancer who answers clients in Gmail and keeps briefs in Drive. Pro on your own email means the thread stays in your inbox.
- Anyone who wants a longer Gemini plan than a single month and does not need heavy video.

The 2-year bundle at Rs 3,350 fits the same person if they already know they will use Gemini past the first year, and they also want one month of YouTube Premium on that email. It is a weak fit if you only wanted YouTube. A month of YouTube on its own is a different product. Do not buy two years of Gemini to get thirty days of ad-free video.

Use the year as a writing tool with a habit, not as a search box you open once.

1. Put the source notes in a Doc first. Gemini is more useful on your material than on a one-line prompt.
2. Ask for an outline, then for the holes in the outline. Fill the holes yourself.
3. Ask for a clearer paragraph only after you have written the messy one.
4. Check names, dates and numbers. A fluent paragraph can still be wrong.
5. If the work is an assignment, follow the written rules for your course.

That is the ordinary use the Rs 1,500 plan is priced for.
