Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

At MALIK DATA CENTRE ("we", "us", "our"), your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website (malikdatacentre.store) and our services, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum information needed to fulfil your order and provide support:

  • Contact details you share with us on WhatsApp, such as your name and phone number.
  • Order details, including the product or subscription you request.
  • Activation information where a subscription is set up on your own email — for example, an email address or an activation code you provide voluntarily.
  • Payment confirmation, such as a transaction screenshot. We do not collect or store your full bank, card, or wallet PIN/credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information solely to process your order, activate and deliver your subscription, provide warranty and support, and communicate with you about your purchase. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Payments

Payments are made directly through third-party services such as EasyPaisa, JazzCash, or bank transfer. Those providers handle your payment data under their own privacy policies. We only receive confirmation that a payment was made.

4. Data Sharing

We may share limited information with the official service providers only as needed to activate a subscription on your behalf (for example, applying an invite to your email). We never share your data for advertising or marketing by others.

5. Data Retention

We keep order and activation records only for as long as needed to honour your warranty and resolve any support requests. You may request deletion of your details at any time by contacting us.

6. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use basic cookies or analytics to understand traffic and improve the experience. These do not identify you personally. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so please avoid sharing sensitive credentials such as passwords or PINs.

8. Children

Our services are intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with an updated date.
