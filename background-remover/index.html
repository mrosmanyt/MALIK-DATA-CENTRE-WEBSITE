Getting clean cut-outs

Use a photo with good light and a clear difference between the subject and the background. Hair and fine edges come out best when the photo is sharp. For products, shoot on a plain surface and fill most of the frame.

Frequently asked questions

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Background removal runs in your browser, so the photo stays on your device.

Is it free?

Yes, with no limit and no watermark.

Can I download a transparent PNG?

Yes. Choose Download → PNG transparent.

Why is the first use slow?

The AI model (about 40 MB) downloads once. After that it is much faster.
