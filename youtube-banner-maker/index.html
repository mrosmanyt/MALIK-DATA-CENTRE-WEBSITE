Getting your channel banner right

YouTube shows different parts of the same 2560×1440 banner on different screens. Only the centre 1546×423 area is visible everywhere, so your channel name, upload schedule and main message must sit there. Use the outer areas for background colour and texture only.

Frequently asked questions

What size is a YouTube banner?

2560×1440 pixels, with a safe area of 1546×423 in the centre that is visible on all devices.

What size is an end screen?

Design it at 1920×1080. Leave the marked zones free for the video and subscribe elements you add in YouTube Studio.

What size is a YouTube profile picture?

800×800 pixels. It is shown as a circle, so keep your logo in the centre.
