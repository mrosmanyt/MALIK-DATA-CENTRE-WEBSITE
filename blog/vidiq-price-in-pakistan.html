What is vidIQ?

vidIQ is a YouTube growth tool. It connects to your YouTube channel and shows you what to make next: video ideas, keyword research, title and description optimisation, tags, and channel analytics. Max is vidIQ's top plan, with its full set of research and AI tools. It is a research tool, not a views service. It does not buy views, subscribers or watch time, and any seller who says a tool will do that is lying.

vidIQ is a YouTube-certified tool and works through YouTube's official access, so connecting it to your channel is allowed by YouTube. That matters, because the growth-tool market is full of browser extensions and panels that break YouTube's rules and get channels flagged. vidIQ is not one of those.

What it actually changes is the hour before you upload. Instead of guessing a title, you check the search volume and the competition for the keyword. Instead of guessing a topic, you look at what is trending in your niche and what your competitors' videos are missing. Instead of publishing and hoping, you publish with a title, description and tags that have data behind them. That is the whole job, and it is a real one — our guide for YouTubers puts vidIQ next to CapCut Pro and Artlist as the three tools a small channel actually needs.

Who should buy vidIQ Max

  • A YouTuber publishing at least weekly, who needs new video ideas and keyword research as a routine, not a one-off. If you upload once a month, a monthly tool is the wrong shape — buy one month, do a month of research in a week, and cancel.
  • A channel stuck under a few thousand subscribers, where the titles and topics are the bottleneck, not the editing. vidIQ answers the question "what should I make next" with data.
  • A creator running a faceless or automation channel, where topic selection is the whole business. The same research that picks a title also picks the niche.

It is the wrong buy if you expect vidIQ to grow the channel for you. No tool does that. It shows you the data; you still have to make the video, write the title and publish on schedule.
