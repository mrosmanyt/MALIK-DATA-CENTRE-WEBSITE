ChatGPT vs Super Grok vs Gemini: Which AI Subscription Should You Buy in Pakistan?

Updated September 21, 2026 7 min read MALIK DATA CENTRE

If you're trying to decide between ChatGPT, Grok, and Gemini, the honest answer is: it depends on what you actually need the AI for, and how much you want to spend. All three are genuinely capable assistants in 2026, but they're priced very differently and each one has a feature or two that makes it stand out. This guide breaks down the real differences so you don't overpay for capability you won't use.

Super Grok + X Premium+ — Best for Real-Time Info & X (Twitter) Power Users

Super Grok bundles Grok AI with an X Premium+ subscription, which means you also get the Blue Verification Tick and an ad-free X timeline on top of the chatbot itself. Grok's biggest advantage is that it's trained with live access to what's happening on X right now, so it's noticeably better at answering questions about current events, trends, and real-time discussions than a model that only knows its training data.

  • Strengths: Real-time X search, Blue Tick badge, ad-free X experience, fast responses.
  • Watch out for: The 10-day plan is short by design — it's meant as an affordable way to try Grok or cover a specific short-term need (like a project or an event) rather than a full year of use.

ChatGPT Go — Best All-Rounder for Work & Study

ChatGPT remains the most well-rounded option for everyday tasks: writing, coding, research, data analysis, and custom GPTs built for specific workflows. The GPT-4o model handles both text and images well, and the 6-month plan gives you enough runway to actually build habits around it — assignments, client work, side projects — without renewing every few weeks.

  • Strengths: GPT-4o & GPT-4 access, advanced data analysis, custom GPT creators, broad general knowledge.
  • Watch out for: It's a shared premium account setup, so if you specifically need a private, single-user login, check the listing details before ordering.

Gemini Advanced — Best for Long Documents & Google Workspace Users

Gemini Advanced's headline feature is its massive 1M token context window — practically speaking, that means you can drop an entire book, a large codebase, or months of meeting notes into a single conversation and Gemini can still reason across all of it. If your daily life already runs through Gmail, Docs, and Drive, Gemini's native Workspace integration also saves a lot of copy-pasting.

  • Strengths: 1M token context window, Gemini 1.5 Pro model, Google Docs/Gmail sync, activated directly on your own email.
  • Watch out for: If you don't already use Google Workspace day-to-day, you won't get the full benefit of the integration — you'd mainly be paying for the large context window and model quality.

So Which One Should You Buy?

As a simple rule of thumb:

  • Pick Super Grok if you're active on X/Twitter and want real-time answers plus the Blue Tick, without committing to a long plan.
  • Pick ChatGPT Go if you want the most generally useful assistant for work, study, and coding, with the longest track record.
  • Pick Gemini Advanced if you regularly work with very long documents or live inside Google Workspace.

Many of our customers actually buy more than one — for example, ChatGPT Go for daily work and Super Grok for a short project that needs real-time info. Since all three are affordably priced individually, stacking two subscriptions is often still cheaper than one official annual plan bought directly.
