FAQ

Which AI tool should a sales team buy first?

LinkedIn Premium from Rs 1,800 if you sell B2B, or ChatGPT Go at Rs 2,200 for 6 months for daily pitches and follow-ups. Most teams buy both — prospecting and drafting solve different problems.

Can AI write follow-ups that do not sound robotic?

Yes, if you give it the context: the client's name, what you discussed, and the next step. A follow-up written from a one-line prompt sounds generic; one written from your call notes sounds like you.

Is it safe to paste client details into AI chats?

No. Never paste contracts, pricing or private client records into any AI chat. Draft with sample figures and fill in the real details yourself.

What is the cheapest starter stack for sales?

ChatGPT Go Rs 2,200 for 6 months plus Notion Business from Rs 1,250 for 3 months — about Rs 3,450 to start, covering pitches and pipeline tracking.

Do these tools replace salespeople?

No. They remove the slow drafting, deck-building and organising — the relationship, the negotiation and the close stay with the salesperson.

Sales team ke liye sab se pehle kaun sa AI tool lein?

B2B selling ke liye LinkedIn Premium lein — Rs 1,800 se shuru. Daily pitches ke liye ChatGPT Go lein, Rs 2,200 chhe mahine ke liye.
