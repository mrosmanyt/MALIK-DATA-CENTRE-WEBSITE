2 devices, private, or team

These are three different ways the Pro features arrive.

2-device login. The 1, 3 and 6 month plans are written as a login on 2 devices. Phone plus PC is the usual pair. Our tool page says the same thing: those three lengths work on two devices, and on a shared-style login you should export important projects to your own device. Do not treat the login as a private hard drive. Finish the video, export it, and keep the file on your phone or laptop.

Private activation. The Rs 1,250 row is monthly access set up as a private activation. It costs more than the 2-device month at Rs 800 because the listing is your own activation rather than a 2-device login. Choose it when the drafts are client work you do not want on a shared login. The 2-device month is the better price when you edit your own page and you can export as you go.

Team invite. The team rows are Pro through a team workspace: Rs 650 for 1 month, or Rs 4,100 for 6 months. The 1-month team invite is the lowest price in the table. It is a seat in a team, not a private login and not a pair of devices you fully control. Use it for your own editing if you are comfortable joining a workspace. Do not use it as the place you store a client's only copy. Export when the edit is done.

The 6-month team plan at Rs 4,100 is cheaper than the 6-month 2-device plan at Rs 4,800. The tradeoff is the access type, not a hidden feature list. Both listings say Pro effects, AI tools and high-resolution exports. The 2-device listing also names a 180-day warranty. The team listing names 6 months of validity. If you want the login on your phone and your PC under the 2-device wording, pay Rs 4,800. If a team invite is acceptable, the Rs 4,100 row is the lower 6-month price.

What CapCut lists as the official price

CapCut's pricing page showed Pro at $19.99 (official) when we checked. The same page says the displayed pricing is a reference only, and that you should sign in to see the final price, taxes and offers. CapCut's help page says the price varies by region, device and promotion. Treat $19.99 as CapCut's reference card, not as a promise that the Pakistan app store shows the same number.

On that pricing page, Pro also listed 1,200 monthly AI credits, premium templates, AI features and 1 TB of space, with a note that plan details vary by region. Ultra and Teams are separate official cards. We do not sell an Ultra row. Our team rows are the team-invite plans at Rs 650 and Rs 4,100, which are our prices, not CapCut's per-seat dollar card.

AI credits on CapCut are metered. Auto captions and standard Pro effects are why most buyers upgrade. A heavy AI video model inside CapCut can spend the monthly credits quickly. If your work is cutting, captions, music and effects, the Pro plan is the right tool. If your work is generating the clip from a text prompt, that is Kling or Higgsfield, and CapCut is where you finish it.

A month at Rs 800 is the test. If you export several videos a week, the 3-month plan at Rs 2,800 or the 6-month plan at Rs 4,800 is the better fit, because you will not want to reorder every four weeks. If you might stop after one campaign, stay on the month.

Two devices means two, not a family of phones. Signing the same 2-device login into a third phone can knock one of the others out. Pick the phone you shoot on and the computer you finish on. Sign out of old devices before you add a new one.

The private month is one month. If you know you want private access for half a year, say so on WhatsApp before you buy three private months in a row. We will tell you whether a longer private option is one of the plans behind Contact Support, or whether the 2-device 6-month plan is the one we can price today.

What you are paying for in the edit

CapCut's free editor already cuts clips. Pro is the upgrade people feel on export and on effects.

  • Pro effects, transitions and templates that the free timeline locks.
  • Auto captions, which matter because many viewers watch without sound.
  • Higher-resolution export. The 1-month 2-device specs name 4K export. Check the export menu on your device after activation, because phone and desktop builds do not always show the same ceiling.
  • AI helpers such as background removal, as listed on the tool page. These depend on the CapCut version you have installed.

None of that types the video idea for you. A clear first frame, a spoken or written offer, and a caption the viewer can read still decide whether the Reel works. Pro removes the watermark and the locked effects. It does not fix a confused script.

Update the app from the official store after we activate. An old install often hides Pro behind a "subscribe" button even when the login is already Pro. Sign out, update, sign in, then open a project.

How to order and what to check

  1. Open the CapCut Pro page and choose a priced row, or Contact Support if you need the All Plans menu.
  2. Pay and send the screenshot on WhatsApp. Say whether you want the 2-device login, the private activation or the team invite, in case the duration exists in more than one access type.
  3. The delivery policy says most orders are ready within 5–30 minutes after payment is confirmed.
  4. Sign in on the devices you named. On a team plan, accept the invite from the CapCut account you want to edit with.
  5. Open a project, apply one Pro effect, turn on captions, and export a 15-second test. The export should complete without a watermark from a Pro asset.
  6. If the account still shows Free, or a device will not sign in, screenshot it the same day. The refund policy explains repair, replacement and a refund when we cannot deliver.

We do not need your email password for a plan that arrives as a login or an invite. Do not send a password because a message asks for one.

Back up the project from the phone to the computer, or export a master file, before the plan's end date. A 2-device login and a team workspace are not a personal archive.
