How to use a check without treating it as proof

The tool page says a Pangram result does not prove who wrote the text. Use it as one signal and read the text yourself. That warning is the most important line on the product, more important than the price.

A false clear and a false flag are both possible on any detector. Short answers, quoted material, and text that was heavily edited by a person can score in ways that surprise you. Do not fail a student, reject a writer, or accuse a colleague on the score alone. Read the paragraph. Ask the person if your process allows that. Keep the original file. The check is a screen you can screenshot. It is not a court.

Do not paste material you are not allowed to upload. A client's confidential draft, an exam that has not been sat, and a private email are not test documents. A ready-made account is a login we deliver. Treat the text you paste as text you are willing to put into that product.
