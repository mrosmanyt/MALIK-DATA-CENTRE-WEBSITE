Gemini Plus Price in Pakistan: 6 and 12 Month Activation Links

Gemini Plus is the cheapest door into Google's Gemini plans, and it is also the most confused one. People search for the Gemini Plus price in Pakistan, find a number, and then wonder whether they are buying Gemini Pro by another name. They are not. This guide gives the two Gemini Plus rows on our store, explains what an activation link actually is, and shows the one case where you should pay more and buy Gemini Pro instead.

What is the Gemini Plus price in Pakistan?

Both Gemini Plus rows are on our Gemini Plus page. Both are activation links, and the longer plan is the cheaper one.

What an activation link means

An activation link is not a login to a shared account, and it is not an activation on your Gmail either. You pay, we send you a link on WhatsApp, and you open it and follow the steps to activate Gemini Plus. The delivery policy says most orders are ready within 5–30 minutes after payment is confirmed.

That matters for two practical reasons. First, nobody needs your Google password. We do not ask for it, and you should not send it to any seller who asks. Second, the plan is tied to the activation, not to a shared login you have to keep signing into. Keep the link and the payment screenshot until the plan is running, and screenshot the plan page once it shows active. If something stops working later, that pair of images is what support needs, and the refund policy explains repair, replacement and a refund when we cannot deliver.

Gemini Plus vs Gemini Pro

Plus and Pro are different tiers, and the store sells them in different ways. Confusing them is the one mistake that costs buyers real money, so here is the split in plain terms.

Gemini PlusGemini Pro
Price hereRs 600 for 12 months, Rs 650 for 6 monthsRs 1,500 for 12 months
DeliveryActivation linkActivated on your own Gmail
TierGoogle's lower-cost planGoogle's stronger plan, with Gemini inside Gmail, Docs and Sheets and 5 TB of storage

Plus is the budget tier. It gives you a paid Gemini experience at a fraction of Pro's price, and for everyday chatting, drafting and study help it is enough for most people. Pro is the stronger plan: a more capable Gemini model, Deep Research, Gemini inside Gmail and Docs, and the storage that comes with it. The full Pro picture is in the Gemini Pro price guide, and the side-by-side with the top tier is on Gemini Pro vs Google AI Ultra.

The rule is simple. If someone offers you "Gemini" without saying Plus or Pro, ask which row it is before you pay. If you wanted Pro on your own email and you bought a Plus link, you bought the wrong product, and no discount makes that right.

Who should buy Gemini Plus

The Rs 600 year fits people who want a paid Gemini without a monthly dollar bill.

  • A student who wants a steadier Gemini for assignments and revision, without paying Pro money. Our guide for university students puts Gemini next to Grammarly and QuillBot and says where each one stops.
  • Anyone who uses Gemini a few times a week for drafting, explaining and planning, and does not need the strongest model or the Google-apps integration.
  • A buyer who wants to try a paid Gemini tier for a year at the lowest possible price before deciding whether Pro is worth it.

It is the wrong buy if your real job is heavy research, long documents, or video. Pro is the writing and research plan, and Google AI Ultra is the video plan. Buying Plus for a job it cannot do, then upgrading anyway, costs more than buying the right row first. The cheapest plan is only cheap if it does the job.

What to check after activation

Spend five minutes so the row you paid for is the row you received.

  • Plan name on the Google account: Plus, for the length you bought.
  • End date: about 12 months, or about 6 months on the shorter link.
  • Gemini opens and answers on gemini.google.com, and the paid features you expected are visible.
  • Keep the activation link and the payment screenshot together until the plan ends.

For a price, a law, a citation or a medical sentence, open the source. Gemini drafts and searches your files. It is not the source. And if you outgrow Plus, the upgrade path is Pro on your own email, not another Plus link.
