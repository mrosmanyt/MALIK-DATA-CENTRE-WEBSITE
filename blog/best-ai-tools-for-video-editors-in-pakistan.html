Six AI tools from the MALIK DATA CENTRE catalog cover that whole chain — the editor itself, the music and template libraries, and the generators for clips and avatars. This guide maps each tool to the editing job it does best, with real Pakistan prices in rupees, and shows the cheapest way to start.

The editor: CapCut Pro

Everything starts in the timeline, and CapCut Pro is the editor most Pakistani editors already open: premium effects, AI editing tools, captions and higher-quality exports. The catalog has seven rows — from Rs 650 for a 1-month team invite, Rs 800 for a month on 2 devices, Rs 1,250 for a private month, up to Rs 4,800 for 6 months — and the CapCut Pro price guide explains every one. The team invite at Rs 650 is the cheapest way to try Pro for a month; the private month at Rs 1,250 is the row for client work you cannot afford to lose to a shared login. If you edit reels, ads or YouTube videos for clients, this is the first tool to buy — the rest of this guide only matters once the cut is good.

Music and stock: Artlist and Envato

A cut without licensed music is a video that gets muted or claimed. Artlist Pro at Rs 3,500 on a ready-made account is music, sound effects and footage in one place — the Artlist price guide notes the catalog does not print a month count for this plan, so confirm the duration on WhatsApp before you pay. Envato at Rs 2,000 for a month or Rs 8,300 for 12 months is the wider library: templates, presets, stock video, fonts and graphics with a commercial licence — the Envato price guide compares both rows. Pick Artlist when music is the gap, Envato when the gap is everything else; Storyblocks Unlimited at Rs 3,400 a month is the third option when footage, not templates, is what you need most.

AI-generated clips: Kling and Higgsfield

When the shot was never filmed, generate it. Kling AI turns text or an image into a clip with realistic motion — Rs 1,300 for 660 monthly credits or Rs 2,400 for a 750-credit Standard plan, covered in the Kling price guide. It is the tool for product clips, ads and the b-roll a client never shot. Higgsfield Plus is the cinematic one: camera presets, effects and access to top video models in one studio — Rs 7,500 for 1,200 credits or Rs 6,400 for 610 credits, covered in the Higgsfield price guide. Our best AI video tools guide compares all of these by output — Kling and Cinem make clips, Higgsfield is the camera-preset studio — so read it before buying credits for the wrong job.

Avatars and translation: HeyGen

HeyGen Creator makes talking-avatar videos from a script, translates a video with lip-sync, and builds a digital twin of the presenter — Rs 5,800 for a 200-credit private pack or Rs 9,150 for a 1-month Creator account, covered in the HeyGen price guide. For an editor, its real job is the version of a video the client did not record: the same ad in Urdu and English, the same explainer with the founder's face when the founder is travelling, the same training video updated without a reshoot. Credits vary by length and model, so confirm what a 200-credit pack covers for your video length on WhatsApp before you pay.

How much does the set cost?

Start with Rs 1,950: CapCut Pro's Rs 650 team invite for a month of editing and Kling AI's Rs 1,300 for 660 credits of generated clips. That pair covers the cut and the shots you never filmed. Add Artlist's Rs 3,500 when music licensing starts mattering, Envato's Rs 2,000 month when templates and presets would save hours, and HeyGen or Higgsfield only when a client asks for avatars or cinematic AI shots by name. Buy the tool for the job on the desk today, not the job you hope arrives next month — credits do not get cheaper sitting unused.
