ElevenLabs vs HeyGen in Pakistan: Voice or Video?

Published 27 November 2026 8 min read MALIK DATA CENTRE Team
Quick answer: ElevenLabs Creator costs from Rs 2,300 for 1 month and is the voice tool — cloning, narration and dubbing. HeyGen Creator costs from Rs 5,800 for 200 credits and is the video tool — talking avatars and AI presenters. Buy ElevenLabs if your output is audio, HeyGen if your output is a face on screen.

ElevenLabs and HeyGen are the two AI tools every Pakistani creator asks about in the same breath, and they solve different problems. ElevenLabs makes voices: clone your own, narrate a video, dub into another language. HeyGen makes faces: a talking avatar that presents your script on camera, without you ever recording. Both are in the catalog, both are bought the same way, and the price gap is real — ElevenLabs Creator from Rs 2,300 for 1 month, HeyGen Creator from Rs 5,800 for 200 credits. This comparison settles which one your work actually needs.

Voice cloning vs talking avatars

ElevenLabs is a voice studio. Record a few minutes of your voice and it clones it — then that clone reads any script you type, in your tone, in minutes. It also dubs: take a video in Urdu and get the same voice speaking English, or the other way round. For a YouTuber who records in one language and wants the other market, that is the feature that pays for the month. For a podcaster or an audiobook narrator, it is the whole product: consistent, natural voice, no studio, no retakes.

HeyGen is a video studio with a face. Pick an avatar — or clone your own face from a short video — type the script, and the avatar presents it on camera, lip-synced, with gestures. The buyer here is not a YouTuber who loves being on camera; it is the agency that needs ten presenter videos a week, the course maker who needs a consistent teacher on screen, or the marketer who needs an ad in three languages without booking a shoot. The avatar never gets tired, never misses a line, and never needs a studio day.

Quality: what each one does best

ElevenLabs wins on voice quality outright — it is the best-sounding AI voice on the market, and the clone of your own voice is close enough that your own audience will recognise it. The weakness is that it is only audio: if your content needs a face, ElevenLabs cannot give you one, and you still need to shoot or edit the video yourself. Pair it with CapCut Pro and you have the full pipeline: voice from ElevenLabs, edit in CapCut.

HeyGen wins on the finished video. The avatar, the lip-sync and the presentation are the product, and for explainers and ads it looks professional. The weakness is the voice — HeyGen's voices are good, not great, and the avatar's delivery is flatter than a real presenter. Many buyers pair the two: generate the voice in ElevenLabs, then drive a HeyGen avatar with it, and get the best of both. That costs Rs 2,300 plus Rs 5,800, and for a working creator it is money well spent.

Which jobs each one fits

Buy ElevenLabs if your output is audio: YouTube narration, faceless-channel voiceovers, podcasts, audiobooks, dubbing a video into another language. A faceless YouTube channel is the classic ElevenLabs job — the voice is the channel, and the clone keeps it consistent across a hundred videos. Our YouTubers roundup puts ElevenLabs in the starter stack for exactly that reason.

Buy HeyGen if your output is a person on screen: agency explainers, course lessons, product demos, multilingual ads. A course maker who records fifty lessons is the classic HeyGen job — one avatar, one consistent teacher, fifty scripts, no camera days. If you are a marketer who needs the same ad in Urdu, English and Arabic, HeyGen does in an afternoon what a shoot would take a week to do.

Which one should you buy?

Buy ElevenLabs Creator if you make audio. It is cheaper (from Rs 2,300 against Rs 5,800), the voice quality is the best available, and cloning your own voice is the feature that changes a faceless channel from generic to yours. Buy HeyGen Creator if you make presenter video. It is the only tool of the two that puts a face on screen, and for agencies and course makers that face is the product.

And if your work is both — a channel that needs narration and a course that needs a presenter — buy ElevenLabs first. It is the cheaper row, it improves everything you already make, and you can add HeyGen the month a video job actually lands. Spending Rs 5,800 on avatars before you have a video job is the mistake; spending Rs 2,300 on a voice you use every day is not.

Which is cheaper, ElevenLabs or HeyGen in Pakistan?

ElevenLabs Creator — from Rs 2,300 for 1 month, against HeyGen Creator from Rs 5,800 for 200 credits. ElevenLabs sells a month of voice; HeyGen sells credits of video, so the rows are not directly comparable.

Is ElevenLabs better than HeyGen?

For voice, yes — ElevenLabs has the best AI voice and voice cloning available. For video with a presenter on screen, HeyGen is the tool, because ElevenLabs makes audio only. Pick by the output, not the brand.

Can I use ElevenLabs and HeyGen together?

Yes, and many creators do: generate the voice in ElevenLabs, then drive a HeyGen avatar with it. You get the best voice and the best avatar in the same video.

Which is better for a faceless YouTube channel?

ElevenLabs. A faceless channel lives or dies on its voice, and a cloned voice keeps it consistent across a hundred videos. HeyGen fits channels that need a presenter on screen.
