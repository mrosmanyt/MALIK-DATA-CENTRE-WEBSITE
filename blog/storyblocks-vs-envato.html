Storyblocks vs Envato: Which Stock Library to Buy

Published 21 November 2026 8 min read MALIK DATA CENTRE Team
Quick answer: Storyblocks Unlimited is Rs 3,400 for 1 month of stock video, audio, and images. Envato Core is Rs 2,000 for 1 month or Rs 8,300 for 12 months, with templates, fonts, and graphics as well. Storyblocks has no 12-month row here. For a year, buy Envato. Artlist Pro is Rs 3,500 when the job is music, and its duration is listed only as Pro plan.

Storyblocks vs Envato is a library choice, and on this store it is also a length choice. Storyblocks Unlimited is Rs 3,400 for 1 month. Envato Core is Rs 2,000 for 1 month or Rs 8,300 for a year. Both say unlimited downloads. They do not contain the same kinds of files. Storyblocks is stock video, audio, and images. Envato adds templates, fonts, and graphics, and its tool page tells you to register the project name when you download.

Buy the month that matches the edit you have open this week. Buy the year only where a year exists. This comparison uses our catalog prices. It is not a tour of either company's card checkout.

Storyblocks specs are unlimited downloads, stock video, audio and images, a royalty-free licence, and 1 month of validity. The tool page says the same library focus: video, audio, sound effects, and images, for YouTube, faceless channels, editors who need B-roll, and marketers making ads.

If you need a month of B-roll and music and you already know the clip is on Storyblocks, the extra Rs 1,400 is the cost of that library, not a fee we add for the same files. Neither tool page prints a download cap beyond the word unlimited. Unlimited still means the downloads you make while the plan is active, into projects you register where the licence asks you to. It does not mean the files stay licensed if you ignore the step the Envato page names. Read that step on the Envato page. The Storyblocks licence line is "royalty-free" on the Storyblocks page. Keep the download record either way.

Storyblocks' site is storyblocks.com. Envato's is elements.envato.com. Their card prices are their invoices. Ours are the rupee rows. This page does not copy a dollar amount from either site.

Which library for which job

Buy Storyblocks at Rs 3,400 when:

  • The next four weeks are B-roll, stock shots, music, or sound effects, and you have a reason to prefer that library.
  • You do not need a 12-month stock plan. There is no Storyblocks year on this catalog. When the month ends, the row ends.
  • You will export the clips into your editor and keep those files. The login goes away with the month.

Buy Envato at Rs 2,000 when:

  • You need one month, and you also need templates, fonts, or graphics, which are on the Envato spec line.
  • You are testing the library before a year. The year is a separate row, not an automatic upgrade.

Buy Envato at Rs 8,300 when:

  • You will download stock and templates across 12 months. Storyblocks cannot match that length here, because the length is not listed.
  • Client projects are regular. Register each project at download, as the tool page says, and keep the note outside the account.

A single YouTube video that needs five clips does not need a year. A channel that publishes every week for a year should not buy twelve Storyblocks months one by one without looking at Envato's year. Twelve times Rs 3,400 is far more than Rs 8,300, and it is also the wrong comparison if the files you need are only in one library. Price the year against the year you can actually buy, then check the library.

Artlist Pro is the music-first option. It is Rs 3,500 on a ready-made account, original price Rs 3,500. Specs name Pro features, royalty-free music and sound effects, stock footage, and setup help. The duration field says Pro plan. It does not say 1 month or 12 months. Ask for the end date before you pay, the same way you would if a length is missing. Use Artlist when the hole in the video is the soundtrack. Use Storyblocks or Envato when the hole is B-roll and, for Envato, templates. The Artlist page is where that product is explained. This comparison does not replace it.

CapCut Pro is where the clips get cut. Stock is the footage. CapCut is the editor. A private CapCut month is Rs 1,250, and a 2-device month is Rs 800. Buy the editor only if you do not already have one. A stock login does not edit the timeline for you.

FAQ

Storyblocks or Envato for B-roll?

Storyblocks Unlimited is Rs 3,400 for 1 month of stock video, audio, and images. Envato Core is Rs 2,000 for 1 month or Rs 8,300 for 12 months, and it also includes templates, fonts, and graphics. For a year, Envato is the row that exists. For a month, pick the library that has the footage you will download, not only the lower price.

Is Storyblocks cheaper?

The monthly Storyblocks price is Rs 3,400. The monthly Envato price is Rs 2,000. Envato's month costs less here and covers a wider file list. Storyblocks is the video-and-audio library at its own price. There is no Storyblocks 12-month row to compare with Envato's Rs 8,300 year.

Can I use the downloads in a client project?

Envato's tool page says the licence covers commercial projects and that you register the project name when you download. Do that for each client and keep the file. Storyblocks lists a royalty-free licence. Keep the download record with the project either way.

Does either plan include the editor?

No. Both are stock libraries on ready-made accounts. Cutting the video is a separate tool. CapCut Pro's private month is Rs 1,250, and the 2-device month is Rs 800, if you need an editor from this store.
