A Windows Pro licence in Pakistan is usually bought the wrong way: a monthly "subscription" that stops working, or a key that turns out to be for the wrong edition. The honest version is simpler — a retail key you pay for once, activate yourself in two minutes, and keep for the life of that Windows installation. This guide covers the price, what a retail key actually is, what Pro adds over Home, and the exact activation steps.

The price: Rs 2,000, once

The Windows 10/11 Pro Key at MALIK DATA CENTRE costs Rs 2,000 for a lifetime licence — an instant retail key, delivered on WhatsApp after payment is confirmed. There is one row in the catalog and one price: Rs 2,000. There is no monthly version, no yearly renewal, and no second tier to compare.

That single price is the whole point. A Windows licence is not a subscription, and you should not be paying for it every month. Rs 2,000 once is the cost of the licence; the only thing you pay again is if you want a key for a second PC.

What a retail key actually is

A retail key is a full licence key sold for one PC, as opposed to an OEM key that is tied to the machine it first shipped on, or a volume key meant for organisations. The practical differences for a buyer in Pakistan are three. First, you activate it yourself with the key — no technician, no remote session, no one else touching your PC. Second, it is a lifetime licence for that Windows installation: it does not expire after a month or a year. Third, it is tied to your Microsoft account when you sign in, which is what lets Windows remember the licence if you reinstall on the same machine.

What it is not: it is not a Microsoft 365 subscription, it is not Office, and it does not include any app. It is the Windows Pro licence only. A volume licence key, by contrast, is sold to organisations for many machines and is the wrong product for a single home or office PC — if a seller offers you a "volume" or "KMS" key for one laptop, that is not a retail licence and it can stop working when the organisation's activation server is unreachable. If you also need Word, Excel and Outlook, Microsoft 365 is a separate product — Rs 9,150 for 12 months on the admin plan or Rs 29,900 for 12 months on your own email — and our Microsoft 365 price guide explains both rows.

What Pro adds over Windows Home

Most PCs in Pakistan ship with Windows Home, and for browsing, YouTube and documents, Home is enough. Pro is worth the Rs 2,000 when your work needs what Home leaves out. BitLocker encrypts your drive, so a stolen laptop does not mean stolen files. Remote Desktop lets you connect to your own PC from elsewhere. Hyper-V runs virtual machines for testing and development. Group Policy gives you real control over updates and system behaviour, which matters on a work machine.

The other common reason to buy Pro is an upgrade path: Windows 10 Home to Windows 10 Pro, or Windows 11 Home to Windows 11 Pro, without reinstalling anything. You change the key, Windows unlocks the Pro features, and your files and apps stay exactly where they are. That is the usual case for this key — a laptop that came with Home, now needed for office or development work.

How to activate with the key

Activation takes about two minutes and you do it yourself. Open Settings, go to System, then Activation, and choose Change product key. Paste the 25-character key exactly as it arrived on WhatsApp — check each group of characters, because one wrong letter is the most common reason activation fails. Click Activate, wait for Windows to confirm, and check the Activation page shows "Windows is activated with a digital licence". Our step-by-step Windows 11 Pro retail key activation guide walks through the same process with the upgrade-from-Home path and the common errors.

After activation, sign in with your Microsoft account if you have not already. That links the digital licence to your account, so a clean reinstall on the same PC reactivates automatically without typing the key again. Keep the key itself saved somewhere safe anyway — a note on your phone or an email to yourself — because you will need it if you ever move the licence to a different machine.

Windows 10 or Windows 11?

The key is sold as a Windows 10/11 Pro key because it activates either version's Pro edition. Which one you should run is a different question, and the answer is mostly your PC's age. Windows 11 needs a newer machine with TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot; if your PC qualifies, Windows 11 Pro is the current version and the one to install. If your PC is older and does not meet Windows 11's requirements, Windows 10 Pro is the right install, and the same Rs 2,000 key activates it.

If you are already on Windows 11 Home, this key is an upgrade, not a reinstall: change the key and the Pro features unlock. If you are on Windows 10 Home, the same applies, and you can then upgrade to Windows 11 Pro later — the licence carries over, because it is a Pro licence, not a version-specific one.

What happens if you reinstall Windows?

A reinstall is the moment a licence proves itself. Because the retail key creates a digital licence linked to your Microsoft account and your PC's hardware, a clean install of the same edition on the same machine usually reactivates on its own as soon as you sign in — no key to type, no second purchase. That is the practical benefit of linking the licence to your account right after activation, and it is worth doing on day one rather than after a crash.

If you replace the motherboard or move to a completely different PC, Windows may ask you to reactivate, and that is when the saved key matters. Keep the original 25-character key somewhere you can find it — a note on your phone, an email to yourself, or a password manager. A retail licence is the type that can move with you; an OEM licence tied to the first machine cannot. That portability is part of what the Rs 2,000 buys, and it is the reason to keep the key even after the digital licence is linked.

Before you buy: check these four things

Your Windows edition. Check Settings, System, About, and read the edition line. A Pro key activates Pro — it will not turn Windows Home into Pro by itself unless you use the upgrade path, and it will not activate an Enterprise or Education install. Know what you have before you pay.

Your PC meets the requirements. For Windows 11, confirm TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot in your BIOS if you are unsure. The key cannot fix a PC that does not qualify for Windows 11.

Your Microsoft account. Have the account you want the licence linked to ready, and sign in after activation so the digital licence is tied to it.

Your payment proof. Pay only after confirming the price on WhatsApp, and keep the payment screenshot. The key is delivered on the same WhatsApp chat, usually within 5–30 minutes of payment confirmation.
