What to make in the first week

Express is worth Rs 650 when you will actually publish. A login you open once is an expensive bookmark at any price.

  1. One square post, one story size, and one flyer, using templates rather than a blank page. The listing includes templates for social and video.
  2. Your real text: the offer, the date, the city. A template with placeholder Latin text is not finished.
  3. One export of each, saved to your phone or laptop the same day. A ready-made login is not your archive. If you lose access at the end of the 12 months, the exported files are what you still have.
  4. A short video only if you need one. Express is not CapCut. If the job is a timed reel with captions, use a video editor and keep Express for the cover image.

Who this subscription is wrong for

Skip Rs 650 if:

  • You need Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere or After Effects. Start at the Creative Cloud page. The cheapest bundle length we list is Rs 3,900 for 4 months.
  • You need the files on an email you already own and Express cannot be put there. Canva at Rs 400 is the on-your-email design plan.
  • You wanted a month-to-month Express cancel button. We list 12 months. The term is the year.
  • You are collecting Adobe logos. One design tool is enough until the posters are actually going out.
