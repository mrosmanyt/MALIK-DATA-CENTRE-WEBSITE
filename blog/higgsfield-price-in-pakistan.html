What each listing actually includes

The two rows are not a short plan and a long plan of the same seat. They are two credit packs with different lines on the product page.

Plus is written as a 1,200 visual credits pack on a creator account. The specs add a 20-day warranty, a premium character animator, and advanced physics and motion curves. The family page describes Higgsfield as a studio with cinematic camera controls and access to more than one video and image model. The 1,200-credit line is the Plus pack. Treat "1,200" as credits on that listing, not as 1,200 exports.

Pro is written as 610 credits on a creator account. The specs name cinematic camera presets, multiple AI video models, and setup help on WhatsApp. The Pro row does not print a warranty length. Ask on WhatsApp what the warranty line is for the Pro order you are placing, and keep that reply with the payment screenshot. Do not copy the Plus plan's 20 days onto Pro.

Higgsfield's own site is higgsfield.ai. Their public credit prices move, and a Pakistan card often cannot complete that checkout. Our prices are the two rupee rows above. A creator account we deliver is not the same invoice as a card subscription you start on their site. After login, screenshot the credit balance so you can see which pack arrived.

A credit, a generation and a finished export are different units. Length, model and resolution change the estimate inside the product. This guide does not invent a "one reel equals X credits" rate. Open the estimator before you render, and stop if the estimate is larger than the budget you planned.

Plus, Pro, or a Kling clip

Pick the row by the job, not by which word sounds larger.

  1. You want the larger credit pack and the 20-day warranty line that is printed on Plus. That is Rs 7,500.
  2. You want the smaller pack, with camera presets named on the Pro listing, and you have asked what the warranty is. That is Rs 6,400.
  3. You need one product turning on a table, or a short insert, and you do not need a multi-model studio. Read the Kling AI price in Pakistan. Kling Standard is Rs 1,300 for 660 credits or Rs 2,400 for a 750-credit plan.

Kling is the single video model. Higgsfield is the studio with presets and more than one model. If the brief is "make this shoe rotate," start with Kling and spend the smaller rupee amount. If the brief is "try three camera moves and two models on the same ad," the Higgsfield login is the one that matches the job. Buying both in the same week is how a test budget disappears. Finish one pack, export the clips you will actually post, then decide.

Do not buy the Rs 7,500 pack to see whether AI video is useful at all. One short Kling generation, or a single Pro pack if you already know you want Higgsfield's presets, answers that question. Move to Plus when you have already burned a smaller pack and you know the estimator.
