Kling, when the job is a clip

Kling AI is the text-to-video and image-to-video tool with a credit number on the listing. The 660-credit plan is Rs 1,300, written as monthly, with an original price of Rs 4,000. Specs name a 660-credit pack, a realistic physics engine line, high-definition output, and fast-queue processing. The 750-credit Standard plan is Rs 2,400, original price Rs 2,400. That duration does not say "monthly". Do not assume the 750 credits refresh. The Kling price guide is the page that keeps those two rows apart.

Kling spends credits by length and settings. This guide does not invent a "one video equals X credits" rate. Read the estimate in the generate button. Screenshot the balance on day one.

Buy Kling when:

  • The output is a short generated clip, not a presenter and not a full edit.
  • You want a stated credit pack, and you accept that 750 credits may not be a monthly refresh.
  • Rs 1,300 is the budget and 660 monthly credits is enough to test.

Do not buy Kling when the shot depends on a specific camera preset from a multi-model studio. That is Higgsfield's listing, not Kling's.

Higgsfield, when you need the studio

Higgsfield is the AI video studio. Plus is Rs 7,500 for 1,200 credits, original price Rs 15,000, with a 20-day warranty on that listing. Specs name a 1,200 visual credit pack, a premium character animator, and advanced physics and motion curves. Pro is Rs 6,400 for 610 credits, original price Rs 6,400. Pro's specs name cinematic camera presets and multiple AI video models. Pro does not print a warranty day count. The word Pro is the cheaper row here because it lists fewer credits. The Higgsfield price guide explains that naming trap.

Buy Plus when 1,200 credits and the 20-day warranty line are what you need. Buy Pro when the camera presets and the multi-model studio matter more than the larger credit number, and you have asked about warranty before you pay. A credit is still not a finished video. Render one clip and read the cost before you spend the pack on a client job.

Higgsfield's own site is higgsfield.ai. Their card price is their invoice. Ours are the two rupee rows.

HeyGen, when a person has to talk

HeyGen makes talking-avatar videos. The 200-credit pack is Rs 5,800 on a private account, original price Rs 12,000. Specs name 200 video credits, 80-plus avatars, voice cloning, and 1080p rendering. The 1-month Creator account is Rs 9,150, original price Rs 9,150, with a 20-day warranty on that listing. It is a monthly account, separate from the 200-credit pack. The HeyGen price guide says credit cost depends on the model and the length, so 200 credits is a small batch, not 200 finished ads.

Buy HeyGen when the output is a presenter: a course, an explainer, a talking ad. Do not buy it to animate a product on a table. That is a clip tool. Voice cloning on the spec line is not a licence to clone a person who did not agree. Use a voice you have the right to use.

If the job is a simple talking photo rather than a full avatar studio, DreamFace is Rs 1,400 for 1 month or Rs 4,200 for 12 months. The DreamFace price guide is that product. It is not HeyGen at a discount.

Voice without a face is ElevenLabs. The Creator price guide lists private accounts at Rs 2,300 for 1 month, Rs 3,600 for 3 months, and Rs 12,000 for 12 months, plus a separate 1-month activation link at Rs 3,350. Narration is not an avatar. Buy the voice when you already have footage.

FAQ

Which AI video tool should I buy in Pakistan?

Match the output. Kling from Rs 1,300 for a generated clip. Higgsfield from Rs 6,400 when you need camera presets or several models. HeyGen from Rs 5,800 for a talking avatar. CapCut from Rs 800 to edit. Cinem at Rs 7,000 for a monthly portal with no printed credit count.

Is the cheapest row the best?

No. CapCut at Rs 800 does not generate a cinematic clip. Kling at Rs 1,300 does not cut a Reel with captions. HeyGen at Rs 5,800 does not replace an editor. The lowest price is the best only when it is the right shape.

Do credits equal finished videos?

No. Kling, Higgsfield, and HeyGen spend credits by length and settings. Read the estimate in the app. Cinem does not print a credit count at all.

Can I pay without a credit card?

Yes. In Pakistan use EasyPaisa, JazzCash, NayaPay, UPaisa, Meezan Bank or bank transfer. Outside Pakistan use USDT via Binance on BEP20 or TRC20. Name the row on WhatsApp +92 348 9057646.

What if I only need a talking photo?

DreamFace is Rs 1,400 for 1 month or Rs 4,200 for 12 months. That is a simpler talking-photo app, not HeyGen's avatar studio. Read the DreamFace page before you pay the HeyGen figure.
