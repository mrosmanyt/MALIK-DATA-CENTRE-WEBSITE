How to size 100, 200, 400 and 600 credits

Lovable spends credits when you build. On Lovable's own docs, the cost of a build message depends on how hard the task is. Plan mode is listed at 1 credit per message. A one-line colour change and a "rebuild the checkout" message are not the same spend. That is why a credit count is a budget, not a page count.

Our sizing, for the plans on this store:

  • 100 credits is a short test or a very small page with few revisions. The 1-month ready-made account is Rs 2,200. The 3-month ready-made account is Rs 4,650 and still has 100 credits a month, not 300 in a single pile on day one. Buy 100 if you want to learn the editor. Do not buy 100 if the brief already has a login, a database and a list of changes.
  • 200 credits is the landing-page size. The plan is Rs 3,500, activated on your email, with private app development, deploy and GitHub sync on the listing. One clear page, a form, and a handful of revisions fit this better than they fit 100. A second round of "make it look like a different brand" can eat the rest. Write the brief before you start so you are not paying credits to discover the offer.
  • 400 credits is the step up for a real app. The plan is Rs 5,000 on your email. The specs add custom domain mapping and a 30-day project-support line, plus deploy and GitHub sync. Use this when the product has more than one screen and you expect to argue with the first version.
  • 600 credits is the largest Pro row we sell, at Rs 6,000 on your email. The listing describes it for agency-scale builds and more advanced apps, with custom domains and API access on the specs. Choose 600 when several people will request changes, or when the first build is already bigger than a landing page. It is still a monthly credit plan, not an unlimited builder.

Lite at Rs 2,400 is 300 credits on a 12-month ready-made account. The name is Lovable Lite. It is the light, long plan. It is the wrong row if you wanted Pro features and 600 credits. Read the plan name on the account after delivery.

A simple way to choose before you pay:

  1. Write the screens on paper. One page is a landing-page job. Five screens with accounts is an app.
  2. Count the revision rounds you are likely to ask for. Each round spends credits. Two rounds on a landing page still belong near 200. Five rounds on an app belong near 400 or 600.
  3. If you are unsure, buy one month of 100 credits at Rs 2,200 or the 200-credit plan at Rs 3,500 and spend ten credits on a real section of the brief. The spend on that section tells you more than a guess.
  4. Move up a tier for the next month if the balance hit zero while the app was still wrong. Message us on WhatsApp and we will point you at the next row we actually sell.

Lovable, Cursor, or Replit

Lovable builds an app from prompts. Cursor edits a repository on your computer. Replit is the browser IDE. The three-way page is Cursor vs Replit vs Lovable. Cursor's rupee prices are in the Cursor Pro price guide: Rs 4,200 for 1 month or Rs 21,000 for 12 months, on a private account.

Stay on Lovable when you do not have a codebase and you want a published interface this week. The 200-credit plan at Rs 3,500 is the landing page. The 400-credit plan at Rs 5,000 and the 600-credit plan at Rs 6,000 are the app sizes.

Move to Cursor when a developer must live in the code. Lovable's Pro docs include GitHub sync and code download, and that export is the handover. Cursor Pro at Rs 4,200 for a month is the editor once the project is a repository. Move to Replit, on the Replit Core page, when the writer wants the IDE and the host in the browser. Spend the first evening on 100 or 200 credits, then decide whether the next month stays on Lovable.
