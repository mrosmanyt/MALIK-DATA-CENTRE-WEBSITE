Accountants live in spreadsheets, reports and client emails — and AI tools now do the slow parts of all three. The right stack for a Pakistani practice is not one expensive tool; it is five cheap ones that each remove a daily chore: drafting, organising, presenting, proofreading, and the Office apps themselves. Every price below is a real MALIK DATA CENTRE catalog price in PKR. This is the shortlist.

ChatGPT Go — drafting and explaining

ChatGPT Go at Rs 2,200 for 6 months is the daily helper: draft a client email, explain a tax rule in plain Urdu or English, check a formula, summarise a circular. It is the cheapest chat plan in the catalog and the one every practice should own first. Our ChatGPT Go price post covers the 6-month and 3-month plans. One rule: never paste client names, CNICs or private figures into any AI chat — draft with sample numbers, then fill in the real ones yourself.

Notion Business — client work

Notion Business from Rs 1,250 for 3 months (or Rs 4,600 for 12 months) is where the practice lives: a page per client, checklists per return, deadlines, notes and templates in one workspace, with AI to summarise and draft inside it. For a firm with more than a handful of clients, it replaces the scattered spreadsheets and the WhatsApp notes. Our Notion Business price post covers the 3-month and 12-month workspace plans.

Gamma Pro — client decks

Gamma Pro from Rs 2,400 a month turns a prompt into a client-ready deck — the quarterly review, the tax-planning presentation, the proposal — with PDF and PowerPoint export. An accountant who presents to clients monthly will save a full day per deck. Our Gamma Pro price post covers the 1-month and 12-month plans. Pair it with ChatGPT Go: Go drafts the content, Gamma builds the slides.

Grammarly Pro — error-free reports

Grammarly Pro at Rs 1,400 a month (or Rs 3,500 for 12 months) is the safety net: grammar, tone and clarity checked as you type in Gmail, Word and Docs, plus a plagiarism check. A report with one wrong figure is a problem; a report with a wrong figure and bad English is a lost client. Our Grammarly Pro price post covers both plans. For a practice, the Rs 3,500 year is the obvious buy.

Microsoft 365 — Excel and Word

Microsoft 365 from Rs 9,150 for 12 months (admin plan; Rs 29,900 on your own email) is the foundation everything else sits on: Excel for the numbers, Word for the reports, Outlook for the client mail. No AI stack replaces it — the AI tools make the work faster, but the work still lives in Excel. Our Microsoft 365 price post explains the two 12-month plans. Buy it once a year and stop thinking about it.

FAQ

Which AI tool should an accountant buy first?

ChatGPT Go at Rs 2,200 for 6 months — drafting, explaining and daily help. Then add Grammarly Pro for error-free reports and Notion for client work.

Can AI do Excel work for accountants?

AI can explain formulas, draft them and check logic — but the numbers still live in Excel, which is why Microsoft 365 from Rs 9,150 a year stays in the stack.

Is ChatGPT safe for client data?

No AI chat is safe for private client data. Never paste client names, CNICs or real figures — draft with sample numbers and fill in the real ones yourself.

What is the cheapest starter stack for an accountant?

ChatGPT Go Rs 2,200 for 6 months plus Grammarly Pro Rs 1,400 for a month — about Rs 3,600 to start, covering drafting and proofreading.

Do these tools replace an accountant?

No. They remove the slow drafting, organising and formatting — the judgement, the law and the client relationship stay with the accountant.

Accountant ke liye sab se pehle kaun sa AI tool lein?

Pehle ChatGPT Go lein — Rs 2,200 chhe mahine ke liye, drafting aur samjhane ke liye. Phir Grammarly Pro aur Notion add karein.
