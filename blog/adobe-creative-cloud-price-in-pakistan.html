Look at the 3-month and 4-month rows together. The 4-month plan is Rs 3,900 and the 3-month plan is Rs 4,000. The longer of those two costs less, and the 4-month row is the one written as activated on your email. If your project fits in a term or a client job, take 4 months rather than 3.

What Adobe lists as the official price

Adobe's Photoshop page is one of the public cards. When we checked it, Creative Cloud Pro — Photoshop plus 20+ other apps — was listed at $69.99 a month (official) on the annual plan billed monthly. The same card showed a new-subscriber offer of $34.99 a month (official) for the first 3 months, then the regular monthly amount. Photoshop on its own was listed at $22.99 a month (official) on that annual billed-monthly plan. Adobe's plans page is the wider menu, and the price changes with country, tax and whether you are a student.

Those dollar figures are Adobe's checkout. Our prices are the rupee rows above. A Pakistan buyer comparing $69.99 a month with Rs 12,000 for a year is comparing a card subscription billed by Adobe with a fixed-length plan bought here. Read the plan name inside the Adobe account after activation. Creative Cloud Pro, a single-app plan, and a student plan are different products. We sell the full bundle lengths in the table, not a Photoshop-only seat and not Adobe's student discount.

The 12-month listing on our side names Photoshop, Illustrator and Premiere, Firefly generative AI, and 100GB of cloud storage, with a one-year warranty line in the specs. The 4-month listing names Photoshop, Premiere and Illustrator, Firefly credits, 100GB of cloud sync, and a 4-month warranty. The 3-month listing says 20+ creative apps, Firefly credits and a 3-month duration. The 6-month listing adds Acrobat and mobile apps. If you need one named app confirmed before you pay, ask on WhatsApp and wait for the reply. The bundle is the full suite, and a single-app need does not get a separate cheaper row on this page.

Do not buy the 3-month row when the 4-month row is cheaper and longer. The only reason to look at 3 months is if you specifically want that listing's setup wording. Ask which login style you are getting. The 4-month and 12-month descriptions say the plan is activated on the client email. The 3-month and 6-month descriptions say Creative Cloud suite. Own-email activation means your files stay in your Adobe account. A shared or pre-made login means you export projects to your disk before the plan ends.

Students who need the apps for a portfolio should still learn the real tools. A semester fits the 4-month plan. A full academic year fits the 12-month plan. Adobe's own student price, when you qualify, is a different offer on Adobe's site. If you already have a student email that Adobe accepts, compare that checkout before you order a rupee plan. Firefly is included as a feature line on these listings. Generative credits on Adobe's own plans are metered, and the meter changes. Do not plan a month of client images on an assumed credit count. Open the Firefly counter after activation and spend a few credits on a test before you promise a client a set of generated images.

How activation works

  1. Open the Adobe Creative Cloud page and choose 3, 4, 6 or 12 months.
  2. Pay in PKR or USDT and send the screenshot on WhatsApp.
  3. For an own-email plan, send the Adobe email you control. Use an address you will still have when the plan ends. We do not need the password.
  4. The delivery policy says most orders are activated within 5–30 minutes after payment is confirmed. Install the Creative Cloud desktop app from Adobe and sign in.
  5. Open Photoshop and Premiere, not only the web page. Confirm the apps launch and the end date matches the row you paid for.
  6. If an app is missing or the login is a different product, screenshot it the same day. The refund policy explains repair, replacement and a refund when we cannot deliver.

Cloud files in the 100GB line live in Adobe's storage. Download anything a client paid for onto your own disk as well. When a fixed-length plan ends, you should still have the PSD, AI and Premiere project files.

What to install on the first day

Adobe's desktop app will offer every product in the suite. Installing all of them at once fills a laptop. Install only what the job needs, then add the rest.

  • A designer: Photoshop and Illustrator first. InDesign if the work is multi-page. Acrobat if you mark up PDFs.
  • An editor: Premiere Pro first, then After Effects if the job has motion graphics. Photoshop for thumbnails and stills.
  • A student: the two apps your course names, plus Acrobat. Add the others when an assignment asks for them.

Sign out of any old Adobe ID on the machine before you sign in with the email we activated. Two IDs on one install is the usual reason an app opens as a trial. After the right ID is in, make one small file and save it locally. That file is the proof the plan works.

Is the 4-month plan really cheaper than 3 months?

No. The listings are the full Creative Cloud bundle: Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects, Acrobat and Firefly features, as written on each row. Adobe Express at Rs 650 is a different, lighter app. Canva Pro at Rs 400 is not Adobe.
