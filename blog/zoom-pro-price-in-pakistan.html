A free Zoom meeting ends at 40 minutes. That is the moment a tuition class, a client demo or a university viva breaks, and everyone waits for a second link. This page is the Zoom Pro price in Pakistan: Rs 1,500 for a month, what the listing actually includes, and who should stay on the free plan. It is for tutors, small teams and anyone hosting the call. It is not a webinar product and it is not Microsoft 365.

What the month is for

The tool page describes Zoom Pro as the plan that removes the 40-minute cutoff and adds cloud recording and Zoom AI Companion. The catalog row is more specific about limits: meetings up to 30 hours, up to 100 participants, cloud recording, monthly warranty. Use those lines. Do not add a participant count you saw in an ad.

Who this month fits:

  • A tutor whose class runs 60 to 90 minutes. The free cutoff is the whole problem. One month at Rs 1,500 covers a term's worth of tests if you only need Pro for exam weeks, or one month of regular classes if you will decide later.
  • A freelancer who demos work to one client and cannot restart the call when the timer ends. Host the meeting from the Pro login. The client can stay on free Zoom.
  • A small office that records a weekly call and wants the recording in Zoom's cloud, as the listing states, rather than a screen recording on one laptop.

Who should not buy it:

  • You only join meetings that someone else hosts. Their plan controls the timer. Your free account is enough.
  • You need Word, Excel and a shared drive. That is Microsoft 365, which is a different price and a 12-month email activation. Zoom does not include Office.
  • You need a webinar for hundreds of strangers, registration pages, or a branded event. This row says 100 participants. It is a Pro meeting login, not an event product we have not listed.

Zoom's own prices are on zoom.us. They bill in the currency and tax of the account, usually on a card, and the public cards change. Our price is Rs 1,500 for the month described above. A card subscription you start on Zoom's site is their invoice. Do not pay Zoom and this store for the same host account in the same month unless you have been told they are different logins.

How a class should use the month

Treat the first paid class as a test, not as the exam.

  1. Start a meeting alone. Confirm it does not warn you about a 40-minute end.
  2. Record two minutes to the cloud, then stop, and find the file. If cloud recording is the reason you paid, this is the check. A local recording on your laptop is a different feature and does not prove the cloud line.
  3. Invite one student or colleague. Confirm they can hear you and that the participant count you need is under 100.
  4. Save the recording somewhere you control if the login is not your old Zoom account. When the month ends, a login you do not own is a bad place for the only copy of a lecture.
  5. Turn off what you do not need. AI Companion, if it shows on the account, is a notes aid. It is not a second teacher, and it should not be left running on a call where students did not expect a transcript.

The tool page says Pro supports up to 100 participants per meeting by default. If your academy puts 120 students in one room, this row is the wrong product. Split the class or ask us before you pay. We will not pretend the listing says 120.

For client calls, send the meeting from the Pro host. If you join the client's room, you are a guest, and their plan decides the length. Buying Pro and then joining someone else's free room does not extend their meeting.

How to order and what to keep

  1. Message WhatsApp +92 344 5739206 with "Zoom Pro, 1 month" and say whether you need a new login or an upgrade on an email you already use.
  2. Pay Rs 1,500 and send the screenshot. The delivery policy says most orders are ready within 5–30 minutes after payment is confirmed.
  3. Do not send your email password. A one-time code from Zoom is also not something to forward.
  4. Run the four checks above on the same day. If the meeting still dies at 40 minutes, screenshot the timer and the account plan page. The refund policy is the process when the login is not the plan you paid for.

The warranty line on the listing is a full monthly warranty. That is the month you paid for, not a second month. Put the end date in your calendar. A tuition centre that forgets the end date finds out during a class.

Cloud recordings can fill storage. Download the files you must keep. We do not promise a particular gigabyte number, because the catalog does not print one. If Zoom shows a storage warning, that warning is Zoom's, and the fix is to download or delete, not to assume a bigger disk was included.
