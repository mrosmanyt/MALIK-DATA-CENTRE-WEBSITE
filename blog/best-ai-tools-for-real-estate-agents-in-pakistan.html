Listing photos and flyers

Photos sell the viewing; the flyer sells the click. Canva Pro at Rs 400 for a year makes the listing flyer, the board, the WhatsApp status image and the Facebook post from one design — the Canva Pro price guide explains the on-your-own-email activation. Picsart Pro at Rs 3,500 for 11 months is the phone editor for the photos themselves: brighten a dark room, straighten a crooked shot, remove clutter from the frame — the Picsart price guide has the full detail. Edit on the phone at the property, design in Canva on the way back, post before the next agent does.

Listing words

ChatGPT Go writes the listing description, the WhatsApp reply and the follow-up script — Rs 2,200 for 6 months, covered in the ChatGPT Go price guide. Give it the facts (area, size, demand, nearby schools and hospitals) and it returns a description in English or Roman Urdu; check the facts before you post, because an invented plot size is a lost viewing and a lost reputation. Gemini Pro at Rs 1,500 a year on your Gmail does the research behind the words: area guides, price trends and the answers to "is this society a good investment?" — the Gemini Pro price guide covers both rows. One writes the listing, the other makes sure the listing is true.

Video tours

Video is where listings are won now. CapCut Pro cuts the walkthrough into a reel with captions and music — from Rs 650 for a 1-month team invite, Rs 800 for a month on 2 devices, or Rs 1,250 for a private month; the CapCut Pro price guide explains every row. vidIQ Max makes the tour findable on YouTube: titles, tags and the keywords buyers actually search — Rs 700 a month for Boost or Rs 1,500 for Max, covered in the vidIQ price guide. Film the tour on the phone, cut it in CapCut the same evening, title it with vidIQ, and the listing works while you sleep.

Winning the seller

Gamma Pro is the tool for the meeting, not the listing: a clean pitch deck for a seller or a developer, built from a prompt — Rs 2,400 for a month or Rs 9,500 for a year, covered in the Gamma price guide. A valuation deck with comparable listings, photos and a marketing plan wins mandates that a verbal promise does not. Run it the month a big mandate is on the table, not every month of the year.
