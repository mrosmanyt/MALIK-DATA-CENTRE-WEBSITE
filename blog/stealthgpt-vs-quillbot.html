Which job is which

Buy QuillBot at Rs 1,800 when:

  • You need to paraphrase a passage you are allowed to rewrite, and you will still cite the source.
  • You want a summarizer for a reading you have already done, not a replacement for reading it.
  • You want the plagiarism checker on that spec line, for 1 month, on a ready-made account.
  • You need the length in writing, and "1 month" is the length you need.

Buy StealthGPT at Rs 2,400 when:

  • You want the humanizer the listing names, which rewrites AI-generated text so it reads more naturally.
  • You also want the essay and content writer on the same Pro row.
  • You have asked for the end date, because the catalog did not print one.
  • You will read the output yourself. A smoother sentence can still be a false sentence.

Buy neither when the task is grammar and tone on an email you own. That is Grammarly Pro, Rs 1,400 for 1 month or Rs 3,500 for 12 months, activated on your email. The year shows an original price of Rs 12,000. The Grammarly price guide explains the plagiarism checker and the AI rewrites on Grammarly's side. Grammarly's rewrites are editing suggestions. They are not StealthGPT's humanizer, and they are not QuillBot's paraphrase modes.

Buy a checker, not a rewriter, when the question is whether a draft looks machine-written. Pangram Pro is Rs 8,350 for 12 months. A result there is one signal, not a verdict. The Pangram price guide says so. Do not buy StealthGPT to "beat" Pangram. The catalog does not claim that, and this page will not describe a bypass.

What neither purchase changes

A course, a client, or a publisher can forbid undisclosed AI writing. QuillBot and StealthGPT do not file a disclosure, and they do not make a forbidden workflow allowed. If the rule says you must write it yourself, follow the rule. The store sells the software. It does not sell a way around an academic policy.

Both tools can drop a fact. Compare the output to the source. Numbers, names, and citations are your job. QuillBot's plagiarism checker is a spec line on the month. It is not a promise that a paraphrase is properly cited. StealthGPT's listing does not include a plagiarism checker at all. If that checker is the reason you are paying, the Rs 1,800 row is the one that names it.

Both accounts are ready-made. Do not change the email or the password until the chat says you can. Do not paste a client's unreleased contract, an exam, or a password into either box. Copy finished text to your own disk. The login is not your archive. A ready-made account is not your old login unless the reply says this order extends it.

Word limits differ in a way the catalog only half describes. QuillBot's row says unlimited paraphrasing. StealthGPT's row does not say unlimited. It says Pro plan features and that the plan raises limits, without a number. Screenshot the cap after login on StealthGPT. If you bought it for a long document and the cap blocks you, that screenshot is the evidence from day one.

StealthGPT or QuillBot?

QuillBot's price is lower, at Rs 1,800 against Rs 2,400. Cheaper is not the same tool. A printed month can be worth more than a cheaper-looking plan whose end date you have not asked for. Ask for StealthGPT's end date before you compare the two numbers.

Neither listing promises that. Do not buy them as a bypass. Pangram, at Rs 8,350 for 12 months, is a checker and still not a verdict. Follow your school or client's rules on AI writing.
