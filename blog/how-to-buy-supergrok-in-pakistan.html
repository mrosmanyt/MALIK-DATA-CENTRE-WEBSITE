How activation works

Activation is done on your own email, not a shared login. That matters: your Grok history, your X account and your settings stay yours, and nobody else can use your month. After payment, our team activates SuperGrok on the email you sent, then messages you on WhatsApp to confirm it is live. Open X, check Grok, and you should see the SuperGrok limits — higher usage, image generation and the full model. If anything does not show up, message the same WhatsApp number and the team fixes it under warranty.

SuperGrok vs ChatGPT Plus

The question most buyers ask before paying is whether to buy SuperGrok or ChatGPT Plus. Our SuperGrok vs ChatGPT Plus comparison settles it in full. If your day is on X, buy SuperGrok; if your day is documents and code, buy Plus.
