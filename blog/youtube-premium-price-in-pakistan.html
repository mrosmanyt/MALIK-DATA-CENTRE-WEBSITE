What is included?

YouTube's help page on YouTube Premium benefits describes the membership as ad-free viewing, background play, downloads for offline watching, and YouTube Music Premium. In practice that means:

  • No ads on YouTube for the membership you activate, across the phone, computer and TV apps signed into that Google account.
  • Background play. The video keeps running when you leave the app, which is the feature commuters notice first.
  • Downloads. Lectures, playlists and long videos can sit on the phone for a bus ride with bad data.
  • YouTube Music Premium on the same membership, so you are not paying a second music app unless you prefer one.

Your subscriptions, watch history and playlists stay where they are, because the plan is added to your account. That is the point of "activated on your own email". A shared Premium login would mix recommendations and, worse, put your history on someone else's account. We do not sell this one that way.

Premium does not remove every paid promotion a creator reads out loud. It removes YouTube's ads. It also does not upload your videos for you or improve your rankings. That job belongs to a research tool such as vidIQ, which we cover in the YouTuber stack guide.

What does YouTube charge in Pakistan?

YouTube shows the monthly price for your country on youtube.com/premium. It is billed by Google, usually on a card, and it renews until you cancel. Google's payment help for Pakistan has long listed card payments rather than JazzCash or EasyPaisa for a direct YouTube charge.

Our plans are a different shape. You pay once for 1, 3 or 12 months, on the email you already watch with, and you are not starting a card trial. If YouTube's Individual price is still near PKR 479, the official monthly plan is cheaper than our 1-month price of Rs 500 for someone whose card works and who is happy to manage renewal. Our 12-month plan is for people who want the year paid and finished, or whose card is declined. Do the maths on the day you buy, using YouTube's live number, not this paragraph.

Family, student, or a plan on your own email?

Pick from how you actually watch.

  • One person, card works, you will remember to cancel. YouTube's own monthly Individual plan is the direct route. Start at the official page.
  • Six people at one address. YouTube's Family plan is built for that, with separate watch histories. Our store plans are for one email. Do not buy one personal activation and expect it to cover a household.
  • A student who can pass YouTube's verification. The official Student price is usually lower than Individual. Use it if you qualify.
  • You want several months paid in PKR without a card. Use our 3-month or 12-month activation on your own Gmail.

We only need the Google email address where Premium should appear. We do not need the password. If someone on WhatsApp asks for the password "to upgrade faster", that is the wrong seller. Our refund policy and delivery policy are the documents to read before you send money. Most activations finish within 5 to 30 minutes of confirmed payment, and you get the steps on WhatsApp.

How to confirm it is active

After we message you, check the membership on the same account you watch with.

  1. Open YouTube on the phone, signed into that Gmail.
  2. Tap your picture, then Purchases and memberships, or open the Premium page while signed in.
  3. Confirm the membership shows as active and that a video which had an ad before now starts without one.
  4. Turn on one download and one background-play test.
  5. Open YouTube Music with the same account and confirm Premium is there too.

If the membership is missing, send a screenshot the same day. Do not create a second Gmail and ask us to move it after the fact. The email you send at order time is the email that gets the plan.

TV apps matter. Sign the TV, the console or the Chromecast into the same Google account. Premium follows the account, not the device, but a TV left on a different family member's login will still show ads.

Who should skip Premium?

Premium is a comfort purchase. Skip it if money is tight and you only watch two videos a week. Skip it if your real problem is views, not ads. A new channel needs ideas, titles and thumbnails more than an ad-free homepage. vidIQ Boost on our store is Rs 700 for 1 month and vidIQ Max is Rs 1,500 for 1 month, both delivered on WhatsApp. Those are research tools. They do not remove ads.

Also skip any "lifetime YouTube Premium" offer. YouTube sells monthly memberships. A lifetime claim is the same pattern we describe in how to spot a fake AI subscription seller: an official product sold in a form the company does not sell.

How to order

  1. Decide the duration from the table above.
  2. Open YouTube Premium and check that the button still shows the same price.
  3. Send your Gmail address on WhatsApp. Not the password.
  4. Pay and keep the screenshot until the membership shows inside your account.
  5. Test ads, downloads and YouTube Music before you close the chat.

If you are buying Premium because you run a channel, pair it with the editing and research tools in the YouTuber guide rather than buying Premium alone and hoping the algorithm moves.

FAQ

What is the YouTube Premium price in Pakistan?

On our store, on your own email: Rs 500 for 1 month, Rs 1,200 for 3 months, Rs 6,200 for 12 months. YouTube's own monthly Individual price was reported at PKR 479 on 28 September 2026. Check youtube.com/premium for today's number.

YouTube Premium apni email pe chalega?

Haan. Plan aap ke Google account pe activate hota hai. History, subscriptions aur playlists wahi rehte hain. Password nahi dena.

Does it include YouTube Music?

Yes. YouTube's description of Premium includes YouTube Music Premium. Sign into Music with the same Google account and confirm it after activation.

Can my whole family use one plan?

YouTube's Family membership is the product built for a household, up to six people at one address on Google's rules. Our activations are for the single email you give us.

Will Premium get me more views?

No. It changes how you watch. Rankings, titles and thumbnails are a separate job. Look at vidIQ and the rest of a creator stack if growth is the goal.
