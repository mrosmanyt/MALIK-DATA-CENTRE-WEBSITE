FAQ

Which AI tool should a marketer buy first?

ChatGPT Go at Rs 2,200 for 6 months — copy, ideas and daily help. Then add Canva Pro for creatives and CapCut Pro for video.

Can AI write ad copy that actually converts?

AI writes strong first drafts and variations fast. The converting part — the offer, the audience insight, the angle — still comes from the marketer. Use AI for volume, use your judgement for the message.

What is the cheapest starter stack for a marketer?

ChatGPT Go Rs 2,200 for 6 months plus Canva Pro Rs 400 a year — about Rs 2,600 to start, covering copy and creatives.

Do I need vidIQ if I only run Facebook ads?

No. vidIQ is a YouTube tool — buy it only when a client has a channel to grow. For Facebook and Instagram campaigns, the Go, Canva and CapCut trio is enough.

Do these tools replace a marketer?

No. They remove the slow drafting, designing and editing — the strategy, the audience and the client relationship stay with the marketer.

Marketer ke liye sab se pehle kaun sa AI tool lein?

Pehle ChatGPT Go lein — Rs 2,200 chhe mahine ke liye, copy aur ideas ke liye. Phir Canva Pro aur CapCut Pro add karein.
