What Bolt.new Pro is

Bolt.new is a browser-based AI development environment built by StackBlitz. You describe the app you want — a booking page, a dashboard, a storefront, a tool — and it writes the code, installs the packages and runs the app in a live preview, all inside the browser tab. You then refine it by chatting: change the colours, add a page, connect a form, fix the bug it just introduced. There is nothing to install and no terminal to open, which is why non-developers can use it at all.

Pro is the paid tier that makes this usable for real work. The free tier gives you a small daily token allowance that runs out in the middle of a build; Pro gives you a much larger monthly allowance, more private projects, and the ability to deploy. If you have ever had Bolt.new stop halfway through a feature because the free tokens ran out, you already know what Pro is for.

What you get

The core of Pro is tokens — the fuel the AI spends while it writes and edits your code. A bigger allowance means longer build sessions, more iterations, and the freedom to ask for a redesign without counting every message. Pro also lets you deploy directly, so the app you built gets a live link you can share with a client or a user instead of a preview that only you can see.

You also get more private projects, which matters the moment you build for clients: your work stays yours instead of public. And because Bolt.new runs on real code — a proper project with files you can download — you are not locked in. Export the project, open it in VS Code, or hand it to a developer to finish. That exit route is what separates Bolt.new from toy builders: the output is a real codebase.

Bolt.new vs the alternatives

The two closest tools in our catalog are Cursor Pro from Rs 4,200 a month and Lovable Pro from Rs 2,200 a month. Cursor is an AI code editor for developers who already write code — more control, steeper learning, and a monthly bill that adds up to far more than Rs 10,850 a year. Lovable is the closest rival: also prompt-to-app, also browser-based, and cheaper per month. The difference is the workflow — Lovable is friendlier for non-developers and integrates Supabase easily, while Bolt.new gives you a fuller development environment and a downloadable codebase.

Our Cursor vs Lovable comparison covers the editor-versus-builder choice in detail. The short version: pick Bolt.new if you want a browser IDE with real code you can export, pick Lovable if you want the simplest path from idea to live app, and pick Cursor if you already code and want an AI pair programmer.

Who should buy it

Bolt.new Pro suits founders validating a product idea, freelancers who prototype client apps, and students or developers learning to build with AI. At Rs 10,850 for the year it is cheaper than two months of Cursor, which makes it the best-value AI builder in the catalog for anyone who builds more than one app a year. It does not suit someone who only wants a marketing website — Framer Pro at Rs 6,650 a year is the better tool for that — or a team that needs production hosting and a database, where the app should live on proper infrastructure after the prototype stage.
