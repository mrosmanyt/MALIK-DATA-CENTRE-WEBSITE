n8n vs Gumloop in Pakistan: Which Automates Your Work?

Published 27 November 2026 8 min read MALIK DATA CENTRE Team
Quick answer: n8n Starter at Rs 6,500 for 12 months is the self-hosted workflow engine for builders who want control; Gumloop Pro at Rs 4,550 for 12 months is the no-code agent builder for teams who want automation without code. Pick n8n for power, Gumloop for speed.

n8n and Gumloop are the two automation tools in the catalog — and they answer the same question in opposite ways. n8n gives you the engine: self-hosted workflows you control completely. Gumloop gives you the agent: describe the job in plain words and it builds the automation for you. Both cost less than Rs 7,000 for a full year, and the right one depends on whether you write code or not. This post compares them.

What n8n does best

n8n is the workflow engine: connect apps, APIs and AI models into flows you design step by step, and run them on your own terms. It is the tool for developers and technical founders who want WhatsApp workflows, scheduled jobs, data pipelines and integrations that nobody else controls. Our n8n Starter price post covers the 12-month plan, the 5 workflows and the 20,000 executions. If you can read an API doc, n8n is the most powerful automation in the catalog.

What Gumloop does best

Gumloop Pro is the no-code agent builder: describe the task — scrape a site, summarise a page, enrich a lead list — and it builds the agent visually, no code at all. It is the tool for marketers, sellers and operators who need automation this week, not after learning a platform. Our Gumloop Pro price post covers the 12-month plan and the scraping use case. If you want results without touching code, Gumloop is the buy.

Who should buy which

Buy n8n Starter if you build with APIs and want workflows you fully control — WhatsApp automations, scheduled jobs, data pipelines. Buy Gumloop Pro if you want automation without code — scraping, lead enrichment, research agents built in plain words. A technical team often ends up with both: n8n for the core systems, Gumloop for the quick jobs the team builds itself. Our developers roundup puts n8n in the builder stack for exactly that reason.

FAQ

Which is cheaper, n8n or Gumloop?

Gumloop Pro is cheaper: Rs 4,550 for 12 months against n8n Starter at Rs 6,500 for 12 months. The Rs 1,950 gap is small — pick by how you build, not by price.

Do I need to code to use n8n?

For full power, yes — n8n is a workflow engine for builders who work with APIs. Gumloop needs no code at all.

What can Gumloop automate?

Scraping, lead enrichment, research summaries and no-code agents — describe the task in plain words and it builds the agent.

Can n8n automate WhatsApp?

Yes — WhatsApp workflows are one of the most popular n8n uses in Pakistan, which is why it is in the catalog at Rs 6,500 for the year.

How many workflows does n8n Starter include?

5 workflows with 20,000 monthly executions, on a private account with a 1-year warranty.

n8n ya Gumloop — beginner ke liye kaun sa behtar hai?

Beginner ke liye Gumloop behtar hai — bina code ke automation ban jati hai. Developer ke liye n8n zyada powerful hai.
