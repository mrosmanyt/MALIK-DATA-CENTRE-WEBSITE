Surfshark vs HMA VPN in Pakistan: Which Is Worth It?

Published 23 November 2026 8 min read MALIK DATA CENTRE Team
Quick answer: Surfshark is cheaper to start at Rs 400 for a shared month; HMA is cheaper per month over a year at Rs 5,500 for a yearly key (about Rs 458 a month). Pick by length and access type — shared login, ready-made account, coupon or licence key — not by the smallest number.

Surfshark and HMA are the two VPNs in the MALIK DATA CENTRE catalog, and they are opposite shapes: Surfshark is short and cheap, HMA is one key for a whole year. Both VPN price posts went live on this blog, and this comparison settles the question buyers ask next — which one is worth it for the way you actually use a VPN. The short answer: Surfshark wins on entry price, HMA wins on a year without renewing, and the access type matters more than either price.

Surfshark's three rows

Surfshark on this store is three different products with one name, and the full detail is in our Surfshark price guide. The shared month at Rs 400 is a shared premium login with CleanWeb ad blocking, 3,200-plus server locations and unlimited device connections on its spec line — a budget connection, not a private vault, and you must not change its email or password. The 2-month account at Rs 1,900 is a ready-made Surfshark login handed to you for two months, with the listing's full warranty. The 2-month coupon at Rs 850 is a code, not a login: buy it only if you already have a Surfshark account to apply it to.

HMA's one row

HMA is simpler, and the full detail is in our HMA price guide. One row: a yearly licence key for Rs 5,500, delivered on WhatsApp, entered in the HMA app under the option for a purchase you already made. The specs are secure encryption tunnels, 1,100-plus servers, up to 5 devices, and a 12-month full warranty. Five devices is a cap, not a suggestion to share the key in a larger group — count phones, laptops and a TV box if the app is installed there. And a key is a secret: whoever redeems it first uses the year, so do not post it in a group or send it to a second seller "to check if it is real".

Devices and servers

On devices, Surfshark's shared month spec lists unlimited connections while HMA caps at five. That sounds like a Surfshark win until you remember what the shared month is: a login other people can be on. Unlimited connections on a shared profile is not the same as five devices on a key only you hold. If the VPN will run on a client laptop or anything you would not want tied to a login you do not control, the private shape matters more than the device count.

On servers, Surfshark prints 3,200-plus locations and HMA prints 1,100-plus. Those are catalog spec lines, not counts we re-measured city by city, and neither is a promise that a particular website loads in a particular city. A site that blocks a datacenter address can still fail on either VPN, and the fix is a different server inside the app, not a new claim. Pick the access type first, then the length; the server sentence comes last.

Which one should you buy?

Buy Surfshark's shared month (Rs 400) when you need a VPN this week on a budget and you accept a shared login with its rules: do not change the password, do not save banking passwords in a browser profile you only opened because the VPN was on, and do not expect it to still be yours in month three.

Buy Surfshark's 2-month account (Rs 1,900) when you want a ready-made Surfshark login for two months with a full warranty, and you do not want to apply a coupon yourself.

Buy HMA's yearly key (Rs 5,500) when you want a year, not a month you will renew by messaging again — about Rs 458 a month over the year, your own key, up to five devices, and a 12-month warranty. This is the row for a household or a traveller with a phone and a laptop.

Buy neither, and look at NordVPN instead, when the requirement is a private login on your own email. NordVPN is Rs 2,500 for monthly access activated on your email, up to 10 devices, 6,000-plus servers — one month, not a year. Our NordVPN vs Surfshark comparison covers that choice in full.

FAQ

Which is cheaper, Surfshark or HMA in Pakistan?

Surfshark is cheaper to start: Rs 400 for a shared month. HMA is cheaper per month over a year: Rs 5,500 for 12 months is about Rs 458 a month. Pick by length, not by the smallest number on the page.

Is the Rs 400 Surfshark month a private account?

No. It is a shared premium login, so treat it as a connection, not a private vault. Do not change its email or password, and do not save banking or email passwords in a browser profile you opened only because the VPN was on.

How many devices does HMA cover?

Up to 5 devices on the yearly key, with a 12-month warranty. Count every device the app is installed on, including a TV box.

Can I use the Surfshark coupon without a Surfshark account?

No. The Rs 850 coupon is a code to apply to an account you already have. If you want us to hand you a finished login for two months, that is the Rs 1,900 ready-made account row.
