What Gemini Plus is

Gemini Plus is a Google AI plan that upgrades the Gemini app on your Gmail: a more capable model, higher limits, and access to Google's AI features inside the apps you already use. It is not the same as Gemini Pro — Pro at Rs 1,500 a year on your Gmail is the bigger plan with Veo video access through Flow, and our how-to-buy Gemini Pro guide covers that route. Plus is the budget door: the cheapest way to stop hitting the free tier's limits, for students and casual users who want a better Gemini without paying for video generation they will never use.

How to activate the link

Activation takes about two minutes. First, sign in to the Gmail you gave us — on the same browser, not a different account. Then open the activation link we sent and follow the prompts to accept the plan on that account. When it finishes, open the Gemini app and check that the plan name shows as active; ask it a question and confirm you are getting the upgraded model rather than the free tier. If the link says it is expired or for a different account, do not try to force it — screenshot the message and send it to us on WhatsApp and we will sort it out. Never share the activation link with anyone else; it is tied to your account.

Gemini Plus vs Gemini Pro

The choice is simple once you know what each plan is for. Gemini Plus at Rs 600 for 12 months is for chatting, drafting, studying and everyday help — the cheapest Google AI upgrade there is. Gemini Pro at Rs 1,500 a year is for people who also want Veo 3 video generation in Flow, bigger limits, and the fuller Google AI workspace; our Gemini Pro study guide shows what students do with it. If you only want a better chatbot, buy Plus and save the Rs 900. If you want to make AI videos, Plus will not do it — buy Pro.
