Google AI Ultra vs Gemini Pro for Video Creators: Which Buys More Veo?

Every video creator in Pakistan asks the same question about Veo: which Google plan buys more video for the money? There is no standalone Veo subscription — Veo 3 runs inside Google Flow and is paid for with Flow credits that come with a Google AI plan. So the comparison that matters is not the plan names, it is Flow credits per rupee. This guide compares the two doors MALIK DATA CENTRE sells — Gemini Pro and Google AI Ultra — on that exact measure, without repeating the full spec table on our Gemini Pro vs Google AI Ultra comparison page.

Flow credits per rupee: the short answer

Google sells three AI plan levels in Pakistan, with published monthly Flow credit allowances: Google AI Plus at Rs 1,400 a month for 200 credits, Google AI Pro at Rs 5,600 a month for 1,000 credits, and Google AI Ultra at Rs 21,999 a month (the 20x plan) for 10,000 credits. At Google's published generation rates, a Veo Fast generation costs about 20 credits and a full Quality generation about 100 credits, so Pro's 1,000 monthly credits mean roughly 50 Fast generations or about 10 Quality generations a month.

Which one buys "more Veo" depends on whether you need a little video every week for a year, or a lot of video right now.

The Gemini Pro door: Rs 1,500 for a year of Veo

Gemini Pro is the Google AI Pro plan activated on your own Gmail for a full 12 months, at Rs 1,500 for the whole year. Because it is the Pro plan, it carries the Pro-level monthly Flow credit allowance alongside Gemini Pro itself, 2 TB of Google storage and the other Pro benefits. For a creator who makes Veo clips a few times a week, this is the cheapest legitimate route into Veo 3 in Pakistan: one yearly payment, on your own account, instead of Rs 5,600 every month to Google.

At the published rates, that monthly allowance is roughly 50 Fast generations or about 10 Quality generations a month — enough to storyboard a video, test a few versions of each scene, and render the finals at full quality. The full breakdown is in our Gemini Pro price guide, and the access routes are explained in the Veo 3 price guide. There is also a Rs 3,350 option for 2 years plus 1 month of YouTube Premium, for creators who want the longest runway.

Which creator should buy which?

  • One video a week, or a few clips for social media: Gemini Pro. A year of Pro-level credits for Rs 1,500 is unbeatable, and you will not hit the ceiling.
  • A faceless YouTube channel publishing daily: Ultra. Pro's monthly allowance runs out in the first week of daily generation, and re-rendering scenes is where credits actually go.
  • A short film, a client project, or one heavy month: the Ultra 25k slot. Buy the fixed pool, finish the project, and do not pay for a second month you do not need.
  • Not sure yet: start with Gemini Pro for the year and add one shared Ultra month (Rs 2,500) when a big project lands. That combination costs less than a single month of Google's own Ultra price.

Many of our video customers run exactly that combination: Gemini Pro for everyday Veo access, and an Ultra slot for the months they produce heavily. Dedicated video tools can cover overflow too — Kling AI plans start from Rs 1,300 — but for Veo itself, these two Google plans are the only doors.

Shared vs dedicated: what to watch

The cheapest Ultra option is a shared plan, which means the account is shared. That is fine for testing, but do not put client work, passwords or anything private in a shared login, and do not change its password or recovery email. For anything you earn money from, the dedicated options — Gemini Pro on your own Gmail, or the Ultra 25k slot — are the right buy. The same rule applies to any cheap subscription in Pakistan, and our shared vs private accounts guide explains the trade-off in full.

FAQ

Google AI Ultra vs Gemini Pro: which is better for Veo?

For value, Gemini Pro: Rs 1,500 for 12 months gives you Pro-level monthly Flow credits, roughly 50 Fast or 10 Quality Veo generations a month. For volume, Ultra: the 25,000-credit slot at Rs 26,000 or the Flow Ultra 20x plan at Rs 30,000 give far more credits in a single month. A few videos a week points to Gemini Pro; daily production points to Ultra.

Google AI Ultra vs Gemini Pro — Veo ke liye kaunsa behtar hai?

Value ke liye Gemini Pro: Rs 1,500 mein 12 months. Volume ke liye Ultra: Rs 26,000 ka 25,000-credit slot ya Rs 30,000 ka Flow Ultra 20x plan. Haftay mein chand videos hon to Gemini Pro, rozana production ho to Ultra.

How many Veo videos do Flow credits make?

At Google's published rates, a Veo Fast generation costs about 20 credits and a full Quality generation about 100 credits. On the Pro level's 1,000 monthly credits that is roughly 50 Fast generations or about 10 Quality generations a month. Ultra's pools are 10,000 credits and above.

Is there a standalone Veo 3 subscription I can buy?

No. Google does not sell Veo 3 on its own anywhere. Veo access always comes through Flow credits attached to a Google AI plan, such as Gemini Pro or Google AI Ultra. Our Veo 3 price guide explains both routes.
