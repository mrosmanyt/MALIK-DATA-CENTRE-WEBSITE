Shared base, Flow Ultra 20x, and the 25k slot

Read the access line before the feature line. The same brand name covers three different deliveries.

The shared listing is a 1-month shared account. The description says unlimited video generation, Antigravity, and the other premium features on that listing. The specs add a 25-day warranty, Veo access, and the premium AI features included with that row. "Unlimited" is the word on the listing. It is a shared login, so it is the budget way to try Ultra, not a private studio. Do not store client files you cannot afford to have on a shared login, and do not change the password. How shared logins differ from an email activation is covered in our guide to private, shared and invitation accounts.

The Flow Ultra 20x listing is a 1-month plan delivered on WhatsApp. The specs name Flow Ultra 20x usage, Veo 3 video generation, Google Antigravity, Gemini Ultra, and the other Ultra features on that row. The catalog does not print a warranty day count on this row. Ask on WhatsApp what the warranty line is for the order you are placing, and keep that reply with the payment screenshot.

The 25k slot is a dedicated slot for 1 month. The listing calls it 25,000 credits, with Gemini Ultra, Veo 3, and high-usage AI features. The specs add a 28-day warranty and manual delivery. A dedicated slot is the row for heavy use. It is not the shared login, and it is not the Flow 20x plan.

Those three paragraphs are the catalog. They are not a guess at how many finished clips a credit produces. Google's own credit table, below, is also not a video counter. A credit, a Flow generation and a finished export are different units. Open the estimator inside the product before you render, and stop if the estimate is larger than the budget you planned.

Pick the row by the job:

  1. You want to see whether Ultra video is useful at all. Start with the shared month at Rs 2,500. Export anything you need to keep.
  2. You already know you need the Flow Ultra 20x plan, with Veo 3 called out on the listing. That is the Rs 29,999 row.
  3. You want a dedicated 25k credits slot for heavy use, with the 28-day warranty line. That is the Rs 26,000 row.

Do not buy the Rs 26,000 or Rs 29,999 row to "try a reel". One shared month answers that question. Move up only after you have used the shared plan, or after you already know the access type from a previous job.

The shared listing's word "unlimited" is also not a promise that every model, length and resolution is open with no queue. If a control you need is missing, screenshot the plan page the same day.

Google AI Ultra on that page is the top consumer tier: higher Gemini limits, Flow, Veo, and Antigravity with higher agent access than Pro. Our rows are the access types in the price table, for 1 month each.

Gemini Pro is the writing and Google-apps plan. The price and the lengths are in the Gemini Pro price guide.

Buy the Rs 1,500 year if the work is Docs, Gmail, study notes and ordinary chat. Pro includes some image and video tools. The amount is whatever Google shows on that account. If the real job is generating video every week, Pro is the wrong row. A shared Ultra month at Rs 2,500 is the test. The Rs 26,000 and Rs 29,999 rows are for people who already know they need the slot or the Flow 20x plan.

A student who needs a stronger Gemini model for essays should not pay Ultra prices to get there. The year of Pro on your own Gmail keeps the chats in the account you already use. Ultra shared does not.

How to order and what to check on day one

  1. Open the Google AI Ultra page and choose shared, Flow Ultra 20x, or the 25k slot.
  2. Pay in PKR or USDT. Send the payment screenshot on WhatsApp, and say which row you paid for.
  3. On the shared plan, wait for the login steps. Do not send us your personal Gmail password for a shared login, and do not change the password on the shared account.
  4. On Flow Ultra 20x, access details come on WhatsApp. Follow those steps until Veo 3 and Flow open.
  5. On the 25k slot, delivery is manual. The listing says a dedicated slot.
  6. The delivery policy says most orders are ready within 5–30 minutes after payment is confirmed. Bank transfers can take longer to show than a wallet payment. The Flow and 25k rows say manual or WhatsApp delivery, so use the time in the reply you receive if it is longer than that window.
  7. If the account does not show the plan you paid for, screenshot it the same day. The refund policy explains repair, replacement and a refund when we cannot deliver.

Spend ten minutes on day one so the row you paid for is the row you received.

  • Plan name and access type: shared login, WhatsApp delivery for Flow 20x, or the dedicated slot.
  • End of the month window, and the warranty line: 25 days on the shared listing, 28 days on the 25k listing. Confirm the Flow warranty in the WhatsApp reply.
  • One short generation, so you can see the tool open. Read the credit or queue estimate before a long render.
  • Your own files exported off a shared login before you close the browser.
  • A screenshot of the plan page, saved next to the payment screenshot.

For a client ad, a price, or a face you do not have rights to use, the model is not the rights holder. Generate only material you are allowed to publish. Country limits on YouTube, Home and Health perks follow Google's rules for the account, not the rupee price.
