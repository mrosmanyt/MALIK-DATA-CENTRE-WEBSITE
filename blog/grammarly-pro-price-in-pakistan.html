Tone, plagiarism and the AI-text check

These three features are why people pay, and each one is easy to misunderstand.

Tone. Grammarly can mark a sentence as formal, confident, friendly or direct, and Pro can rewrite it toward the tone you pick. That matters for an email to a supervisor, a client you have never met, or a university admissions office. A fluent sentence can still be the wrong tone. Read the rewrite before you send it. The tool does not know your relationship with the reader.

Plagiarism. The check compares your text with published sources and flags overlap. It is there so you can add a citation you forgot, or rewrite a sentence that sits too close to a paper you read. It is not a certificate that your university's own checker will agree. Universities use their own software and their own thresholds. Run Grammarly first, then follow your department's rules. Our guide to AI tools for university students puts this check next to Gemini and QuillBot and explains where each one belongs.

AI-generated text. Pro includes a detector for text that looks machine-written. Treat the result as a signal, not a verdict. A detector can miss edited AI text and can flag a human sentence. If your department bans undeclared AI writing, the safe path is to write the final answer yourself and to keep notes of what you used the tool for. Grammarly will not hide that choice from a teacher who asks.

Fluency. For writers who think in Urdu and draft in English, the fluency suggestions fix word order and articles. That is the feature most of our buyers notice in the first hour. It does not replace knowing the subject. A grammatically perfect paragraph can still be factually wrong.

How own-email activation works

Own-email activation is the reason to buy Grammarly from a rupee checkout instead of sharing a login.

  1. Open the Grammarly Pro page and choose 1 month or 12 months.
  2. Place the order and pay in PKR or USDT.
  3. Send the payment screenshot on WhatsApp, plus the email address where Grammarly should be activated. Use an email you control. A university alumni address you will lose in June is a bad choice.
  4. We activate Pro on that email and send the setup steps. You sign in at Grammarly's site and install the browser extension or the Word add-in. We do not need your email password. Do not send a password to anyone who asks for it.
  5. Open a real document and accept or reject a few suggestions before you consider the order finished. If Pro does not show on the account, send a screenshot the same day.

Timing for a normal order is the window in our delivery policy: usually a few minutes to half an hour after payment is confirmed. Keep the inbox open, including spam, because Grammarly may send its own confirmation.

Because the plan sits on your email, your documents and your suggestion history stay with you. That is the right setup for a thesis, a client's unpublished copy, or a job application. A shared Grammarly login would mix your drafts with other people's text. We do not sell Grammarly that way.

What to check on the first day

Spend fifteen minutes so you know the account matches the row you paid for.

  • Sign in at grammarly.com with the email you gave us.
  • Open the account page and confirm the plan name is Pro, and the end date matches 1 month or 12 months.
  • Install the browser extension and open a Google Doc or a Word file you wrote yourself.
  • Run one tone change and one full-sentence rewrite. Read both before you keep them.
  • If you are a student, run the plagiarism check on a paragraph you know you cited, and on one you have not cited yet, so you can see what a flag looks like.
  • Screenshot the plan name. If it is Free, or a different product, message us that day.

Grammarly can suggest a rewrite that changes your meaning. You are the author. Keep the sentence that says what you mean, even when the tool prefers a smoother one.
