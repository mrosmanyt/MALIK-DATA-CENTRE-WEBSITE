What you get on your own email

"Activated on your email" means the Pro plan is applied to an address you control. Your existing Picsart projects stay in that login. You are not sharing a password with other buyers, and you should not be asked for the password of that inbox. We send the activation steps on WhatsApp. You sign in yourself.

The tool page says the same Pro account works in the Picsart mobile app and on the web. Install the app, sign in with the email that was activated, and confirm the Pro mark before you start a client file. If the phone shows the free editor and the browser shows Pro, you are in two different accounts. Sign out and use the email on the order.

The listing's "no ads or watermarks" line is the Pro claim on that row. Export one image on day one. If a watermark is still on the file, screenshot the export and the account page the same day. Do not wait until month two. The refund policy covers a plan that was not the plan you paid for.

Picsart's own site is picsart.com. Their checkout price depends on country, store and whether the app store is billing you. A subscription started inside Google Play or the App Store is their invoice, not this Rs 3,500 row. Do not pay both for the same email in the same week. If Play Store already renewed Pro, cancel that renewal before you ask us to activate the same address, and tell us in the WhatsApp message.

What this plan is not:

  • It is not Canva. Canva is a different login and a different file format.
  • It is not Adobe Photoshop or the Creative Cloud bundle. Those desktop apps are a separate catalog.
  • It is not a shared shop login. The row says your email. Do not hand the password to a second person and then lose the email when they change it.

Picsart or Canva for a phone editor

Canva Pro is Rs 400 for 12 months, also activated on your email, with a listed original price of Rs 3,000. That price is hard to ignore. The tools are still different, and buying both by habit is how a student spends twice.

Use Picsart when the work already happens inside Picsart: retouching a portrait, removing a background on a product shot you took on the phone, stickers and filters, a story you will post from the same app. The 11-month term at Rs 3,500 is the plan for someone who will open that app most weeks.

Use Canva when the work is a template: a menu, a price list, a poster with the shop name, a thumbnail with several text boxes. Canva's year is Rs 400 because it is Canva, not because Picsart is "overpriced." If you only need templates, pay Rs 400 and leave Picsart on the free app.

A simple split that matches how shops actually work:

  1. Product photo from the phone camera, background messy, needs a cleanup. Picsart.
  2. Same photo placed into a price poster with the shop logo and three lines of Urdu or English text. Canva.
  3. You do step 1 every day and step 2 once a week. Picsart is the main plan, and Canva at Rs 400 is still cheap enough to add if the posters are slow.
  4. You do step 2 every day and almost never retouch. Skip Picsart.

Students who need slides and a brand kit should start with Canva. The longer Picsart term makes sense when the phone is the studio. Neither plan writes your assignment. For grammar and paraphrasing, those are other tools, and they are not included in this Rs 3,500.

How to order and what to check on day one

  2. Pay in PKR, or in USDT if you are outside Pakistan. Send the screenshot.
  3. Do not send your email password or a one-time code. Activation on your email does not need the password handed to a stranger.
  4. Most orders are ready within 5–30 minutes after payment is confirmed, as the delivery policy describes. A bank transfer can take longer to appear than JazzCash or EasyPaisa.
  5. Sign in on the phone and on the web. Check the term, the Pro mark, and one export without a watermark.

Keep three things with the payment screenshot: the email address, the end date, and one exported image. If Pro does not show, send those the same day. Waiting a week makes the warranty conversation harder, because the account page may have changed and you will not remember which email you typed.

If you already have a Picsart login full of projects, say so before you pay. The point of an on-your-email plan is that those projects stay put. A ready-made login you have never seen is a different product, and we do not list one for Picsart.
