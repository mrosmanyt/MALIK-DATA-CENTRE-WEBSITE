What Google lists as AI Pro

Google's AI plans page is the public description. The comparison table on that page lists Google AI Pro at $19.99 a month (official). The same table lists Google AI Plus at $4.99 a month (official), Google AI Ultra 5x at $99.99 a month (official), and Google AI Ultra 20x at $199.99 a month (official). Those are Google's prices. Ours are the rupee rows in this article.

The Pro section of that page describes:

  • Expanded access to Gemini's stronger models and to Deep Research.
  • Gemini inside Gmail, Docs and Sheets.
  • 5 TB of cloud storage across Gmail, Drive and Photos.
  • YouTube Premium Lite where that perk is offered. Google says availability varies by country.
  • Other add-ons, including Home and Health perks, that are also limited by country and device.

A promotional 12-month activation on your Gmail is not the same invoice as $19.99 a month. After we activate, open the Google account page and read the plan name and the end date. Storage size and YouTube perks follow Google's rules for the country of the account. Do not assume every line written for a US subscriber appears on a Pakistan Gmail. If a perk you care about is missing, screenshot the plan page and message us. We can tell you whether that perk is part of the row you bought.

Google also sells AI Plus below Pro, and Ultra above it. Plus is a smaller plan. Ultra is the top tier. Our store's Gemini Plus rows are a third thing again: activation links, not Pro on your Gmail. The 12-month Gemini Plus link is Rs 600. The 6-month Gemini Plus link is Rs 650. The tool page says those links are not Gemini Pro. If you want Pro on your own email, buy the Rs 1,500 year or the Rs 3,350 two-year bundle. If someone offers "Gemini" without saying Pro or Plus, ask which row it is before you pay.

Who the 1-year plan is for

The Rs 1,500 year fits people who write and research inside Google.

  • A student drafting essays in Docs, with Gemini for an outline, a counterargument and a cleanup pass. The final sentences should still be yours. Departments differ on what they allow. Our guide for university students puts Gemini next to Grammarly and QuillBot and says where each one stops.
  • A freelancer who answers clients in Gmail and keeps briefs in Drive. Pro on your own email means the thread stays in your inbox.
  • Anyone who wants a longer Gemini plan than a single month and does not need heavy video.

The 2-year bundle at Rs 3,350 fits the same person if they already know they will use Gemini past the first year, and they also want one month of YouTube Premium on that email. It is a weak fit if you only wanted YouTube. A month of YouTube on its own is a different product. Do not buy two years of Gemini to get thirty days of ad-free video.

Use the year as a writing tool with a habit, not as a search box you open once.

  1. Put the source notes in a Doc first. Gemini is more useful on your material than on a one-line prompt.
  2. Ask for an outline, then for the holes in the outline. Fill the holes yourself.
  3. Ask for a clearer paragraph only after you have written the messy one.
  4. Check names, dates and numbers. A fluent paragraph can still be wrong.
  5. If the work is an assignment, follow the written rules for your course.

That is the ordinary use the Rs 1,500 plan is priced for.

Gemini Pro includes some image and video tools, and the amount is not a number we are going to invent. If your real job is generating video — ads, reels, Veo, Flow — Pro is the wrong row. Buy Google AI Ultra.

The shared listing describes unlimited video generation, Antigravity, and other Ultra features, with a 25-day warranty line. "Unlimited" is the word on that listing. Google's own Ultra prices on the plans page are $99.99 a month (official) for Ultra 5x and $199.99 a month (official) for Ultra 20x. Our rows are the access types above, not a copy of that dollar checkout.

The side-by-side is on Gemini Pro vs Google AI Ultra. Start on Pro if you write. Move to Ultra if you are generating video every week. A shared Ultra month at Rs 2,500 is the test for video. The Rs 26,000 and Rs 29,999 rows are for people who already know they need the slot or the Flow 20x plan.

What to look at on day one

Spend ten minutes so the row you paid for is the row you received.

  • Plan name on the Google account: Pro, for the length you bought.
  • End date: about 12 months, or about 2 years on the bundle.
  • Gemini opens inside a Doc and inside Gmail, not only on gemini.google.com.
  • A short prompt on a document you wrote, so you can see the model answer.
  • Storage if you bought Pro expecting more than the free 15 GB. Read the number Google shows. Country and plan rules apply.
  • On the bundle only: YouTube Premium visible, with a 1-month horizon, not a 2-year horizon.

Keep the screenshot of the plan page with your payment screenshot. If something drops later in the warranty window, that pair of images is what support needs. For a price, a law, a citation or a medical sentence, open the source. Gemini drafts and searches your files. It is not the source.
