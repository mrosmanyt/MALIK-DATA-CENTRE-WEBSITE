On your email, or admin access

On your email. You keep the address. Documents in that OneDrive stay in an account you can open after we have finished the setup. We should not ask for the password of that inbox. You sign in on Windows, Mac, Android or iPhone with the account that was activated. The "up to 5 devices" line is the listing. Sign in on the machines you actually use, and sign out of a shared lab PC when you leave.

Admin access. The Rs 9,150 row is for someone who needs the admin side of a Microsoft 365 setup for a year. The catalog says it is separate from the email activation plan, with a full warranty and setup help on WhatsApp. Ask, before you pay, what you will be able to open: an admin centre, seats you can assign, or something else. If the answer is not the thing you need, do not pay the lower price to "get Office cheaper."

We are an independent store. We are not Microsoft. Microsoft's own plans and country prices are on microsoft.com/microsoft-365. Those checkouts change with region, tax and whether you are a home user or a business. Our prices are the two rupee rows. A card subscription you start on Microsoft's site is their invoice. Do not run both on the same email unless you know they are meant to stack, which they usually are not.

What we do not sell, so this page will not price it:

  • A Word-only key.
  • A Microsoft 365 Personal or Family card with a made-up rupee amount.
  • A student plan at a student rate.
  • A one-month Office rental.

If you need one of those, Microsoft's site is the place that lists them. This store lists the 12-month email activation and the 12-month admin plan.

What to install on day one

  1. Confirm which row you paid for. The WhatsApp message should say "email activation" or "admin," not only "Office."
  2. On the email plan, sign in at office.com and then in the desktop apps. Install Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook. Open a blank file in each. A web-only login is not proof that the desktop apps activated.
  3. Check OneDrive. The listing says up to 1TB. If the account shows a much smaller quota, screenshot the storage page the same day.
  4. Sign in on the phone if you need Outlook or the mobile apps. The tool page says the account works on Windows, Mac, Android and iPhone.
  5. Count devices. The spec says up to 5. A sixth PC that knocks an older laptop out of the list is a Microsoft device limit, not a missing payment.

Windows itself is a different product. A PC that still says "Activate Windows" needs a Windows 10/11 Pro key, which is Rs 2,000 for a lifetime retail key, with a listed original price of Rs 6,000. Office does not activate Windows. Windows does not include Word. People mix those up because both logos are on the same laptop.

The delivery policy says most orders are ready within 5–30 minutes after payment is confirmed. Bank transfers can take longer to show than a wallet payment. If the apps still ask you to buy a subscription after you have signed in with the right account, screenshot the account page and the app. The refund policy explains repair, replacement and a refund when we cannot deliver the row you paid for.

Keep the email address, the end date, and a screenshot of the subscription page next to the payment proof. Twelve months is long enough to forget which address was used. Write it down on day one.

Who should pay Rs 29,900

Pay for the email plan if the files are yours and they need to stay yours.

  • An office or an accountant who already lives in Excel workbooks and cannot move those files into a random shared login.
  • A teacher who assigns work in Word and collects it from the same Microsoft account the class already knows.
  • A student whose university accepts Office files and who will still need the apps after this semester. Eleven months from now, the alternative is another purchase. That is still one year, not a lifetime.

Do not pay Rs 29,900 if you only need to read a file someone sent. Word and Excel on the web, and the free viewers, open a lot of documents. Do not pay it if the real job is a logo, a menu or a social post. Canva Pro is Rs 400 for 12 months on your email and is the design tool, not a spreadsheet.

Do not pay the admin price as a shortcut to the email plan. If you run a small company and you truly need admin access, say that in the first WhatsApp message and wait for the description of what will be delivered. The warranty line on that row is full warranty for the year. The email plan's device and storage lines are the ones in the table above.
