Editor or Creator

Pick from the job, not from the smaller number.

Editor at Rs 1,400 is the lower plan. The tool page says Editor is the lower plan and Creator has higher limits. Buy Editor when you are testing the tool, or when the work is light enough that you do not need the higher cap, and when the end date still covers the test. You are not buying a shorter calendar just because the price is lower. You are buying the lower feature row. If the end date is the same on both and you already know you will hit a cap, the Rs 700 gap is the cost of the higher row, not a fee for the same limits.

Creator at Rs 2,100 is the row when the listing's "higher limits than Editor" is the reason you are paying. Ask what those limits are before you pay if the job depends on a number. "Higher" without a number is not a quote you can plan a client week around. If the chat cannot name the limit, buy Editor, use it, and move up only if the screen shows you ran out.

Neither row says "activated on your email". Both say ready-made account. Do not change the email or the password until the chat says the account is yours to edit.
