Teams, students and agencies look up the Notion Business price when notes, a wiki and a light project board need to live in one workspace. What is the Notion Business price in Pakistan?

Both rows are on our Notion Business page. Both are a workspace activation.

The 12-month listing shows an original price of Rs 15,000. The 3-month price and original price are both Rs 1,250, so that row has no separate discount figure. The 12-month specs include a 1-year warranty. The 3-month specs say the warranty covers the plan period, with setup help on WhatsApp.

Four times the 3-month price is Rs 5,000, and the year is Rs 4,600, so the year is the cheaper way to stay for 12 months if you already know the workspace will still be in use in month four. The 3-month row is the right size for a semester project, a client retainer that ends, or a trial of Business before you commit the year.

What the listing includes: workspace, history and AI

The 12-month description says the plan unlocks collaborative workspaces, infinite page history, advanced block permissions, and Notion AI features. The specs name collaborative workspace blocks, infinite page edits history, advanced export and print, and the 1-year warranty. That is the sentence on our catalog. It is the sentence to test on day one.

The 3-month description is shorter. It says Notion Business for 3 months, activated on a workspace, and that it is the shorter plan. The specs say Notion Business features, the 3-month activation, and warranty for the plan period. They do not repeat the word "infinite" or spell out a separate AI line. Treat the 3-month row as Business for a quarter. After activation, read the plan name and the history control in the workspace. If you needed the longer history line in writing, the 12-month listing is the one that states it.

What you can do with that, in ordinary team use:

  • Keep meeting notes, a wiki and a task database in one workspace instead of a shared drive full of files.
  • Give teammates a page without emailing a document back and forth.
  • Use the AI features the 12-month listing names, for a draft, a summary of a page you wrote, or a cleanup pass. The final wording is still yours. Notion is not a source for a price, a law, or a medical sentence.
  • Export or print when the specs' export line matters, for a handover at the end of a project.

What the catalog does not say, and what we will not invent:

  • It does not print a member count. Notion's own billing is per member. Our price is the workspace activation in the table. Before you add a class or a ten-person agency, ask on WhatsApp how many members that activation covers and whether we upgrade a workspace you already own or set up another one.
  • It does not print an AI credit allowance. Do not assume unlimited Agent runs, meeting transcription for every call, or a pile of add-on credits.
  • It does not include a slide designer. A pitch deck is a different tool. That split is below.

What Notion lists for Business

Notion's pricing page is the public card. When we checked it, Business was listed at $20 per member a month (official). Plus was listed at $10 per member a month (official). Free was $0. The FAQ on that page says a paid plan is charged per member, and that guests are free but can only open the pages they are invited to. They do not see the whole teamspace. The page can show a monthly or yearly billing choice, so read the billing period on the card before you compare the dollar figure with our rupee plan. Checkout can also add tax.

Business, on that page, adds these on top of Plus:

  • Notion Agent
  • AI Meeting Notes
  • Enterprise Search, marked beta
  • SAML SSO
  • Granular database permissions
  • The option to verify a page
  • Private teamspaces
  • Domain verification
  • Premium connections

Plus includes a trial of Notion AI, custom forms, sites, charts and collaborative blocks. The trial is not the same thing as Business. A free student Plus seat on Notion's education offer, for one member with a school email, is also Notion's own checkout. It is not our Business activation.

Page history on the comparison table we read is 7 days on Free, 30 days on Plus, and 90 days on Business. Our 12-month listing says infinite page edits history. Those two lines are not the same sentence. After we activate, open a page's history and read the limit the workspace actually shows. If the workspace shows 90 days, that is the limit Notion is applying on that account. Screenshot it and message us if you ordered the 12-month row because of the infinite-history line and the product shows something shorter. We would rather correct the order than leave you with a surprise in month two.

Notion's pricing page also lists Custom Agents as free to try, then $10 per 1,000 monthly Notion credits (official). That is extra usage on Notion's side. It is not folded into our Rs 4,600. If your team wants agents running on a schedule, read that credit price before you plan the workflow. Meeting notes and Enterprise Search follow the same rule: they appear on Notion's Business list, and the account you receive has to show them. Country, beta labels and workspace settings can hide a feature. Check the feature on day one if it is the reason you bought Business rather than a notes doc.

Notion for notes, Gamma for a deck

Notion holds the pages you keep editing: the client wiki, the syllabus, the operating notes. Gamma makes the presentation. Mixing them up is how people buy a year of the wrong tool.

Gamma Pro on our store is Rs 2,400 for 1 month or Rs 9,500 for 12 months, both on a private account. The yearly Gamma listing shows an original price of Rs 24,000. Both include PDF and PowerPoint export. The details are on the Gamma Pro price guide and the Gamma Pro page.

Use Notion when the value is the page six weeks later, with comments and a database. Use Gamma when the value is a deck you will export and present. A founder who needs both can buy the 3-month Notion workspace and one month of Gamma, then extend the one they actually open. Buying the Notion year because a conference needs slides is the expensive version of the wrong choice.

Students organising a thesis can live in the 3-month workspace if the deadline is inside the quarter. A lab or a society that will still share notes next year is the Rs 4,600 plan, after you have confirmed the member count. An agency tracking several clients should keep one workspace and separate pages, and should ask us about members before inviting every contractor as a full member. Guests, on Notion's own FAQ, are the cheaper seat for someone who only needs one page.

How workspace activation works

  1. Open the Notion Business page and choose 3 months or 12 months.
  2. Message us on WhatsApp with the member count you need, and say whether the pages already exist in a workspace.
  3. Pay in PKR or USDT only after that reply tells you the activation matches the workspace you want.
  4. We activate the workspace. You accept the access from the inbox of an email you control. Do not send the password for that email.
  5. The delivery policy says most orders are ready within 5–30 minutes after payment is confirmed. Bank transfers can take longer to show than a wallet payment.
  6. Open Settings, read the plan name, and open page history on a test page. On the 12-month row, also open the AI control once and write a sentence in your own words for it to revise.
  7. If the plan name is still Free or Plus, screenshot it the same day. The refund policy explains repair, replacement and a refund when we cannot deliver.

Day-one checks:

  • Plan name says Business, for 3 months or 12 months, matching the order.
  • A second person can open a page you shared, if you bought this for a team.
  • Page history opens, and you have written down the length it shows.
  • Export works on a test page if the handover matters.
  • AI, on the 12-month order, responds inside a page you wrote. If it does not, send the screenshot before you build the whole wiki on top of a missing feature.
  • The payment screenshot and the plan screenshot are in the same chat.

Keep client pages in a workspace you control. A ready-made login posted into a group chat is a shared inbox. Our activation is for the workspace on the order, and the email that receives it should be one your team still has next month.
