Team invite vs private activation

The Rs 650 team invite and the Rs 1,250 private activation are both one month of CapCut Pro, and they are not the same product. The team invite is cheaper because you join a shared Pro team; it is the right buy for trying Pro, for a short project, or for a second account. The private activation costs more because Pro is activated on your own account, with no team in the middle — the right buy if CapCut is your daily editor and you want the account to be entirely yours. The 3-month and 6-month rows are team invites, so they suit editors who are happy on a team and want to pay once and forget about renewing. If you are editing client work every day, the private row is the honest choice; if you are a student or a seller posting a few Reels a week, the team invite is the honest choice.

Mistakes to avoid

First, buying the 6-month row before you have tried Pro for a month — Rs 650 teaches you everything you need to know about whether Pro fits your workflow. Second, paying before confirming the plan and price on WhatsApp — the catalog price and the price on the day can differ, and the confirmation message is your record. Third, giving the wrong email for a team invite — double-check the email linked to your CapCut account before you send it, because the invite goes there. Fourth, ordering from a seller who asks for your CapCut password or an OTP — that is the single clearest sign of a scam in this market. Fifth, forgetting to test Pro on day one: open the app, check the Pro badge, and try one Pro feature before the week gets busy.
