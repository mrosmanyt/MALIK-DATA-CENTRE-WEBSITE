ChatGPT Go is for one person. ChatGPT Plus is for one heavy user. ChatGPT Business is the plan for a team — a small agency, a tuition centre, a shop with staff, or a school office that needs more than one person on the same workspace. This guide explains the ChatGPT Business price in Pakistan, the two ways to buy it, and how to tell whether your team actually needs it.

Ready-made account vs dedicated slot: what is the difference?

The two options sound similar and are not. A ready-made account at Rs 4,000 is a login prepared for you: we hand over an account with the Business plan already on it, and your team signs in with it. It is sold with a 1-day warranty, and it is the cheaper way in — right for a team that wants to try Business for a month and see whether it changes their work.

A dedicated slot at Rs 5,150 is your own seat on a Business workspace, rather than a shared login. The difference is separation: your team's work sits in its own slot, and you are not sharing the login with anyone else's team. For anything with client data, student records or business conversations, the dedicated slot is the right buy, and the Rs 1,150 difference is the price of that separation. If you are unsure which one you need, the question to ask is simple: would it matter if someone else could see what is in this account? If yes, buy the slot.

What Business gives a team

Business is OpenAI's team plan, which means it is built for more than one person working together: a shared workspace instead of three separate logins, and a plan your whole team uses under one arrangement. For a small team in Pakistan the practical difference from Go or Plus is not a feature list — it is that the plan is bought once for the team, managed in one place, and paid for once in rupees on WhatsApp instead of three people paying separately in dollars.

That only matters if the team actually shares work. A tuition centre where three teachers draft lessons, answer parent messages and prepare tests from the same account is a Business buyer. Three friends who each use ChatGPT alone are not — they are three Go buyers, and Go at Rs 2,200 for 6 months each costs less per person.

Who should buy Business?

  • Small agencies: a two- or three-person team drafting proposals, captions and client replies from one workspace.
  • Tuition centres and schools: teachers sharing lesson plans and parent messages, where one account keeps the work in one place.
  • Shops with staff: a shop where two people answer customers and write product descriptions, and the owner wants one bill, not two.
  • Freelancer teams: a small team delivering client work together, where a shared workspace beats forwarding screenshots between personal accounts.

If that is not you — if you work alone — buy ChatGPT Go and spend the difference on another tool. Our freelancers roundup shows what pairs well with it.
