Claude Pro vs Claude API: the honest split

Pro and the API are two different products with the same name on the box, and most of the confusion in this market comes from mixing them up.

Pro is a seat in the chat app. You open claude.ai, you talk to Claude, you get higher usage limits than the free tier. The API is raw access: you buy tokens or credits, your code calls Claude, and you pay for what you use. A chat login cannot run your app, and an API key cannot give you the Pro chat experience. Buying the wrong one is the most common waste of money in this category, and the full explainer is in our guide to Claude API keys and AI subscriptions.

Who should buy Claude Pro

Pro fits people who live inside the Claude chat window.

  • A writer or researcher drafting long documents, where Claude's long-form writing and careful editing are the point. Pro's higher limits mean you stop hitting the free-tier wall mid-draft.
  • Anyone using Claude as a daily thinking partner for analysis, planning and rewriting, rather than a one-off question box.
  • A student or freelancer who has already tried the free tier, hit its limits, and knows the paid chat is the bottleneck — not tokens, not an app.

If you have never used Claude, start on the free tier first. Pro is a monthly bill, and the free tier tells you in a week whether Claude's writing style is actually the one you want. Our guide for freelancers puts Claude next to the other chat tools so you can see where it earns its place.

Who should buy Claude API tokens instead

If you write code, buy tokens, not Pro. The Claude API tokens page lists token packs, credit packs and Kiro packs for developers calling Claude from their own apps. Every pack is price on request: message WhatsApp and we confirm the amount before you pay, and no rupee price is listed on the page. That is deliberate. Token pricing moves with the pack size and the duration, and a printed number would be wrong within days.

API tokens fit a developer shipping a feature, a team wiring Claude into an internal tool, or anyone whose usage is measured in calls rather than chats. If that is you, do not buy a chat subscription and try to stretch it into an API. It will not work, and the money is gone. Message us with the pack size you need — 10M, 50M, 100M, 200M, 500M tokens, 1B credits, or the $50, $100 and $200 credit packs — and we confirm the price first.

How to pay for Claude from Pakistan

There are three real routes, and they solve different problems.

  1. Apple App Store. Subscribe inside the Claude iOS app and pay Rs 4,900 a month through Apple. This is the simplest route if you have an iPhone and an Apple payment method that works in Pakistan. You manage it in your Apple subscriptions, not on Anthropic's site.
  2. Anthropic's website. Pay $20 a month with a card that supports international USD billing. Most Pakistani debit cards fail here, and the bank adds markup and tax on top of the dollar price. This is the route that produces the most failed payments, and it is covered in our guide to buying AI tools in Pakistan without a credit card.

Whichever route you take, the rule is the same: know which product you are buying — Pro chat or API tokens — before money moves.

What to check before you pay

  • Are you buying the Pro chat subscription, or API tokens? Say it in one sentence to the seller and get the same sentence back.
  • What is the delivery method, and what exactly shows on your account when it is done? Pro should show as a paid plan on your Claude account.
  • What is the warranty if the plan drops? The refund policy explains repair, replacement and a refund when we cannot deliver.
  • Never send your Claude or Google password to a seller. A chat subscription does not need it, and an API key is delivered as a key, not a login.
