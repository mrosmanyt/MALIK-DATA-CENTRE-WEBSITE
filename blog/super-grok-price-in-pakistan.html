Premium, Bot, and Heavy

The Rs 3,500 row is one month of Grok Premium on X. The listing describes the Grok assistant with real-time X search, the blue verification badge, and an ad-free X experience. The specs repeat those three, and they add a 25-day warranty. This is the plan most people mean when they ask for SuperGrok or X Premium+ on a single account.

The Rs 4,999 row is Grok Bot plus Grok Premium for a monthly plan, also on your account. The specs name Grok AI bot access, Grok Premium features, and real-time X search. The catalog does not print a separate warranty day count on this row. Ask on WhatsApp what the warranty line is before you pay, and save the reply.

The Rs 19,999 row is Grok Heavy for 1 month. The listing calls it xAI's most powerful Grok plan on our store, for heavy research, coding and analysis, with the highest Grok usage limits we list. The specs name Heavy model access, those higher limits, and real-time X search. It does not print a warranty day count. Confirm that line on WhatsApp with the order.

Use the rows this way:

  • Everyday Grok, the badge and a quieter timeline: Grok Premium at Rs 3,500.
  • You specifically want the bot row as well as Premium: Rs 4,999.
  • Heavy research or the highest limits we sell under the Grok name: Rs 19,999.

A month of Premium is enough to learn whether you open Grok every day. Heavy is a poor first purchase if you have never used Grok. The gap between Rs 3,500 and Rs 19,999 is the usage tier, not a longer calendar. Both Premium and Heavy are 1-month listings.

Grok is tied to X. ChatGPT Go is a separate assistant with no X badge and no timeline. The lengths and prices are on the ChatGPT Go price guide and the ChatGPT Go page.

The 6-month listing shows an original price of Rs 5,000. The 3-month price and original price are both Rs 2,900.

Buy Grok Premium if you post on X, you want search over the live timeline, or you want the badge and the ad-free line on that account. Buy ChatGPT Go if the work is essays, client drafts and ordinary chat, and you do not care about X. Buying both in the same week is reasonable only if those are two different jobs. A student who never opens X does not get more writing done by paying for a badge.

Grok Heavy is still a Grok plan. It is the high-limit row, not a second ChatGPT. If the work is a codebase on your laptop, the editor plans are a different family. This post does not price those.

How activation works on your account

  1. Open the SuperGrok page and choose Premium, Bot plus Premium, or Heavy.
  2. Pay in PKR or USDT. Send the payment screenshot and the X username on WhatsApp.
  3. We activate on that account. Follow the setup steps we send. Do not send the email password.
  4. The delivery policy says most orders are ready within 5–30 minutes after payment is confirmed. Bank transfers can take longer to show than a wallet payment.
  5. Sign in, open Grok, and run one search that needs the live timeline. Open the X profile and look for the badge. If it is not there yet, check again after X's review window rather than placing a second order.
  6. On the Bot row, open the bot once so you know that half of the order is present.
  7. If the account still shows the free plan, screenshot it the same day. The refund policy explains repair, replacement and a refund when we cannot deliver.

Day-one checks:

  • Plan name on the account matches Premium, the Bot plus Premium order, or Heavy.
  • Grok answers, and a question about a live X post uses the timeline rather than a stale guess.
  • The profile is complete enough for X's badge review: a name, a photo, and a normal bio.
  • You are signed in on your own phone. A shared computer left logged in leaves the account open to the next person.
  • The screenshot of the plan page sits with the payment screenshot.

The 25-day warranty line is on the Grok Premium listing. For Bot and Heavy, use the warranty sentence we confirm on WhatsApp. A badge that is still in review is not the same thing as a missing subscription. Send both screenshots if the plan itself is missing.
