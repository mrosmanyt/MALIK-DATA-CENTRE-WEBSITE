LinkedIn Premium Price in Pakistan: Which Plan to Buy

Sales Navigator is Rs 1,800 for 2 months, and Advanced is Rs 5,200 for 1 month. Official Career includes 5 InMail credits a month, Business 15, and Sales Navigator 50. Buy Career for a job search. LinkedIn Premium is either a serious job-search tool or an expensive blue badge you never open. The difference is the plan name. Career, Business and Sales Navigator are not three lengths of the same product. This is the LinkedIn Premium price in Pakistan, what each of our vouchers maps to, and how to use the one you buy. It is for graduates, freelancers and anyone doing B2B outreach from Pakistan.

What LinkedIn itself includes

LinkedIn's help article on the difference between free and Premium is the table that matters. InMail credits per month on that chart are:

  • Premium Career: 5
  • Premium Business: 15
  • Sales Navigator Core: 50
  • Sales Navigator Advanced: 50

Career is the job-seeker plan. LinkedIn's Premium Career page describes applicant insights, who viewed your profile, private browsing, profile help, and LinkedIn Learning. The help chart says who-viewed-your-profile insights on Career go back 365 days.

Business adds company insights and a custom profile button, with more InMail. It is aimed at people selling or representing a small business, not at someone applying to graduate roles.

Sales Navigator is a different product with its own interface. LinkedIn's Sales Navigator account types page says Core is the single-user plan: advanced lead and account search, saved leads, custom lists, alerts and InMail. Advanced adds team tools Core does not have: shared lists, Buyer Intent, Smart Links, and CSV upload of a book of business. Advanced Plus is the CRM version. We do not list Advanced Plus. If you need Salesforce sync, buy that from LinkedIn.

LinkedIn Learning comes with Career, Business and Sales Navigator on that comparison chart. It does not, by itself, make you a stronger candidate. You still have to finish the courses. If you want a year of certificates rather than a short Premium window, our Coursera Plus plan is Rs 3,200 for 12 months on a private activation. The two products overlap on "I should learn a skill" and do not overlap on "I need InMail".

Do not buy Sales Navigator for a job search. The interface is for sellers, and the extra search filters will not write your CV. Do not buy Career if you send twenty cold messages a day. Five InMail credits are gone on Monday.

Premium does not guarantee interviews. LinkedIn's own pages market higher reply rates for InMail than for a cold email. That is a comparison with email, not a promise that a recruiter will answer. A precise message to the right person beats five vague ones.

How to use Career for a job search in Pakistan

Buy the month only after the profile is ready. Premium will not fix an empty headline.

  1. Write a headline that says the role you want, not "seeking opportunities". Open to work can be visible to recruiters only.
  2. Put two or three projects in Featured, with a real outcome in the first line.
  3. Turn on Career, then use applicant insights on roles you are actually qualified for. If you are a weak match, fix the CV or skip the role. Spraying Easy Apply is how people burn a week.
  4. Spend InMail on hiring managers you cannot reach any other way. Five credits means five careful notes, not five templates.
  5. Read who viewed your profile once a day, not once an hour. Reply to relevant viewers while the visit is recent.
  6. Run every InMail through Grammarly Pro first. Our Grammarly plan is Rs 1,400 for 1 month or Rs 3,500 for 12 months, on your own email. Tone and clarity matter more on a short InMail than on a long post.

If English is the thing holding the message back, our guide for freelancers on Fiverr and Upwork shows where Grammarly sits next to the rest of a client-hunting stack. LinkedIn is the same muscle: a clear offer, sent to a named person.

How to use Sales Navigator without wasting it

Core is a search tool. Treat it like one.

  • Build a lead list with filters you could explain out loud: country, industry, headcount, title. "Marketing" alone is not a search.
  • Save accounts you can actually serve from Pakistan. A 10,000-lead cap does not mean you should save 10,000 leads.
  • Write one observation from their profile in the first two lines. The InMail credit is the expensive part. The research is the part that gets the reply.
  • Advanced is worth Rs 5,200 only if more than one person will share lists or you need Buyer Intent. A solo freelancer rarely needs it for a single month.

When the voucher ends, export anything you are allowed to keep and do not leave the only copy of a client list inside a seat you no longer have.

What we need from you, and what we never need

Voucher and upgrade plans are redeemed on the profile you already use. We need the email address on that LinkedIn account, and sometimes a screenshot of the checkout step LinkedIn shows. We do not need your password. Anyone asking for the password is a reason to stop, which is the same rule as in how to spot a fake subscription seller.

Order path:

  1. Pick the row on the LinkedIn page and send us the exact plan name.
  2. Pay in PKR or USDT. Keep the screenshot.
  3. Redeem only the steps we send. Do not enter the code on a lookalike page.
  4. Open your LinkedIn settings and confirm the product name and the end date.
  5. If the wrong tier is active, screenshot it the same day and message us. The refund policy covers a plan we cannot deliver or cannot correct. The delivery policy covers timing.

LinkedIn can ask the account owner to confirm a purchase. That prompt is normal. Read it. Do not approve a plan name you did not order.

FAQ

What is the LinkedIn Premium price in Pakistan?

Career is Rs 1,800 for 3 months. Career Duo is Rs 3,200 for 2 months and Business Duo is Rs 3,700 for 2 months. Sales Navigator is Rs 1,800 for 2 months. Sales Navigator Advanced is Rs 5,200 for 1 month. All of them go on your own profile.

Premium interviews ka promise nahi karta. Career se pata chalta hai kaun profile dekhta hai, aur 5 InMail milte hain. Profile khali ho to pehle profile theek karein.

Career or Sales Navigator?

Do I get LinkedIn Learning?

LinkedIn's comparison chart includes LinkedIn Learning with Career, Business and Sales Navigator. For a full year of other courses, Coursera Plus on our store is Rs 3,200 for 12 months.

Will you ask for my LinkedIn password?

No. Vouchers and email activations need the email on the account, not the password. Do not send the password to us or to anyone else who offers the same plan.
