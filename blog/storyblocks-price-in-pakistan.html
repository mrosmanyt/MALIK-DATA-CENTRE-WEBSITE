Video editors, YouTubers and designers in Pakistan all hit the same wall: good stock footage, music and templates cost money, and paying for them in dollars with an international card is a hassle. Storyblocks Unlimited solves the supply side — one subscription, unlimited downloads of stock video, audio, images and templates. MALIK DATA CENTRE sells it at Rs 3,400 for one month on a ready-made account. This guide explains what that price buys, what "ready-made" means, and when Storyblocks beats its main rival, Envato.

The price, first: Rs 3,400 for one month

Storyblocks Unlimited costs Rs 3,400 for a 1-month plan on a ready-made account. One payment, one month, unlimited downloads for the whole month. There is no longer plan in the catalog — if you need it for three months, you buy it three times.

That flat monthly price is the whole point of Storyblocks. Stock sites usually charge per clip, and a single 4K clip can cost more than Rs 3,400 on its own. With the Unlimited plan, the tenth download costs the same as the first: nothing extra. For an editor cutting several videos a week, the month pays for itself after a handful of downloads.

The price is in PKR and payable on WhatsApp through EasyPaisa, JazzCash, NayaPay, UPaisa, Meezan Bank or bank transfer, or USDT from abroad. No international card, no dollar billing, no subscription that renews itself while you forget about it — one month, then you decide whether to buy another.

What "ready-made account" means

A ready-made account means the Storyblocks account is already set up and handed to you, so you can start downloading straight away instead of creating an account and waiting for a payment to clear. You log in, search the library, and download. Delivery is usually within 5 to 30 minutes after payment is confirmed, the same as every other tool in the store.

One thing to understand before you buy: a ready-made account is not the same as a plan activated on your own email. The account is provided for your month of use, so treat it as a working account for downloads, not as a personal account to store a long-term library in. Download what you need during the month and keep the files on your own drive — that is how a monthly stock plan is meant to be used.

What you get with Storyblocks Unlimited

Storyblocks Unlimited covers four kinds of assets in one plan: stock video footage, stock photos, royalty-free music and sound effects, and templates for video editing and motion graphics. The library is large enough that most editors find what they need without going to a second site, and everything downloads with a licence for commercial use — YouTube videos, client work, ads and social media posts included.

For a YouTuber, the practical value is the music and the B-roll: background tracks that will not get a video claimed, and cutaway footage that makes a talking-head video watchable. For a freelancer, it is the templates: lower thirds, transitions and title packs that make a client's video look finished without hours of extra work. For a designer, it is the photos and vectors that fill a presentation or a post.

Unlimited really does mean unlimited inside the month. There is no credit system and no per-download counter — if you need forty clips for a project, you download forty clips. The only limit is the month itself, so heavy users get the best value and light users should think about whether one busy month beats a longer plan elsewhere.

Pick Storyblocks if video is the job: its footage and music library is the stronger of the two, and Rs 3,400 for a month of unlimited video downloads is the better deal when you are cutting video every day. Pick Envato if you want templates, fonts and graphics as well, or if you want a whole year — Rs 8,300 for 12 months is about Rs 692 a month, which is far cheaper per month than Storyblocks if you actually use it all year. Many editors end up using both at different times: Storyblocks for a video-heavy month, Envato for a year of design work.

Who should buy Storyblocks

Buy Storyblocks if you edit video every week — YouTubers, wedding and event editors, agency editors, and freelancers whose clients expect stock footage and licensed music in every delivery. A single busy month justifies the Rs 3,400 easily.

Buy Envato instead if your work is mostly design and templates, or you want a year of access at a lower monthly cost. The Envato price guide has the full breakdown.

Buy neither yet if you only need one or two clips for a single video. Free stock sites cover a one-off need, and Rs 3,400 is only good value when you download in volume.
