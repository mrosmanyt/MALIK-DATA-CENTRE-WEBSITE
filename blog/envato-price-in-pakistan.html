Twelve months at Rs 8,300 is Rs 2,000 times four, plus a bit, against a single month at Rs 2,000. Someone who downloads every week should buy the year once. Someone who needs a template for one wedding film or one client pitch should buy the month, download what the project needs, and stop. A second month bought "just in case" is how a one-off job turns into Rs 4,000.

The licence is at the download

Envato's own help pages, and our tool page, say the commercial licence is tied to the item when you download it. The FAQ on our page says to register the project name at download time for client work. Do that. A file sitting in a Downloads folder with no project name is the file you cannot defend when a client asks for proof six months later.

Practical rules that match that licence line:

  • Download the item while the subscription is active. The row is 1 month or 12 months. When the term ends, do not assume you can keep pulling new files.
  • Put the client or project name in at the download step, as the tool page says. "Client work" is not a project name. "Ahmed wedding titles, November 2026" is.
  • Keep the licence confirmation with the project file. A ready-made account is not your long-term archive. If you cannot sign in next year, the downloaded file on your disk is what you still have.
  • One download is for the project you named. Do not hand the same licensed file to a second client and call it a new job. Download again, for the second project, while the plan is active.
  • Music, fonts and templates follow the same idea. A font you used in a logo still needs the licence trail. A track under a YouTube video needs it too.

Envato's site is elements.envato.com. Their public price depends on country and promotions. Our prices are the two rupee rows. A ready-made account is not the same thing as a subscription on an email you have used for years. Your old Envato downloads do not move across by magic. Say so in the WhatsApp message if you were hoping to extend an account you already own. We will tell you if this row can do that. Do not pay first and ask second.

"Unlimited downloads" is the phrase on the listing. It is not a promise that every item you search for is included, and it is not a count of files in the library. If an item shows as a separate purchase inside Envato, it is not covered by the sentence "unlimited" on our row. Screenshot that item and ask before you build a client job around it.

Envato or Storyblocks

Storyblocks Unlimited is Rs 3,400 for 1 month on a ready-made account. The listing says unlimited downloads of stock video, audio and images, with a royalty-free licence. There is no 12-month Storyblocks row in our catalog.

Choose by the library, not by the cheaper month.

  • You need After Effects templates, fonts, mockups, graphic packs and music in one login. That is the Envato description. The month is Rs 2,000. The year is Rs 8,300.
  • You need B-roll, stock video and audio for a YouTube edit, and you do not need the template-and-font side. Storyblocks at Rs 3,400 for a month can be the closer tool. It costs more than one Envato month because it is a different library, not because it is "more unlimited."
  • You need both in the same month. That is Rs 2,000 plus Rs 3,400. Most one-person channels do not. Pick the hole in the current edit.

CapCut or Premiere still does the cutting. Envato does not edit the video. It supplies the pieces. A channel that only needs a music bed should look at Artlist as well, on its own page, before paying for a full stock subscription.
