Canva Pro vs Adobe Express in Pakistan: Which Design Tool to Buy?

Two design tools, one question, and a price gap of Rs 250 a year. Canva Pro is the tool every Pakistani seller, creator and student already knows: templates for everything, brand kits, and a price — Rs 400 for 12 months on your own email — that is barely a rounding error. Adobe Express is Adobe's answer: quick social posts, flyers and short videos, at Rs 650 for 12 months on a ready-made account. Both have dedicated price posts on this blog, but no head-to-head. This is the comparison that settles the budget-design question small shops actually ask.

Price in Pakistan

Price is the first split, and it is not close. Canva Pro is Rs 400 for 12 months activated on your own email, with a separate admin-panel option at Rs 4,500 for 12 months. Adobe Express is Rs 650 for 12 months on a ready-made account. Both are one-year plans, both are paid once, and both are bought the same way: message MALIK DATA CENTRE on WhatsApp +92 344 5739206 and pay in PKR.

Templates and ease of use

Canva's template library is the reason it wins this comparison for most buyers. Instagram posts, stories, carousels, flyers, banners, CVs, presentations, product listings — there is a template for the exact thing you need, and it is usually good. Adobe Express has strong templates too, with a cleaner, more Adobe-styled look, but the library is smaller and the categories are narrower. If your work is mostly social posts and shop graphics, Canva's library saves you an hour a week. If your work is mostly one polished flyer a month, the difference does not matter.

Ease of use follows the same pattern. Canva is the easiest design tool in the catalog to learn: drag, drop, done, and a first-time user makes a decent post in ten minutes. Express is also easy, but it assumes a little more design sense — it is built for people who already know what they want and want it fast. For a shop owner who is not a designer, Canva is the safer first buy. Our Canva Pro activation guide walks through the setup once you have it.

AI tools and photo editing

Both tools have AI features, and both do background removal, magic eraser-style cleanup and text-to-image. Canva's Magic Studio — background remover, Magic Resize, Magic Write — is the more complete set for a seller: remove a background, resize one design into every platform size, and write the caption in the same place. Express counters with Adobe's photo tools and its Firefly image generation, which produces cleaner, more photographic images than Canva's generator. If your product photos need serious cleanup, Express has the edge. If your job is volume — ten posts a day across three platforms — Canva's Magic Resize is the feature that pays for the year.

Brand kits and teams

Brand kits are where Canva pulls away. One kit per client or per store: colours, fonts and logos saved, and every post on-brand without thinking about it. That is the feature a social media manager or a seller with two stores uses every single day, and it is the reason our social media managers roundup calls Canva Pro the first tool to buy. Express has brand kits too, but they are simpler, and they do not scale across clients the way Canva's do. If you manage one brand, either works. If you manage three, buy Canva.

Video and social posts

Neither tool is a video editor — that job belongs to CapCut Pro, from Rs 650 a month, and our CapCut Reels workflow guide covers it. But both Canva and Express make short social videos, and here Express is the stronger of the two: quicker animations, better effects, and a smoother path from a static post to a moving one. Canva's video tools are fine for a slideshow-style post, but if video is a real part of your marketing, neither of these is the answer — buy CapCut Pro and keep Canva for the stills.

Which one should you buy?

Buy Canva Pro if you are a seller, a creator, a student or a small shop. It is cheaper (Rs 400 against Rs 650), it is activated on your own email, it has the bigger template library, the better brand kits and the easier learning curve, and it is the tool you will open every day. At about Rs 33 a month, it is the best-value design plan in the catalog, and it is the one most of our buyers end up with.

Buy Adobe Express if you already live in Adobe's world — you use Adobe fonts, Adobe Stock, or you work with designers who send you Adobe files. It is Rs 650 for 12 months on a ready-made account, and for that buyer it is the right tool: the Adobe look, the Firefly images, and the quick-video edge. If you need the full Adobe suite instead of just Express, read our Canva Pro vs Adobe Creative Cloud comparison and our Creative Cloud price post — Creative Cloud is a different purchase entirely, from Rs 3,900, and it is not a substitute for this comparison.

And if you genuinely cannot decide, buy Canva Pro first. It is Rs 400 for a year. Use it for a month. If you hit a wall that Express would solve, the Express plan is still Rs 650, and you will have spent less than a thousand rupees finding out which tool fits your hand.

Which is cheaper, Canva Pro or Adobe Express?

Canva Pro — Rs 400 for 12 months on your own email, against Adobe Express at Rs 650 for 12 months on a ready-made account. Canva is also the one that works out at about Rs 33 a month.

Is Canva Pro better than Adobe Express?

For most buyers in Pakistan, yes: more templates, better brand kits, and easier to learn. Adobe Express is the better pick if you already use Adobe tools or need its specific stock and font libraries.

Can I use Canva Pro and Adobe Express together?

Yes. Many sellers design the post in Canva and finish a detail in Express, or the other way round. But at Rs 400 and Rs 650 for a year each, most buyers only need one — start with Canva Pro.

Which is better for Instagram posts?

Canva Pro. Its social templates, Magic Resize and scheduling-friendly sizes are built for Instagram, and the brand kit keeps every post on-brand. Express is fine for a quick post, but Canva is the daily tool.
