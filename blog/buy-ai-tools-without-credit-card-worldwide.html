Most AI tools, from ChatGPT and Gemini to Canva, CapCut and Cursor, are billed in US dollars and expect an international credit card. Millions of people do not have one, or their card is declined for foreign subscriptions. This happens to students in India and Bangladesh, freelancers in the Gulf, and small businesses in Europe alike. This guide explains the routes that work in 2026, wherever you live, and how to avoid losing money.

Why AI tool payments fail

A payment for an AI subscription usually fails for one of these reasons:

  • No card that works online abroad. Many debit cards are not enabled for international online payments, or have a very low limit.
  • Currency conversion and extra fees. When the card does work, the bank adds conversion fees and tax, so you pay more than the price you saw.
  • Strict checks on offers and trials. Discounted offers often reject cards that work fine elsewhere.
  • Regional pricing and availability. Some plans or features are not offered in every country, or cost more in some regions.

If any of these sound familiar, the options below avoid the problem.

Option 1: Pay the company directly in your currency

Some AI companies now bill certain plans in local currencies and accept more payment methods than before. If your card works and the plan you want is offered in your country, paying the company directly is the simplest route.

The limits are that you still need a card the company accepts, and many tools, especially developer and creative tools like Cursor, Lovable, ElevenLabs and Adobe Creative Cloud, still bill mainly in dollars. Long plans, such as 12 or 18 months, are often not available at all.

Option 2: Pay with USDT from any country

USDT is a dollar-based stablecoin. If you have a Binance account (or another exchange that supports it), you can pay in USDT from almost any country, with no credit card and low fees. This is how most of our international customers pay.

At MALIK DATA CENTRE the process is:

  1. Choose a plan on the website and tap Buy Now.
  2. Enter your name and WhatsApp number. Your order number appears on screen.
  3. On WhatsApp, say you want to pay with USDT. We confirm the exact amount and share our official wallet address.
  4. Send the USDT on the network we name (BEP20 or TRC20) and share the transaction screenshot.
  5. We activate the plan on your email or send login details, usually within 5–30 minutes.

Always check the network before you send. Coins sent on the wrong network usually cannot be recovered.

Option 3: Local payment in Pakistan

Customers in Pakistan can skip crypto and pay with EasyPaisa, JazzCash, NayaPay, UPaisa, Meezan Bank or any local bank transfer. Our Pakistan payment guide explains each method step by step.

What to check before you pay any reseller

Cheap prices are only useful if the plan works for its full period. Before you pay any seller, including us, check these points:

  • Access type. Is the plan activated on your own email (private), sent as a family or team invitation, or a shared login? Shared plans cost less but are used by more than one person. Read our guide on whether cheap AI subscriptions are safe.
  • Warranty in writing. Ask what happens if the plan stops working and how long you are covered. Our refund and warranty policy is public.
  • No password requests. A real seller never needs your email password. For email activations, only your email address is needed.
  • Official payment details only. Pay only to the wallet or account shared on the seller's official number, and keep your payment screenshot and order number.
