What the month is for, and what it is not for

Use the month to speed up reading and drafting you are willing to stand behind: a literature list you will open, a paragraph you will edit, a summary you will check against the paper. The tool page's own audience is university students, researchers, thesis writers, and teachers preparing material. A teacher preparing a handout is a different job from a student submitting that handout as their own untouched work.

The month is not for these:

  • Submitting generated text as if you wrote it, when your department requires the writing to be yours. The course rule wins. A tool subscription does not change it.
  • Skipping the source. If the tool names a paper, open the paper. A title in a draft is not a citation until you have seen the page.
