Canva Pro — concept boards

Canva Pro at Rs 400 a year is the cheapest tool in the stack and the one architects use most often: mood boards, material palettes, concept boards for the first client meeting, and social posts for the studio. The brand kit keeps the studio's colours and fonts consistent across every board, and the AI background tools clean up site photos in seconds. Our Canva Pro price post covers the 1-year plan on your own email. For a small studio, this is the first buy — it pays for itself the first time a client says yes to a board.

Adobe Creative Cloud — drawings and renders

Adobe Creative Cloud from Rs 3,900 is the professional core: Photoshop for renders and site photos, Illustrator for drawings and diagrams, InDesign for the portfolio and proposal documents. The 4-month plan at Rs 3,900 is the entry point; longer plans run to Rs 12,000 for 12 months. For an architect the value is not any single app but the set — a render edited in Photoshop, a plan drawn in Illustrator, and a proposal laid out in InDesign all stay in one workflow. Our video editors roundup also uses Adobe tools heavily and shows the same stack from the editing side.

Framer Pro — the studio website

Framer Pro at Rs 6,650 for 12 months is how the studio's website gets built — a portfolio site with project galleries, animations and a CMS the team can update without a developer. Clients judge an architecture studio by its website before they ever visit the office, and a Framer site looks like an agency built it. Our Framer Pro price post covers the 12-month plan in detail. Pair it with Canva: Canva makes the boards, Framer publishes the portfolio.

Ideogram Plus — concept images

Ideogram Plus at Rs 2,400 is the AI image tool that handles text inside images properly — signage, labels, posters, and concept visuals where the words have to be spelled right. For architects that means concept renders with street names, cafe boards, and presentation slides where the text is part of the image. Our Ideogram price post covers the Plus plan on a ready-made account. Use it for early concepts and client mood images, not for final renders — the real drawings still come from your own software.

Gamma Pro — client presentations

Gamma Pro from Rs 2,400 a month turns a prompt into a client-ready deck — the design proposal, the material palette presentation, the project pitch — with PDF and PowerPoint export. An architect who presents to clients weekly will save a full day per deck. Our Gamma Pro price post covers the 1-month and 12-month plans. Pair it with Ideogram: Ideogram makes the concept images, Gamma builds the deck around them.

FAQ

Which AI tool should an architect buy first?

Canva Pro at Rs 400 a year — concept boards, mood boards and client visuals from day one. Then add Ideogram Plus for concept images and Gamma Pro for presentations.

Can AI tools make architectural renders?

AI tools make concept images and mood visuals well. Final renders and construction drawings still come from your own software — AI is for the pitch, not the permit.

Is Adobe Creative Cloud worth it for a small studio?

Yes if you do your own renders, drawings and proposals. The 4-month plan from Rs 3,900 is the cheapest way to test whether the full suite earns its place.

What is the cheapest starter stack for an architect?

Canva Pro Rs 400 a year plus Ideogram Plus Rs 2,400 — about Rs 2,800 to start, covering boards and concept images.

Do these tools replace an architect?

No. They remove the slow visual and presentation work — the design judgement, the structure and the client relationship stay with the architect.

Architect ke liye sab se pehle kaun sa AI tool lein?

Pehle Canva Pro lein — Rs 400 saal ke liye, concept boards aur mood boards ke liye. Phir Ideogram Plus aur Gamma Pro add karein.
