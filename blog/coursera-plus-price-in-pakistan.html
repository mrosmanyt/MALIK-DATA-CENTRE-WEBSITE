What Coursera itself says is included

Coursera's Coursera Plus page is the public description. When we checked it, the page showed an annual price of $239 (official), with $399 (official) beside it. Coursera says that outside the United States the checkout can show a local currency. Treat those dollar figures as Coursera's price, not as our price. Our price is Rs 3,200 for the 12-month private activation.

The same page answers "what is included" in plain language:

Our catalog still describes the plan as 7,000+ courses. Coursera's own page currently says more than 10,000, with exclusions. Both sentences can be true in different ways: our listing is the store description, and Coursera's page is the live catalog, which changes. The badge on the course page is the check that matters for the program you care about. If you are buying this year for one named certificate, send us the course link first. We would rather tell you it is excluded before you pay.

Coursera also says certificates earned in the first 14 days of an annual purchase made on Coursera's own site are revoked if that purchase is refunded inside Coursera's 14-day window. Our warranty is the separate one in our refund policy. Read it before you pay.

What a certificate does

A Coursera certificate is proof that you completed that course or program under Coursera's rules. Coursera's Plus page says you can put it on your LinkedIn profile, add it to a CV, and share it with an employer as evidence of the skills you practised.

Used well, it gives a recruiter a link that is more specific than "familiar with Excel". It can sit beside a degree as a named skill, such as a data certificate under a BCom. Where the program includes a guided project, the file you built is what you talk about in an interview. The certificate is the label. The project is the evidence. People who schedule two modules a week finish. People who only save videos do not.

Our tool page says certificates for completed courses included in Coursera Plus are issued on your profile. Finish the graded work. Paying for the year does not issue the certificate by itself.

What a certificate does not do

Buyers get disappointed when they expect the PDF to do a job it never does.

  • It is not a university degree, and it is not a MasterTrack or a degree program. Coursera says those are outside Plus. If you need a degree, this plan is the wrong product.
  • It does not guarantee a job, a salary, or an internship. Coursera publishes learner-outcome surveys. A survey is not an offer letter. Hiring in Pakistan still depends on your degree, your projects, and how you speak about the work.
  • It does not include every course on the Coursera website. Excluded programs stay excluded no matter how the year was paid for.
  • It does not, by itself, prove you can do the job. If the course had a peer-graded essay and you rushed it, the certificate still downloads. Employers who ask you to demonstrate the skill will notice.
  • It does not replace a licence that a regulator requires. A certificate in a health or finance topic is not a professional licence in Pakistan unless the regulator says so. Check that separately.
  • It does not move onto a different email by magic. If the profile we activate is not the profile you want on your CV, say so before payment.

If your goal is "I want InMail and a look at who viewed my profile", you want LinkedIn, not another course. Our LinkedIn Premium price guide separates Career, Business and Sales Navigator. Our LinkedIn page lists two 3-month Career rows, at Rs 800 and at Rs 1,800, both applied to your own profile, plus other vouchers. LinkedIn Learning comes with those Premium plans. It is a smaller, job-site library, not a year of Coursera certificates. People use Coursera to finish a program and LinkedIn to show the certificate and message a hiring manager. The tools stack. They do not copy each other.

Writing on the CV still has to be yours. A certificate title with broken English underneath it wastes the year. Grammarly Pro is Rs 1,400 for 1 month or Rs 3,500 for 12 months on your own email, which is enough to clean the bullet points you add under the certificate.

How to start the year without wasting the first week

  1. Pick one program, not five. Write its name in the order chat and ask us to confirm it carries the Plus badge.
  2. Confirm where the profile will live, so the certificate lands on an account you can show.
  3. Open the Coursera Plus page, pay Rs 3,200, and send the screenshot.
  4. We activate and send the steps. Sign in only through Coursera's site. The usual timing is in the delivery policy.
  5. Complete the first graded item in the first seven days. A year that starts with a finished quiz is a year you are more likely to finish. A year that starts with a bookmark is how the plan expires.
  6. When a certificate is issued, download the PDF and add it to LinkedIn from the share options Coursera provides. Then write two CV bullets about what you built, not only the certificate name.

The 1-year warranty on our listing is for the access we sold. It does not extend a course Coursera has retired, and it does not add programs Coursera excludes from Plus. If the login fails, screenshot it and message us inside the warranty window.
