Which one to buy

Choose Gemini Pro when your work already lives in Gmail, Docs, Drive or Sheets. Both listed lengths are activated on your Google account. The 12-month plan is Rs 1,500. The 2-year plan is Rs 3,350 and includes 1 month of YouTube Premium. Use the Gemini Pro page to confirm which Google features are in the plan you tap.

Choose ChatGPT Go when you want a second writing tool and you accept a shared login. The 6-month plan is Rs 2,200. The 3-month ready-made account is Rs 2,900. Do not put client contracts, identity documents or passwords in a shared chat. If you need a private ChatGPT workspace for a month, look at ChatGPT Business instead of stretching Go.

Choose SuperGrok when you want Grok and X Premium+ on your own account. Grok Premium is Rs 3,500 for 1 month. Grok Bot plus Grok Premium is Rs 4,999. Grok Heavy is Rs 19,999 for 1 month. The badge and the model limits are controlled by X, not by a promise on this page. Read the SuperGrok page before you pay the heavy plan.

How to pay from Pakistan

Outside Pakistan, the payment method on the site is USDT (Binance, BEP20 or TRC20), and the amount in your currency is confirmed on WhatsApp before you pay.

We never ask for the password of your Google, OpenAI or X account. Setup usually takes 5–30 minutes after the screenshot is confirmed. If a plan fails during the warranty, the refund policy says we fix it, replace it, or refund the remaining value.

What this comparison leaves out

Google AI Ultra, Claude API and Codex API are different products. Ultra is a video and high-limit Google plan with its own prices, from Rs 2,500 for the shared month. Claude API and Codex API are key packs with Contact Support and no rupee price on the page. Do not treat them as a cheaper Gemini.
