Best AI Tools for Lawyers in Pakistan

A lawyer's day is research, drafting, and explaining things to clients — and all three are exactly what AI tools are good at now. A Pakistani advocate or in-house counsel does not need twenty subscriptions; five well-chosen tools cover the whole practice, and together they cost less than one hour of billable time a month. This roundup prices each one in rupees, says what it actually does in a law office, and tells you which to buy first.

If you buy only one thing this month, buy ChatGPT Go at Rs 2,200 for six months. It is the cheapest tool on this list and the one you will open every day. The rest of this guide works through the five in order, with the real prices and the honest limits.

ChatGPT Go: research and first drafts

ChatGPT Go is the budget tier of ChatGPT, and for a lawyer it is the right tier. At Rs 2,200 for 6 months on a shared premium account — or Rs 2,900 for 3 months on a ready-made account — it costs about Rs 367 a month, and it handles the work a lawyer actually delegates to a junior: summarise this judgment, explain this clause in plain English, draft a first version of this letter, list the arguments for and against. The rule is the same as with any junior: never file the first draft without reading it. AI invents case citations — every lawyer using it must check every citation against the real record before it goes anywhere near a court or a client. Used that way, Go turns an hour of first-draft work into ten minutes of editing. Our ChatGPT Go price post and our ChatGPT Go how-to guide cover the two rows in full.

Claude API: long documents and review

Claude is the model lawyers reach for when the document is long. Where ChatGPT Go is the quick assistant, Claude API credits are the heavy reader: feed it a fifty-page contract, a pile of case notes or a full agreement, and ask for the risks, the inconsistencies and the summary. The store sells Claude API credits at a price on request — message the team on WhatsApp for a quote, because the right pack depends on how much you read in a month. For a firm that reviews contracts weekly, API credits are the honest buy; for a solo practitioner who mostly needs short answers, ChatGPT Go already covers it. Never paste a client's confidential file into any AI tool without the client's consent and without checking your bar rules — the convenience is real, and so is the duty of confidentiality.

Gamma Pro: client presentations

Clients do not read memos; they remember the meeting where someone showed them the picture. Gamma Pro turns a prompt or a rough outline into a clean, presentable deck in minutes, and at from Rs 2,400 for 1 month — or Rs 9,500 for 12 months — it is the tool that makes a small firm look like a big one in front of a client. Use it for pitch decks, for explaining a case strategy to a client, and for internal training slides. The 12-month row at Rs 9,500 works out at about Rs 792 a month, so if you present more than once a month, buy the year. Our content writers roundup also prices Gamma, and the verdict is the same: it is the fastest way from an idea to a deck a client will actually look at.

Notion Business: case files and matters

Every firm has the same quiet problem: the case file lives in five places — WhatsApp, email, a notebook and someone's memory. Notion Business is the single place. At from Rs 1,250 for 3 months, or Rs 4,600 for 12 months, it holds matters, deadlines, client notes and checklists in one searchable workspace, and the whole team sees the same file. The 12-month row works out at about Rs 383 a month. For a two- or three-lawyer chamber, that is the cheapest case-management system you will ever buy, and it replaces the spreadsheet that only one person understands. Set it up once per matter — client, court, next date, documents, tasks — and the weekly "where is that file" conversation disappears.

Grammarly Pro: clean drafting

A typo in a client letter costs more than a subscription. Grammarly Pro, from Rs 1,400 for 1 month or Rs 3,500 for 12 months, catches the grammar, the punctuation and the tone problems in every draft before the client sees it — and the tone check is the part that matters in law, where a letter that sounds aggressive when you meant firm can cost a negotiation. The 12-month row at Rs 3,500 works out at about Rs 292 a month. It is the last tool on this list because it is the polish, not the engine — buy it after ChatGPT Go, and pair it with it: draft in Go, polish in Grammarly, file with confidence.

FAQ

Which AI tool should a lawyer buy first in Pakistan?

ChatGPT Go at Rs 2,200 for 6 months. It covers research, first drafts and plain-English explanations for about Rs 367 a month, and it is the tool a lawyer opens every day.

Can lawyers use AI for legal drafting in Pakistan?

Yes, for first drafts and research — but never file an AI draft unchecked. AI can invent case citations, so every citation must be verified against the real record before it goes to a court or a client.

How much do AI tools for lawyers cost in Pakistan?

The five in this guide run from Rs 2,200 for 6 months of ChatGPT Go to Rs 9,500 for a year of Gamma Pro. A solo lawyer can cover the basics for under Rs 1,000 a month.

Is Claude API better than ChatGPT Go for lawyers?

For long documents, yes — Claude reads fifty-page contracts and finds the risks better. For everyday questions and first drafts, ChatGPT Go at Rs 2,200 for 6 months is the better buy. Claude API credits are price on request.

Which tool makes client presentations for a law firm?

Gamma Pro, from Rs 2,400 for 1 month or Rs 9,500 for 12 months. It turns an outline into a clean deck in minutes — the fastest way from an idea to a presentation a client will look at.

Lawyer ke liye sab se pehle kaunsa AI tool loon?

ChatGPT Go — Rs 2,200 mein 6 mahine, research aur pehle draft ke liye. Uske baad Grammarly Pro Rs 1,400 mahina drafting ki safai ke liye, aur Gamma Pro client presentations ke liye.
