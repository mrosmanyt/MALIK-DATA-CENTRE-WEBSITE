The Surfshark VPN price in Pakistan depends on which of the three rows you mean. On our Surfshark page the shared month is Rs 400, the 2-month ready-made account is Rs 1,900, and the 2-month coupon is Rs 850. Those are different products. A shared login is the budget way to open the app. A ready-made account is a login we hand to you for two months. A coupon is a code, and it is not the account listed beside it.

What not to do on the shared month

The tool page says a VPN account stores connection settings, not your files, and that full privacy means a login on your own email. The shared month is still a login other people can be on. Treat it as a connection, not as a private vault.

Do not do these on the Rs 400 row:

  1. Do not change the email or the password. A shared login that you edit is a login the next person cannot open, and it is not yours to redesign.
  2. Do not save banking, email, or store passwords inside a browser profile you only opened because the VPN was on. The VPN row is not a password manager.
  3. Do not use the shared month as the account you will still need in month three. It is monthly access. When it ends, the login is not a 2-month account you forgot to upgrade.
  4. Do not message us a new password you set yourself and expect the warranty on the 2-month account to apply. That warranty line is on the Rs 1,900 row.

If the work is a client laptop, a trip, or anything you would not want tied to a login you do not control, skip the shared month. Buy the 2-month ready-made account, or buy NordVPN at Rs 2,500 for monthly access activated on your email. NordVPN's listing describes access on up to 10 devices. Its specs name 6,000-plus servers, a threat-block engine, and private double-hop. The original price on that row is Rs 4,500. It is one month on your email, not a Surfshark coupon.

Account, coupon, or the shared month

Pick from the job, not from the smallest number.

The shared month at Rs 400 is the row for ordinary browsing on a budget, with the spec lines above, when you accept a shared profile and you will leave the password alone. The original price of Rs 1,500 is the figure printed beside it. It is not a second plan.

The 2-month account at Rs 1,900 is the row when you want a ready-made Surfshark login for two months and the listing's full warranty. It costs more than two shared months (Rs 400 twice is Rs 800) because the access type is different. You are not buying "the same thing, longer". You are buying an account row instead of a shared profile. Say so in the chat if you thought they were identical.

The coupon at Rs 850 is a code for two months. The catalog says it is separate from the 2-month account. Buy it when you will apply a coupon, not when you want us to hand you a finished login. If you have no Surfshark account to apply it to, say that before you pay. A code without a place to redeem it is not the Rs 1,900 account.

There is no 12-month Surfshark row on the tool page. Do not pay a yearly figure from a chat unless it is one of these three prices or a written quote that names a different product. HMA VPN is the yearly key on this store, at Rs 5,500, if a year is the actual requirement. That is a licence key, not a Surfshark login.

What this price is not

Rs 400 is not "Surfshark for as long as you want". Rs 850 is not the ready-made account. Rs 1,900 is not NordVPN on your own email. None of the three rows is a promise about a particular website loading in a particular city. Server lists on a spec line are the catalog text. A site that blocks a datacenter address can still fail, and the fix is a different server inside the app or a different product, not a new claim we did not print.
