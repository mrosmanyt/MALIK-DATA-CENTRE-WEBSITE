Every day, buyers message us asking for the Veo 3 price in Pakistan, expecting a simple subscription they can buy like CapCut Pro. The honest answer is different: Google does not sell Veo 3 on its own. This guide explains how Veo 3 access actually works, what Google's own plans cost in Pakistan, and the two routes at MALIK DATA CENTRE that get you generating Veo videos today.

Is there a Veo 3 price in Pakistan?

There is no standalone Veo 3 subscription, in Pakistan or anywhere else. Veo 3 is Google's AI video model, and it lives inside Google Flow, Google's filmmaking tool. Instead of a Veo plan, you pay for a Google AI plan and receive a monthly allowance of Flow credits, and each video you generate spends some of those credits. So the real question is not "what does Veo cost?" but "which Google AI plan gives me enough Flow credits for my work?"

Anyone with a Google account can open Flow and gets a small free daily credit allowance to test it. Serious, daily video production needs a paid plan, which is where the prices below come in.

How Veo 3 access works: Flow credits explained

Flow credits are the currency of Google Flow. Veo generations come in different quality modes, and at Google's published credit rates a fast draft generation costs far fewer credits than a full-quality one, so the same plan can mean many quick clips or a smaller number of polished videos. How many videos do Flow credits actually make?

Credit maths decides which plan you need, so it is worth doing before you buy. At Google's published rates, a Veo Fast generation costs about 20 credits and a full Quality generation about 100 credits, with the lighter Lite mode costing less. On the Pro level's 1,000 monthly credits, that works out to roughly 50 Fast generations a month, or about 10 Quality generations, or a mix of both. That suits a creator polishing a few videos a week, especially if drafts are made in the cheaper modes and only final scenes are rendered at full quality.

Ultra's allowances change the workflow completely: with 10,000 or more credits a month, a faceless channel can generate daily, test several versions of every scene and still stay inside the plan. This is why we ask buyers about their upload schedule before recommending a plan. One video a week rarely justifies Ultra, while a channel publishing daily AI stories will feel Pro's ceiling within the first month. FAQ

Is Veo 3 free in Pakistan?

Partly. Any Google account can open Google Flow and receives a small free daily credit allowance, which is enough to test Veo. Regular or high-quality production needs a paid Google AI plan, because generations spend Flow credits.

Can I buy Veo 3 on its own, without a Google AI plan?

No. Google does not sell a standalone Veo 3 subscription anywhere. Veo access always comes through Flow credits attached to a Google AI plan, such as Gemini Pro or Google AI Ultra.

Choose Gemini Pro at Rs 1,500 for 12 months if you generate Veo clips a few times a week. Choose Google AI Ultra, from Rs 2,500, if you produce videos daily and need the largest Flow credit allowances.
