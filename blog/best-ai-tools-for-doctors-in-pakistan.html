Writing and patient messages

ChatGPT Go is the drafting tool: referral letters, discharge summaries, explanations of a diagnosis in plain Urdu or English, and replies to patient messages on WhatsApp. At Rs 2,200 for 6 months it is the cheapest paid chat plan in the catalog, and the ChatGPT Go price guide covers both rows (the 3-month ready-made account is Rs 2,900). Write the draft, then read it back as a doctor before it goes to a patient — the tool writes the words, you keep the judgement.

Grammarly Pro is the second pair of eyes on anything formal: a referral letter to a consultant, a case report, a paper for a journal. Rs 1,400 for a month or Rs 3,500 for a year, and the Grammarly price guide has the full detail. For a doctor, its value is not grammar alone — it is tone and clarity in letters where a careless sentence can confuse the receiving doctor.

Research and staying current

Gemini Pro at Rs 1,500 for 12 months on your own Gmail is the research tool: summarise a guideline, compare two treatment approaches, prepare for a CME talk, or turn a long paper into the five points that matter. It also puts Gemini inside Gmail, Docs and Sheets, so the same plan handles the clinic's email and paperwork. The Gemini Pro price guide covers the 2-year row too (Rs 3,350 with a month of YouTube Premium). Use it to learn faster — never as a substitute for checking the guideline itself before you act on it.

Patient education

Most patients forget half of what they hear in the consulting room. Gamma Pro turns a topic — diabetes diet, blood pressure, vaccination — into clean slides from a prompt, at Rs 2,400 for a month or Rs 9,500 for a year; the Gamma price guide has both rows. Canva Pro at Rs 400 for a year makes the waiting-room posters, awareness graphics and social posts to match; the Canva Pro price guide explains the on-your-own-email activation. Together they are the cheapest patient-education setup in this guide, and both are usable by clinic staff, not only by the doctor.

Running the clinic

Notion Business is where the clinic's memory lives: protocols, drug lists, duty rota, leave record and the checklists for opening and closing the clinic. Rs 1,250 for 3 months or Rs 4,600 for 12 months as a workspace activation, covered in the Notion Business price guide. Zoom Pro at Rs 1,500 a month is the teleconsultation room: meetings up to 30 hours, 100 participants and cloud recording, covered in the Zoom Pro price guide. For follow-ups with patients who live far from the clinic, a recorded video follow-up beats a missed appointment.

What AI must not do in a clinic

Three rules, and they are not negotiable. First, AI output is a draft. Every letter, summary and message needs a doctor's review before it reaches a patient — the tools in this guide write well, but they do not examine anyone. Second, never put patient-identifiable information into a shared account. Several plans in this catalog are shared or ready-made logins; use them for general writing and education, and keep anything with a patient's name, number or record off them entirely. Third, AI is not a diagnosis. It can summarise a guideline and draft an explanation, but the clinical decision stays with the doctor who saw the patient.
