Buying Gemini Pro in Pakistan is a payment problem, not a Google problem. Google sells Google AI Pro — the plan most people call Gemini Pro — for $19.99 a month, and that checkout wants an international card most Pakistani buyers do not have. The local route is simpler: order on WhatsApp, pay in rupees with EasyPaisa or JazzCash, and get Gemini Pro activated on your own Gmail. This guide walks through every step, what to send, what to check after activation, and when a cheaper plan would do the same job.

What you are actually buying

Gemini Pro is Google's paid Gemini tier, sold by Google as Google AI Pro. At MALIK DATA CENTRE it costs Rs 1,500 for 12 months, activated on your own Gmail, or Rs 3,350 for 2 years of Gemini Pro plus 1 month of YouTube Premium. The 12-month listing shows an original price of Rs 3,000. The full Gemini Pro price guide explains both rows in detail.

What the plan gives you is the stronger Gemini model for writing, study, research and everyday work, Gemini inside Gmail and Google Docs, and 2 TB of Google storage as part of Google AI Pro. It is a full year of access on the account you already use — your chats and Drive files stay on your own Gmail, and nothing about the plan is shared with another buyer. That last point matters: this is an activation on your email, not a shared login, so nobody else can read your chats.

It is not a video-production plan. If you are buying Gemini Pro mainly to make Veo 3 videos in Google Flow, read the Gemini Pro vs Google AI Ultra comparison first — Ultra buys far more Flow credits per rupee, and our Veo 3 in Flow guide shows how the free daily credits work before you pay anything.

Before you start: what you need

Three things, and all of them take under a minute to prepare. First, a Gmail address you control and will keep for a year. A university address that disappears when you graduate is a bad place to put a 12-month plan — use a personal Gmail you will still own next November. Second, WhatsApp on your phone, because the whole order happens there. Third, a payment method: EasyPaisa, JazzCash, NayaPay, UPaisa, Meezan Bank or a bank transfer in Pakistan, or USDT from abroad.

You do not need a credit card, a debit card, or a Google payment profile. You also do not need to create a new Google account or hand over your Gmail password — activation works on the Gmail address you send, and nobody at the store needs your password. If any seller asks for your Gmail password, stop: that is not how this works, and our guide to spotting fake AI subscription sellers explains the red flags.

How activation on your Gmail works

Activation on your own email means the plan is applied to the Google account you already use, the same way Google would apply it if you paid Google directly. You keep your own login, your own password and your own history. Your Drive files, your Gmail and your Docs stay exactly where they are — the plan adds the Pro tier on top of them.

This is different from the two other access types you will see in the catalog. A shared account means several buyers use one login, which is cheaper but means your chats sit on someone else's account. A ready-made account means you get a fresh login made for you. Gemini Pro at Rs 1,500 is neither of those: it is your Gmail, upgraded. Our guide to private vs shared vs invitation AI accounts explains the trade-offs if you are comparing plans across tools.

What to check on day one

Do these four checks in the first few minutes, while the WhatsApp chat is still open. First, open Gemini on the web and confirm the Pro model is selectable — if you only see the free model, say so immediately. Second, ask Gemini a question that needs the stronger model, such as summarising a long document, and confirm the answer comes back properly. Third, check your Google storage allowance shows the AI Pro storage. Fourth, open Gmail and Docs and confirm Gemini appears there, since that integration is part of what you paid for.

If anything looks wrong, message the team on the same WhatsApp chat with a screenshot. The warranty is described in our warranty policy, and the fastest fixes happen when the problem is reported on day one, not a month later.

Gemini Pro vs the cheaper alternatives

Not everyone needs the full Pro plan, and the catalog has two cheaper doors into Gemini. Gemini Plus costs Rs 600 for 12 months or Rs 650 for 6 months, delivered as an activation link — it is the budget route if you mainly want Gemini access without paying for the full Pro tier, and the Gemini Plus price guide explains exactly what it is and is not. ChatGPT Go at Rs 2,200 for 6 months is the other cheap seat at the table: a different chatbot, strong for everyday chat and study, and our ChatGPT Go vs Gemini Pro for students guide compares the two for study work.

The honest rule is simple. If you live in Gmail and Docs and want the stronger Gemini model for a full year on your own account, buy Gemini Pro at Rs 1,500 — it costs about Rs 125 a month over the year, less than a single month of Google's own $19.99 price. If you only need Gemini occasionally, Gemini Plus at Rs 600 for the year is the cheaper test. And if your real goal is Veo 3 video, skip both and look at Google AI Ultra from Rs 2,500, because that is where the Flow credits live.

Common problems and fixes

The activation went to the wrong Gmail. This is almost always a typo in the address you sent. Re-check the spelling in your WhatsApp message before you send it, and if the mistake already happened, message the team immediately with the correct address.

Gemini still shows the free model after activation. First, make sure you are signed in to the same Gmail you sent us — most people have two or three Google accounts on one phone, and Gemini opens on whichever one was last used. Sign out and back in on the right account, then check again.

Payment confirmed but no reply yet. Activation usually takes 5 to 30 minutes after payment is confirmed, and busy hours run longer. If an hour has passed, send a short follow-up on the same chat with your payment screenshot — do not start a second order.

You want Gemini Pro but only for a few months. The catalog sells Gemini Pro by the year, not by the month. If you only need a few months of a chatbot, ChatGPT Go at Rs 2,200 for 6 months or Gemini Plus at Rs 650 for 6 months fits a short horizon better.
