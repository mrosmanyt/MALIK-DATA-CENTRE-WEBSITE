When the seat is enough, and when you want it built

The tool page FAQ says we can build the automation, and it points to a quote. That quote is not included in Rs 6,500.

Buy the seat if:

  1. Someone on the team can connect nodes, or is willing to learn on a real workflow this month.
  2. You know the apps. A WhatsApp inbox, a Google Sheet, a CRM, or an email tool you already use.
  3. You want the login for 12 months, with the 1-year warranty on the listing, and you will keep the workflow files yourself.

Ask for a build on AI automation services if:

  • Nobody can open the editor after delivery. A private account with zero workflows is a login, not a follow-up system.
  • The job is "when a WhatsApp lead arrives, write the row, send the reply, and tell the owner." That is a project. The price of the project is the quote, not the Starter row.
  • You need more than 5 active workflows on day one. Starter's spec says 5. Do not pay and then discover the sixth flow has nowhere to sit. Say the number in the chat before you pay.

We will not ask for the password of your personal email. A private account still needs the API keys of the apps you connect. Those keys are yours. Do not paste a live payment key into a group chat beside the n8n password. Create the smallest key the workflow needs, and turn it off if you stop using the flow.

n8n or Gumloop

Gumloop Pro is Rs 4,550 for 12 months, with a listed original price of Rs 18,000. Setup is activation on your email. The specs name a visual AI pipeline builder, web data extraction, scheduled scraping, and a 1-year full warranty.

The tool pages separate them this way: Gumloop is the easier builder for people who are not writing nodes. n8n is the more flexible one for developers and for workflows that have to branch. A lead list that needs scraping and a sheet can be a Gumloop job. A WhatsApp inbox that has to call your own API, wait, and write back is closer to n8n.

Do not buy both to "see which one feels nicer" in the same week unless you already know you have two different jobs. Rs 6,500 plus Rs 4,550 is a lot of builder for one follow-up message. Pick the job:

  • You will maintain the workflow yourself and you may outgrow a simple canvas. n8n at Rs 6,500.
  • A marketer needs to scrape and enrich a list without learning nodes. Gumloop at Rs 4,550.
  • You want neither login and you want the workflow handed to you working. That is the automation service quote, and you may still need one of these seats underneath it. Ask which seat the quote assumes.

Claude API and other API packs are a third thing. They are price on request and they are not a chat login. This n8n page does not quote those packs. If a node in your workflow calls an API, the key is a separate order. Say so before you pay for Starter alone and expect tokens to be inside it.
