Manus Pro vs Genspark Plus in Pakistan: Which AI Agent Does More?

Manus and Genspark are the two AI agents Pakistani buyers compare most — tools that do not just answer, but work: research a topic, build a report, make the slides. Both are in the catalog, and the choice is not about price alone, because the cheaper one is not the weaker one. Manus from Rs 2,900 does the whole task; Genspark at Rs 5,300 does slides and search brilliantly. This post compares them.

What Manus Pro does best

Manus Pro is an autonomous agent: give it a task — research a market, compare suppliers, build a report — and it works through the steps itself, browsing, gathering and writing, then hands you the finished result. It is the tool for the buyer who wants the work done, not a chat. Our Manus Pro price post covers the 1-month and 12-month plans. If your job is research that ends in a document, Manus is the buy.

What Genspark Plus does best

Genspark Plus is the agent for slides and search: ask a question and get a researched answer with sources, or ask for a deck and get slides that are actually presentable. Its strength is the output format — the slide, the structured page, the cited summary — where Manus gives you a report, Genspark gives you something ready to present. Our Genspark Plus price post covers the 1-month plan. If your job ends in a presentation, Genspark is the buy.

Who should buy which

Buy Manus Pro if you want tasks completed — research, reports, comparisons done for you, at Rs 2,900 a month or Rs 13,000 for the year. Buy Genspark Plus if you present — decks, cited research pages, search answers you can show a client. If you do both, start with Manus for the month and add Genspark when a presentation lands.
