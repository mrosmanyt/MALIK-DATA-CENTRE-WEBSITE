Picsart Pro vs Canva Pro in Pakistan: Which Design App to Buy?

Published 27 November 2026 7 min read MALIK DATA CENTRE Team
Quick answer: Picsart Pro at Rs 3,500 for 11 months is the phone photo editor for quick edits and stickers; Canva Pro at Rs 400 a year is the template machine for posts, brand kits and presentations. Most designers buy Canva first and add Picsart for phone edits.

Picsart and Canva are the two design apps on almost every Pakistani phone — and they are not the same tool. Picsart edits photos; Canva designs everything else. The price gap is just as stark: Picsart Pro costs Rs 3,500 for 11 months, Canva Pro costs Rs 400 for a whole year. This post compares them so you buy the one your work actually needs.

Canva Pro is nearly nine times cheaper for a longer plan — Rs 400 for 12 months against Rs 3,500 for 11 months. That is not a close call on price, and it is why Canva is the first design buy for almost everyone. Picsart's price buys a different thing: a phone-first photo editor with AI tools, stickers and background removal that Canva does not match. Pay the Rs 3,500 only if your work is photo editing on your phone.

What Picsart Pro does best

Picsart Pro is the phone photo editor: quick edits, AI background removal, stickers, filters and collages, all from the app in your pocket. It is the tool for sellers editing product photos, creators making thumbnails on the go, and anyone whose design work starts with a photo. Our Picsart Pro price post covers the 11-month plan in full. If your day is photos, Picsart earns its price.

What Canva Pro does best

Canva Pro is the template machine: social posts, presentations, brand kits, logos, flyers and videos, all from templates, at Rs 400 a year on your own email. It is the cheapest design plan in the catalog and the one every small shop, teacher and freelancer in Pakistan starts with. Our Canva Pro price post covers the 12-month plan. If your day is posts and presentations, Canva is the buy — and at Rs 400, it is barely a decision.

Who should buy which

Buy Canva Pro first — at Rs 400 a year it covers posts, presentations and brand work for almost everyone, and it is the best value in the whole catalog. Add Picsart Pro at Rs 3,500 for 11 months if your work is photo editing on your phone: product photos, thumbnails, stickers and quick edits. FAQ

Which is cheaper, Picsart Pro or Canva Pro?

Canva Pro is far cheaper: Rs 400 for 12 months against Picsart Pro at Rs 3,500 for 11 months.

Is Picsart better than Canva for photo editing?

Yes, for phone photo editing — AI tools, background removal, stickers and filters. Canva is better for templates, posts and presentations.

Can I use both Picsart and Canva?

Yes. Many sellers edit the product photo in Picsart, then design the post around it in Canva. Both are sold separately on the store.

11 months on your own email, for Rs 3,500. Canva Pro lasts 12 months for Rs 400.
