Cheap is fine when the account is yours

Canva Pro at Rs 400 for 12 months is activated on your email, so posters and class presentations stay in your account. That is the design plan to buy before a shared design login. YouTube Premium at Rs 500, Rs 1,200 or Rs 6,200 is also on your email.

Gemini Plus at Rs 600 for 12 months, or Rs 650 for 6 months, is an activation link. Gemini Pro at Rs 1,500 for 12 months is the plan that is activated on your Gmail and is the one to use for long notes and Drive files. The gap between those two starting prices is the difference between a link and your own Google account. For a thesis, pay for Gemini Pro.

edX at Rs 400 for 12 months is a ready-made account. Use it for courses. Do not treat a ready-made login as a private inbox.

Where a shared plan hurts a student

ChatGPT Go at Rs 2,200 for 6 months is shared. It is useful for practice questions and outlines. It is the wrong place for a draft that names your university ID, a medical note, or another student’s work. The 3-month ready-made ChatGPT Go plan is Rs 2,900, which is still not the same as a private email upgrade. If the chat must stay yours, use Gemini Pro on your Gmail or ask on WhatsApp before you pay.

Grammarly Pro is on your email: Rs 1,400 for 1 month or Rs 3,500 for 12 months. Buy the month if you have one deadline. Buy the year if you write in English every week. QuillBot Premium is Rs 1,800 for 1 month on a ready-made account. Paraphrasing a source without a citation is still plagiarism. The tool does not change that.

CapCut’s cheapest row is a team invite at Rs 650 for 1 month, or Rs 4,100 for 6 months. A private month is Rs 1,250. Some CapCut packs are price on request. Those rows say Contact Support and have no rupee price.

A student budget in three steps

  1. Spend Rs 400 on Canva Pro for the year if you make slides or society posts.
  2. Spend Rs 1,500 on Gemini Pro if you study in Google Docs. Skip Gemini Plus if you need the work on your own Gmail.
  3. Add Grammarly for the month (Rs 1,400) only when a submission is in English and you will check the suggestions yourself.

That is Canva plus Gemini before Grammarly, or those two plus one Grammarly month. ChatGPT Go is optional on top, not a replacement for Gemini when the files are personal.

No. Gemini Plus starts at Rs 600 and is delivered as an activation link. Gemini Pro starts at Rs 1,500 and is activated on your Gmail. Read both pages before you pay the cheaper one.
