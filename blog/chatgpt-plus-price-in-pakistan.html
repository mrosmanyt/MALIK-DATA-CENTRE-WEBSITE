What is the ChatGPT Plus price in Pakistan?

ChatGPT Plus officially costs $20 per month from OpenAI. On Pakistan's App Store, the same subscription shows as Rs 4,900 per month (checked November 2026). At recent exchange rates the web price works out to roughly Rs 5,600 a month before your bank adds its foreign-transaction markup and taxes, so the true monthly cost of paying OpenAI directly is usually higher than the headline dollar price suggests.

Why buying Plus directly is hard from Pakistan

Most local debit cards in Pakistan are not enabled for international recurring payments, and OpenAI's checkout declines many of the cards that are. Buyers end up borrowing a relative's card from abroad, paying dollar-billing markups every month, or giving up at the payment screen. That payment wall is the entire reason guides like ours on buying AI tools in Pakistan without a credit card exist, and why local stores like ours take payment in rupees instead.

What ChatGPT Plus gives you

Plus is OpenAI's full individual tier. Compared with the free plan it gives you much higher usage limits, priority access at busy times, OpenAI's most capable models and reasoning, image generation and editing, advanced voice mode and early access to new features as OpenAI releases them. It is built for people who use ChatGPT heavily every day for professional work.

OpenAI also sells a lower-cost tier called ChatGPT Go, which sits between Free and Plus: more usage than Free, at a much lower price, without the very top limits of Plus. Our ChatGPT Go price guide explains exactly what Go includes, because for many buyers it is the smarter purchase.

Is ChatGPT Plus worth it in Pakistan?

At roughly Rs 4,900 to 5,600 every single month, Plus is one of the most expensive ways to buy AI help in Pakistan, so it has to earn its place. It is worth it if ChatGPT is a core work tool for you: you hit free or Go limits regularly, you need the strongest reasoning for coding, analysis or long documents, or your income directly depends on producing more with it. A freelancer earning from international clients, for example, can justify Plus the way they would justify any professional tool.

It is harder to justify for study help, everyday writing, brainstorming and general questions, because a mid-tier plan covers those comfortably at a fraction of the monthly cost. This is also why comparing across brands matters: a full year of Gemini Pro at our store costs less than a single month of Plus bought directly, and six months of ChatGPT Go costs less than half of one Plus month. Buy the tier that matches your actual usage, not the tier with the biggest name, and upgrade only when you keep hitting the ceiling of the plan you are on.
