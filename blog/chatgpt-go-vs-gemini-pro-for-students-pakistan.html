If you are a university student in Pakistan, you have probably used the free versions of ChatGPT and Gemini and hit their limits during assignment week. Which is better for students: ChatGPT Go or Gemini Pro?

Gemini Pro is the better all-round choice for most students, and ChatGPT Go is the better choice if you only want a strong chat assistant for writing and explanations.

The main reasons are simple:

  • Cost per month. Our Gemini Pro plans cover 18 months, while our ChatGPT Go plan covers 6 months.
  • Your own account. Both Gemini plans are activated on your own Google account. Our ChatGPT Go plan is a shared account.
  • Where you study. If your notes, slides and assignments already live in Google Docs and Drive, Gemini works inside those apps.

ChatGPT Go still has real strengths. Many students find ChatGPT's answers easy to follow, and Go gives higher limits than the free plan for file uploads, image generation and data analysis.

Shared vs private: why the access type matters for students

A shared account is one ChatGPT login used by more than one customer, so other users can see the chat history on it. A private account or a plan on your own email belongs only to you.

For students this matters more than it seems:

  1. Privacy. On a shared ChatGPT account, do not upload your CNIC, transcripts, fee slips, or anything personal. Treat every chat as visible to others.
  2. History. Your chats on a shared account are not yours to keep. Copy important answers into your own notes.
  3. Continuity. With Gemini on your own Google account, your chats, Drive files and settings stay with you after the plan ends.

If privacy is your top concern, Gemini on your own Google account is the safer fit. For more on this, read our guide on whether cheap AI subscriptions are safe.

Where ChatGPT Go shines

ChatGPT Go is strong for clear explanations and fast drafting. It works well when you want to:

  • Ask "explain this like I'm new to it" questions about a lecture topic.
  • Get feedback on the structure of an essay you wrote yourself.
  • Analyse a spreadsheet for a statistics or business course.
  • Build a custom GPT or Project for one subject, such as accounting or physics.

OpenAI says Go does not include legacy models, and some advanced features need Plus or higher. For a typical undergraduate, Go's limits are usually enough.

Where Gemini Pro shines

Gemini Pro is strongest when your study work involves long documents and Google apps. It is a good fit when you want to:

  • Upload a full research paper or a long PDF of lecture notes and ask questions about it.
  • Run Deep Research to get a multi-page overview of a topic before you start reading sources.
  • Turn your notes into revision material with Gemini Notebook.
  • Use Gemini directly inside Google Docs while you write, or inside Sheets for lab data.

Google also bundles extras with the official Pro plan, such as cloud storage and AI credits for creative tools. What you get on our plans is listed on the Gemini Pro page.

How to use AI honestly in university work

AI can help you learn faster, but most universities in Pakistan have rules on plagiarism and AI-written work. Use it to understand, plan and check, not to submit text you did not write.

  1. Use AI to explain a concept, then write the answer in your own words.
  2. Ask it to quiz you on your notes before exams.
  3. Check every fact, date and reference it gives you against your textbook or a real source.
  4. Read your department's policy on AI use, and ask your teacher if you are unsure.

One more point: Google says Gemini in Google apps and some AI benefits are only for users aged 18 and over, so school and college students under 18 should check eligibility first.

FAQ

ChatGPT Go ya Gemini Pro, students ke liye kaun sa behtar hai?

For most students, Gemini Pro is better value because our plans last 18 months and work on your own Google account. ChatGPT Go is a good choice if you mainly want writing help and are comfortable with a shared account.

Is ChatGPT Go from your store private?

No. Our ChatGPT Go plan is a shared premium account, so other users may see chats on it. Do not share personal or university documents on it.

Can I use ChatGPT or Gemini to write my assignment?
