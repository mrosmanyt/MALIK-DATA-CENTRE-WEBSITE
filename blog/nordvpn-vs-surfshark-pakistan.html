What you are buying on each row

NordVPN, Rs 2,500, monthly, on your email. Our listing says the plan is activated on your email, with access described as 6,000+ servers, a threat-blocking feature, and a private double-hop VPN, on up to 10 devices. Because it is your email, the subscription is not a password you share with other buyers. That is the privacy difference that matters more than the server count.

Surfshark monthly, Rs 400, shared. Our listing calls it a shared premium account, with CleanWeb ad blocking, 3,200+ server locations in the specs, and unlimited device connections. The family note on our tool page is direct: a VPN account stores connection settings, not your files, and for a login that is yours alone, NordVPN on your email is the closer fit. The Rs 400 plan is honest only if you know it is shared before you pay. You know that now.

Surfshark 2-month account, Rs 1,900. A ready-made account, longer than the monthly shared plan, listed with a full warranty. It is still a ready-made account we deliver, not an activation inside an email you have owned for years. Ask in the order chat who else can sign in, and do not assume it behaves like the Rs 400 shared month or like NordVPN.

Surfshark 2-month coupon, Rs 850. A coupon, delivered on WhatsApp, separate from the 2-month account. The warranty on this row is confirmed on WhatsApp. A coupon is a code you redeem, not a username we keep logging into for you. Ask where it is redeemed before you pay, and redeem it on an email you control if the instructions say so.

If you want a yearly VPN key instead of a monthly email login, HMA VPN is Rs 5,500 for a yearly licence key, with a listed original price of Rs 12,000. Our HMA specs say 1,100+ servers and support for up to 5 devices. That is a third product, for someone who wants a key in the HMA app for a year. It is not a cheaper NordVPN.

Devices and what the companies publish

Device limits are the other half of the decision, and the official pages are more specific than a slogan.

NordVPN's pricing page says a plan secures 10 devices at once. Nord's additional terms say the VPN service allows a maximum of ten simultaneous connections per account, and that proxy connections count toward that ten. Nord's support article adds a practical note: devices that share one server need different protocols, and a router setup uses one slot while covering the home network. A phone, a laptop and a TV fit easily. A room full of phones on one server may need a second server or a router.

Surfshark's pricing FAQ says a paid subscription allows unlimited simultaneous device connections, and that a free trial is limited to 3 devices until the trial ends. Apps exist for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS and common TV platforms, plus browser extensions. Our monthly Surfshark specs also say unlimited device connections. Extra phones do not turn a shared login into a private one.

Server counts differ by source. Our NordVPN listing says 6,000+ servers. Our Surfshark monthly specs say 3,200+ locations. Surfshark's own pricing page, when we read it, said 4,500+ servers in 100 countries. Use the server list inside the app you receive.

What the official checkouts charge

Our prices are rupee prices for the access type in the table. The companies' own sites sell longer bundles, in dollars, on a card.

NordVPN's pricing page shows several lengths. The long introductory Basic offer there was $2.99 per month (official), billed as $80.73 for the first 27 months (official), renewing later at $139.08 a year (official). The month-to-month Basic price on the same page was $14.99 per month (official). Higher Nord bundles add antivirus and identity tools and cost more. Our Rs 2,500 row is a monthly activation on your email. It is a different offer from that 27-month intro. Read the plan name inside the Nord account on day one.

Surfshark's pricing page showed Starter at $2.49 a month (official) on a long intro, with a sale price of $67.23 for the first 27 months (official). One and One+ add extra security products and are priced separately. Surfshark says the price can change with currency, VAT and coupons. Our three rows are the shared month, the 2-month account and the 2-month coupon. Screenshot the plan name in the app so you can see which product arrived.

Privacy: own email, shared login, coupon

Our guide to private, shared and invitation access is about AI logins. The same question applies here.

On NordVPN activated on your email, you hold the account. Other buyers are not in that inbox, and the device list is yours. This is the plan for café Wi-Fi, travel, and a laptop that also holds client files. The VPN encrypts the path from your device to the VPN server. Type bank addresses yourself. A VPN does not make a phishing page honest.

On the Rs 400 shared Surfshark login, other customers use the same account. A VPN account does not hold your documents or chats. It does hold the subscription. Someone with the password can change it, can fill the session list, and can see account settings. The risk is the shared credential.

On the coupon, redeem the code from the delivery message, and do not post it in a group. A redeemed code cannot be used twice.

On the shared monthly login, leave the password, the email and any saved payment method alone. Skip paid add-ons such as a dedicated IP, because those can bill a card or attach your name to a login you do not control. Keep your own passwords in a manager that is yours. If a company wants the VPN on a staff email, this shared row is the wrong product. Unlimited devices is not an invitation to pass the password to friends.

If those limits are a problem, buy NordVPN on your email, or ask who can sign in on the Rs 1,900 Surfshark account before you pay. CleanWeb and the threat-blocking line in our listings are filters for ads and known bad sites. They are useful on public Wi-Fi. Read the plan name in the app before you treat the install as a full antivirus suite. Our Nord row describes threat blocking and double-hop, not Nord's Prime identity product.

FAQ

NordVPN vs Surfshark: which is cheaper in Pakistan?

The shared Surfshark month is Rs 400. NordVPN monthly access on your own email is Rs 2,500. Surfshark also has a 2-month account at Rs 1,900 and a 2-month coupon at Rs 850. Cheaper and more private are different choices.

NordVPN Pakistan mein kitne ka hai?

Monthly access, aap ki email par, Rs 2,500 ka hai. Listing ki original price Rs 4,500 hai. Surfshark ka shared month Rs 400 ka hai. Apni email chahiye to NordVPN lein.

How many devices can I connect?

NordVPN's terms allow up to 10 simultaneous connections on one account. Surfshark's pricing FAQ says unlimited simultaneous devices on a paid plan, with a free trial limited to 3. Our HMA key lists up to 5 devices.

Is the shared Surfshark account safe?

It can encrypt your connection, and it does not hold your files. Other buyers have the same login, so do not change the password or store your own passwords in that account. For a login that is yours, choose NordVPN on your email.

Do I need a credit card?

No. You pay in rupees or USDT. NordVPN and Surfshark's own checkouts bill in dollars by card, PayPal or crypto, often on a long introductory bundle that is a different offer from our monthly rows.
