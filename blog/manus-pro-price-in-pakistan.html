When an agent is the right buy

ChatGPT is a conversation. You type, it answers, you copy the useful part. Manus, on our listing, is built to take a multi-step job: browse, work through the steps, and leave you a file.

ChatGPT Go is Rs 2,200 for 6 months on a shared account, with a listed original price of Rs 5,000, or Rs 2,900 for 3 months on a ready-made account. That is the chat product. It is not named ChatGPT Plus. If the work is "help me rewrite this paragraph" or "explain this error," the chat plan is the smaller bill and the better fit.

Buy the 12-month Manus row at Rs 13,000 when:

  1. The task is research a person would otherwise do: gather sources, compare them, and leave a document.
  2. You want a private account for a year, not a shared chat login.
  3. You have checked the warranty sentence in the WhatsApp reply, because the specs do not print days.

Buy the 1-month row at Rs 2,900 only if you accept the words "no warranty" and you have a job that finishes inside the month. It is a test of the agent, not a cheap year. Rs 2,900 is the same number as the 3-month ChatGPT Go ready-made plan, and the products are still different. One is a month of Manus with no warranty. The other is three months of chat.

Do not buy Manus because a video called every chatbot obsolete. If you will not give it a real task in the first week, the year is an expensive idle login. One month, with the warranty line you just read, answers "does this agent finish my kind of work?" The year is for the person who already knows the answer is yes.

What to check in the first task

  1. Open the Manus page and say "12-month private" or "1-month, no warranty" in the WhatsApp message to +92 348 9057646. Do not say only "Manus."
  2. Pay and send the screenshot. The delivery policy says most orders are ready within 5–30 minutes after payment is confirmed.
  3. Sign in. On the 12-month plan, do not change the password until you know whether the account is yours alone. On the 1-month plan, it is a ready-made account. Save files out of it the same day.
  4. Run one task you already understand, so you can tell a good file from a wrong one. A topic you know is a better test than a client project on minute one.
  5. Read the output before you send it to anyone. An agent that browses can still mis-state a price, a date or a name. The listing does not say the file is checked by a person.
  6. If the term is not the month or the year you paid for, screenshot it the same day. The refund policy applies to a plan we cannot deliver. The 1-month row's "no warranty" line still means you should report a failed delivery immediately, not that a missing login is acceptable.

Keep the payment screenshot, the plan end date, and the exported file. A ready-made month is a bad place to store the only copy of a report.

Browser actions on the 12-month spec are not permission to log the agent into a client's bank, ads account or email. Give it sources you are allowed to use. API integrations on that spec are a capability line, not a bundle of third-party API credit. If the task needs a separate API key, that key is a different order and many of those packs are price on request.

Choose Genspark when the job is a deck or a research pack and you only need this month. Choose the Rs 13,000 Manus row when you want the private year and the browser-and-workflow lines on that listing. Choose ChatGPT Go when you want a chat, not a file-producing agent. Buying all three in one week does not make the research better. It makes three logins to check.

Gamma is another slides tool, at Rs 2,400 for 1 month or Rs 9,500 for 12 months, and it is a slide generator rather than this kind of agent. If the only output is a deck from a prompt, compare Gamma's page before you pay for an agent.
