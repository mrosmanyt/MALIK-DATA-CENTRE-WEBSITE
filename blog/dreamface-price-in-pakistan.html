Talking photos are not a client avatar pipeline

The tool page describes DreamFace as a simple, lower-cost app for talking photos, and HeyGen as the professional avatar platform. That split is the buying rule.

Use DreamFace when:

  • The clip is a joke, a trend, a birthday message, or a test of whether a photo can speak.
  • You already have a photo you are allowed to animate. A face you do not have rights to use is not made acceptable by a lip-sync model.
  • You will export the video the same day and edit it somewhere else.

Do not use the Rs 1,400 plan as the whole production stack for a course or a paid ad. HeyGen is Rs 5,800 for a private 200-credit pack, with a listed original price of Rs 12,000, or Rs 9,150 for a 1-month Creator account. The 1-month HeyGen listing includes a 20-day warranty. HeyGen is the tool page we point at for avatar videos, lip-sync translation and a digital twin. Credit cost inside HeyGen depends on length and model. 200 credits is not 200 finished ads. Read HeyGen's own estimate before you render.

A talking photo still needs an edit. CapCut Pro starts at Rs 800 for 1 month if the missing piece is captions, cuts and export. DreamFace does not replace the editor. The clip it makes is an ingredient.

We do not publish how many videos Rs 1,400 produces. Length, language and the features you turn on change the result inside the app. Make one short clip on day one and read whatever limit the screen shows. If the screen shows a cap our specs did not print, that screenshot is the fact. Send it if the cap makes the plan useless for the job you named in the order.

Month, year, or HeyGen

  1. One experiment, or a week of casual clips. DreamFace for 1 month, Rs 1,400.
  2. You already like the output and you will use it across the year. DreamFace for 12 months, Rs 4,200. You are still on a ready-made account. Export as you go.
  3. A client, a course, or a brand face. Price HeyGen before you buy a year of DreamFace out of habit. Rs 5,800 for 200 credits can be the right smaller professional test. Rs 9,150 is the 1-month Creator account. Those numbers are higher because the product is the studio, not the party app.

Buying DreamFace and HeyGen in the same week is reasonable only if you already know they do different jobs. It is wasteful if you are hoping one of them will "feel right." Make one DreamFace clip first if the job is a talking photo. Open HeyGen first if the job is a presenter.

Voice is a separate bill if you need a cloned voice rather than the voice inside the talking-photo app. ElevenLabs is on its own page. Do not assume DreamFace includes a commercial voice clone. The specs do not say that.
