Ideogram or Canva

Canva Pro is Rs 400 for 12 months, activated on your email, with an original price of Rs 3,000. The specs on that yearly row include a full year, Magic Resize and the AI design studio, stock images and templates, and private canvas settings. Canva is the layout tool: you place text in a box you can edit. The letters are typed, so they are as correct as what you typed.

Ideogram is the generator: you describe a poster and the model draws the letters as part of the image. The tool page calls that its strength, and the spec line says accurate text in images. Accurate is the claim on the listing. It is not a guarantee that every word survives. Read the image at full size before you print it or upload it. A missing letter on a shop sign is a second generation, not a refund for a plan that did generate.

Use Ideogram when:

  • The design needs words painted into the picture, such as a title on a thumbnail or a short line on a poster.
  • You want private generations, which is the spec line, on a ready-made account.
  • You have asked for the end date and the length matches the job.

Use Canva when:

  • You will edit the text yourself after the layout exists.
  • You want a year on your own email for Rs 400, which is a different product and a known 12-month term.
  • The job is a brand kit, a resize, or a template, which is what the Canva row describes.

Many people use both: Ideogram for a first image with the title drawn in, Canva when that title has to change next week. Do not buy Ideogram expecting a text box you can retype later. If the letters are pixels, changing a word means a new image. Ask yourself which of those you need this week. Rs 2,400 plus Rs 400 is still two tools. The Canva year is inexpensive next to Ideogram, and it is still a second login to learn.

What to put in the prompt, and what to keep out

The useful test on day one is a short line you can spell-check: a shop name, a video title, a price you already decided. Long paragraphs inside an image fail more often than three or four words, and the catalog does not promise a paragraph.
